# The Palace of Loth Logan - West Wing Suite

The West Wing Suite of the Palace of Loth Logan serves as the private residential quarters for Ryan Adame and his three wives—Charlotte, Florence, and Estra—along with their extended household. Queen Maya kept these rooms preserved for fifteen years, awaiting her lost son's return. The suite includes a bedroom with a large bed capable of accommodating multiple occupants, a bathing chamber with a copper tub, an antechamber for attendants, and access to a secured solarium suitable for divine power practice.

The suite is positioned within the west wing of the palace, chosen for its defensible layout and distance from the main court traffic. Howard Blakeney Ragnar has stationed twelve Ragnar men as the first layer of security, and the rooms have been swept for listening devices. The family uses hidden servant passages to move between the suite and other areas of the palace, aware that Loren Claymore may have planted agents among the staff during her fifteen years of access to Duran's court.

Notable features include interior-facing windows in the solarium that prevent outside observation, a locking door to the main bedroom, and proximity to the east wing where the Claymore Manor staff—Henry, Priti, Claire, and Fiona—have been quartered. The suite represents both sanctuary and strategic position, balancing the need for rest and intimacy with constant vigilance against potential infiltration.
