# Gentlemanly Request

Claire, the head of Claymore Manor's staff and Henry's wife, is known for her efficiency and strict adherence to maintaining appearances. When Ryan asks her to fetch a specific suit for the wedding, she is dispatched not just as a messenger, but as the manager of the household's logistical needs, ensuring the future Governor-General looks presentable for the public ceremony.
