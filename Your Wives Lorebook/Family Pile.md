# Family Pile

The 'Family Pile' is a spontaneous, intimate gathering in the War Room of Claymore Manor. It occurs after Ryan Adame explicitly grants Estra Hamilton permission to be imperfect in her vows, allowing the group to embrace the moment despite the looming threat of Loren's attack. The four wives (Estra, Florence, Charlotte, and implicitly the others present) physically huddle together in the chairs, sharing body heat and silence. It serves as a momentary reprieve from the chaos and violence of their lives, solidifying their bond as a family unit. During this event, Estra admits to never having this kind of belonging before, while Florence and Charlotte banter about Charlotte's stoic enjoyment of the sentiment.
