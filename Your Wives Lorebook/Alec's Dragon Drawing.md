# Alec's Dragon Drawing

Alec, a boy whose age is near Marten's, has created a detailed drawing of a dragon. The dragon is meticulously rendered with 53 scales, asymmetrical wings for unpredictable movement, and a range of 4 to 6 tail spikes, suggesting an unfinished design. This drawing is a point of connection between Alec and Marten, as Estra uses it to introduce the concept of Alec to the anxious boy.
