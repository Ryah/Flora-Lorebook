# Null State (The Between)

The null state is the **formless, timeless void** between reality and Ryan's projected shadow. It is nowhere – the raw absence before any world is dreamed.
See also: [[Ryan's Golden Thread – Projection]], [[The Sensory Link (Ryan & Charlotte)]].
## What It Is (and Isn't)
- **Not the dream labyrinth** – no water, stars, texture.
- **Not painful** – no nerves, no body.
- **Not hostile** – simply does not care.
- **No time** – not slow or fast; time as a concept does not exist. Desync inconsistent.
- **No space** – no ground, walls, horizon. Only grey.
- **No body** – pure consciousness, floating.
- **Only the golden thread** – single line of warmth and direction.

## Access
1. **Controlled release** (safe, tested): Charlotte deliberately releases tether (if not knotted). Ryan falls back through null state to body. Thread remains; Charlotte can still pull. Survivability: 10 real seconds ≈ 12 subjective hours minimum. Ryan disoriented, shaky, needed recovery.
2. **Rupture** (unsafe, never tested): Projection snaps, thread breaks. Althena does not know if return is possible – may be permanent loss. **Avoid at all costs.**

## The Experience (Firsthand)
Ryan's description after the June 22 release test:
> *"Time simply does not exist there. Trying to track it is a fool's errand. There's no ground, because ground doesn't exist. That's fine, because you also don't have feet, because feet don't exist. There's nothing to map, no variables to account for, because they simply aren't and never will be."*
>
> *"The fall wasn't the worst part. In fact, it was honestly the best. It was the first feeling I felt in what felt like ages. The first few hours were peaceful, then I grew tired of not being able to do anything. I didn't even have a body to walk around with. I was just… a consciousness floating in a sea of grey. There is nothing there, not even time. And it just… doesn't care."*
>
> *"You know the saying 'if you stare too long into the abyss, the abyss sometimes stares back'? Imagine that, but literally. I was staring at the abyss, and it not only stared back, it tried making conversation."*
>
>*"I honestly and truly don't it's possible for me to describe what it feels like with words. Even now, my brain just refuses to comprehend the memory. It's not a word limitation, it's simply something our mortal minds should never even have the opportunity to comprehend."*

**Analogy for understanding:** Cover your eyes with your hands and try to look *through* them. Not just closing your eyes (you still see black), but actively trying to see through your palms. That absence, that nothing, stretched for what feels like eternity. That's the null state.

## Time Desync (Tested)
| Real Time | Subjective (min) |
|-----------|------------------|
| 1 sec | ~1.2 hours (estimated) |
| 10 sec | ~12 hours (tested, estimated by Ryan with a pull every second) |
**Warning:** Inconsistent – first pull felt like an hour, second shorter, third another eternity.

## Mental Toll
- Disorientation upon return (shaking, need to "adjust to having a body again").
- Isolation – profound loneliness.
- Memory degradation – brain refuses to fully comprehend.
- Prolonged exposure risks **losing one's mind**. Ryan: would rather return to brick room than null state again.

## Golden Thread Role
- Only connection to reality. Thread remains, stretches from chest into grey.
- Charlotte's anchor pulses are the only sensory input – proof of existence.
- Without thread (rupture), no reference, no direction, no way to know he exists.

## Charlotte's Experience During Release
- Bond goes **dormant** – feels absolutely nothing from Ryan (worse than the pain).
- When she pulls back, rough/knotted loops on her end **smooth out** – purpose burns through strain.

## Althena's Knowledge
Null state not fully understood – projection + sensory bond were never meant to interact. Ryan and Charlotte are writing new rules.

## Summary
1. Null state = nowhere – no time, space, body, variables.
2. Controlled release survivable (10 sec → ~12 subj hours). Rupture may be permanent.
3. Golden thread is only lifeline.
4. Return requires anchor pull.
5. Mental toll significant – prolonged exposure risks sanity.
6. Avoid rupture – knot technique prevents accidental release but not snap.
