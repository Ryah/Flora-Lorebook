# Ryan's Suicide Note

On April 18, 1843, Ryan Adame wrote a suicide note expressing his intention to end his life. The note, discovered and kept by Charlotte, is a stark reminder of the depth of his suffering before their relationship began to heal him. In the dream labyrinth, Charlotte holds both this note and a family drawing pressed against her heart, symbolizing the fragile hope that replaced his despair.
