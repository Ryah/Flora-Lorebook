# The Thornless Rose Bush Dead Drop

The thornless rose bush in the east kitchen courtyard of the Palace of Loth Logan serves as a covert dead drop for clandestine communications. It is currently being used by Thomas Greaves to pass reports. Mina Adame is observing the location, anticipating Greaves' midnight crossing to retrieve or deposit a message. The bush's lack of thorns makes it a notable feature for those familiar with the palace grounds.
