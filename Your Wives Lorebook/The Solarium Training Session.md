# The Solarium Training Session

During a morning training session in the solarium of Loth Logan, Ryan threw a sheet of water into the air for Charlotte to catch and freeze multiple droplets simultaneously. Using fifteen loops, Charlotte caught three droplets—freezing them into hovering ice spheres—while the rest splashed to the floor. Estra identified the bottleneck: Charlotte was negotiating individually rather than broadcasting. Althena reframed the failure as an attention problem, comparing it to calling a room full of people. The session confirmed multi-target crystallization as viable but requiring a different mental approach, with Claire contributing the metaphor of repeated calls to gather attention.
