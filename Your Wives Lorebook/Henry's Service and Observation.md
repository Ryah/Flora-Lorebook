# Henry's Service and Observation

Henry is the chief butler of the Palace of Loth Logan, recognizable by his immaculate black suit, perfectly groomed blond hair in a wolfcut, and a demeanor of suave, professional warmth that masks deep concern and loyalty. He moves with a ghostlike, fluid grace, often appearing exactly when needed—such as in the early hours after a divine manifestation, bearing trays of simple, real food like dark bread, sharp cheese, and cured meats. Henry witnessed Ryan's golden divine resonance from the deck of the extraction ship, describing the harbor turning to amber and the rain suspending like glass beads, an event that left the crew and staff in awe. He maintains operational security, coordinating with staff like Claire and Fiona, and ensures the west wing is sealed and secure for the family's rest.
