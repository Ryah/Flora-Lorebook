# East Hall Tapestries

The east hall of Claymore Manor is adorned with tapestries depicting dragons. According to Marten, the artists of these tapestries rendered the dragons with fewer than the fifty-three scales Alec uses, stopping after roughly twenty. This observation highlights Marten's attention to detail and provides a minor cultural artifact within the manor.
