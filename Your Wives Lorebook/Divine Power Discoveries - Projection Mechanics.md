# Divine Power Discoveries - Projection Mechanics

During testing at the Palace of Loth Logan, Ryan discovered several critical mechanics of his projection ability. The 'knot technique' involves tying the golden thread around his anchor (Charlotte) to prevent them from releasing the connection during projection, this stabilizes the projection during vessel injury. The 'null state' (or 'nowhere state') is a brief void between emotional tethers where Ryan exists nowhere for approximately 2-3 seconds during projection transfers, he described it as being 'outside of time itself.' The 'vulnerability window' is a critical discovery: if his vessel body is injured during projection, his projection destabilizes and flickers for approximately three seconds, creating a window where he is neither fully present nor absent. During this window, Charlotte (as anchor) experiences full phantom pain through the sensory bond. The anchor can also 'pull' Ryan back through the golden thread, reeling him in like a weight on a rope. These discoveries have significant tactical implications, enemies could exploit the three-second vulnerability, and Charlotte's identity as the anchor makes her a strategic target.
