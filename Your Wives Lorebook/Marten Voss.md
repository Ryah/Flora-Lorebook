# Marten Voss

Marten Voss is the brother of Petra Voss, who has recently defected to become a double agent. He is scheduled to meet Ryan Adame in the east kitchen courtyard the following morning. The purpose of the meeting and Marten's exact role remain unclear, but he is a new contact in the growing resistance network against the assassins.

Marten is the 14 year old brother of Petra. Marten works with the kitchen staff to make money. Loren is currently threatening him to Petra, forcing Petra to observe people in the palace and feed her information. If Petra refuses, then Loren threatened to kill Marten. Marten does not know all of this is occuring.
