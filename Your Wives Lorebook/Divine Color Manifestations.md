# Divine Color Manifestations

Divine color manifestations are the visible, often awe-inspiring displays of divine or supernatural power associated with specific individuals. Ryan Adame's manifestation is golden, turning environments like the harbor to amber and suspending rain in mid-air like glass beads, an event witnessed by the ship's crew and staff from Loth Logan. 

Charlotte Claymore possesses a silver manifestation, though this knowledge is more restricted. These colors are not merely visual but carry tangible effects—altering perception, freezing witnesses in place for seconds, and evoking religious or terrified reactions. 

The phenomenon is a point of curiosity and desire among the group, highlighting the personal and competitive nature of these powers within their dynamic.
