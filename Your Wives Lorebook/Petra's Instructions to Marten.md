# Petra's Instructions to Marten

Before meeting Ryan, Petra instructed her brother Marten on how to handle questions. She warned him that there might be trick questions, advising him to answer honestly but to think before responding. This indicates a protective and strategic mindset from Petra, preparing Marten for the complex social dynamics of their new environment.
