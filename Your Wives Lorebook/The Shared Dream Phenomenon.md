# The Shared Dream Phenomenon

During their first simultaneous sleep since the sensory bond was established, Ryan Adame and Charlotte Claymore discovered they could share dream experiences. Charlotte described dreaming of standing in warm, shallow water with a glass-like surface that reflected Ryan's childhood memories—the brick room, the chain, Mina's hand reaching through a gap in the door. She reported feeling safe despite seeing traumatic imagery, because it belonged to Ryan and he was present in the dark with her, unafraid of her witnessing it.

This phenomenon suggests the sensory bond persists even during unconsciousness and is not merely a consciously activated ability but a permanent state. The implications are significant: if dreams can bleed through the link, other unconscious content—memories, pain, emotional states—might also transfer during sleep. This raises questions about whether injury sustained by one party while the other sleeps would transmit sensation, and whether deeper psychological material could cross the bond without conscious filtering.

Althena has not commented on this development, and it appears to be another unintended consequence of the bond's harmonics, similar to Charlotte's silver power manifestation. The family's immediate reaction ranges from Florence's romantic interpretation to Estra's tactical assessment of new vulnerabilities. Further testing may be warranted to understand the full scope of unconscious transmission through the divine link.
