# Thomas Greaves

Thomas Greaves is the steward assigned to Loren Claymore's household at the Palace of Loth Logan. He works the noon-to-midnight shift in the east wing and is responsible for a dead drop exchange at the thornless rose bush in the east kitchen courtyard. His loyalties are suspect, and he may be connected to the assassination plot targeting Ryan and his allies. Mina Adame is currently monitoring his movements.
