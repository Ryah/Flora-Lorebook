# Loren's Letter

The letter was left by Loren at Claymore Manor after Ryan projected there. Its contents are: 'I know where you were. I know where you went. We'll talk soon.' This message serves as both a confirmation that Loren is surveilling Ryan's activities and a threat of future contact. The letter is written on high-quality stationery, with a lingering scent that Charlotte recognized as Loren's signature perfume. Estra is concerned that this indicates Loren has agents in Duran and that the royal court may be compromised.
