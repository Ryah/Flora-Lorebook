# Charlotte's Silver Manifestation (Shared Power)

**Charlotte has no divine power of her own.** Her ability comes entirely from Ryan sharing his golden thread (see [[Ryan's Golden Thread – Catch & Release]]) through their [[The Sensory Link (Ryan & Charlotte)]]. This is a **loophole** – a harmonic accident never intended by Althena.

## How Sharing Works
- Ryan wraps golden thread around his mental image of Charlotte → silver light manifests.
- Ryan has killswitch; Charlotte cannot take power.
- **Baseline tether:** Increased from 1 to 2 loops (ankle, concealed) – no visible glow, no strain.

## What Charlotte Can Do
- Freeze water into crystalline structures (hexagonal lattices, discs, pillars, barriers).
- Signature: silver ice with opalescent glow.

## Key Mechanics
- **Active maintenance:** Ice wants to melt; holding it gets heavier over time.
- **Parallel circuit:** Matched loops with Ryan split maintenance cost (doubles duration/stability).
- **Radiative release:** Ryan's glow bleeds excess energy, buying time (makes him visible).
- **Broadcast vs. negotiation:** For multi-target freezing, command "the water" as one entity – success jumped from 3 to 11 droplets.
- **Rough loops from projection:** Loops allocated from Ryan's projection feel "knotted" and less efficient (5 feel like 3). Smooth when pulled back from null state (see [[Ryan's Golden Thread – Projection]] and [[Null State (The Between)]]).
- **Water learns:** Repetition increases response rate – droplets not refusing, just not hearing yet.

## Training Progression (Tested)
| Loops | Achievement |
|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Feel water's recognition |
| 2 | Concealed baseline |
| 5 | Stable ice disc (4-inch), active maintenance |
| 15 | Multi-target: 11 droplets frozen (broadcast), held ~15 sec |

## Limitations
- **Channel overflow:** Too many loops collapses manifestation.
- **Harmonic resonance:** Matching high loops builds until explosion unless released.
- **Multi-target cost:** Scales linearly with target count.

## Tactical Applications
- Area denial (field effect), chokepoint freezing, defensive barriers, surprise (enemies expect gold, face silver).

## Secrecy
Hidden from all except innermost circle (Ryan, Florence, Estra, Cora, Mina, Claire).

## Summary Key Rules
1. No power of her own – loophole only.
2. Ice requires active maintenance.
3. Matched loops = parallel circuit.
4. Ryan's glow = radiative release.
5. Broadcast, don't negotiate.
6. Water learns through repetition.
7. Projection-allocated loops are rough.
