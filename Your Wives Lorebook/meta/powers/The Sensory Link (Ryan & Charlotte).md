# The Sensory Link (Ryan & Charlotte)

Permanent bond forged by the goddess [[Culture - The Faith of Althena]] when Charlotte bit Ryan. Shares strong emotions and physical sensations as **vague impressions** – no telepathy, no context, no omnipresence.
See also: [[Charlotte's Silver Manifestation (Shared Power)]], [[Ryan's Golden Thread – Catch & Release]], [[Ryan's Golden Thread – Projection]].

<div class="meta-block">
[Who Knows: Ryan, Charlotte, Florence, Estra, Cora, Mina, Claire | Danger: Pain transmission, dormancy during release | Triggers: Intense emotion, vessel injury, mutual REM sleep]
</div>

## Core Rules
- **Only allies know** – enemies never sense it.
- **No telepathy** – thoughts/memories private.
- **No context** – heat could be arousal or fever.
- **Charlotte has no power of her own** – any power comes from Ryan sharing.
- **Silver thread does not naturally exist** – it's Ryan's golden thread resonating through bond.
- 
## Shared Sensations
- Frustration → agitation.
- Arousal → rising warmth.
- Orgasm → full-body wave (non-participant edged).
- Pain → dull ache in similar area.
- Calm → peace/gentleness.
**During projection vessel injury:** Charlotte feels full phantom pain (tested – shallow cut felt as own).

## Dream Sharing (Convergence Event)
- Requires **mutual REM sleep** (both unconscious).
- Creates shared dreamscape – impressions/symbols only, not full memories.
- Property of the bond itself, not power sharing. See [[The Dreamscape Labyrinth]].

## Bond Mechanics
- **Parallel circuit:** Matched loops with Ryan distribute maintenance cost.
- **Radiative release:** Ryan's glow bleeds excess energy (trade-off: visibility).
- **Anchor release effect:** When Charlotte deliberately releases tether, bond goes **dormant** – she feels nothing from Ryan (worse than pain). Returns when she pulls him back.
- **No overreaction:** Only serious emotions warrant visible response.

## Known Limits & Future Tests

- **Long-term connection:** One loop (now two, unbeknownst to Charlotte) maintained throughout the day.
- **Controlled release:** Venting energy on command without relying on glow.
- **Killswitch:** Ryan controls the thread and can release it instantly.

## Important Limitation: No Overreaction

Charlotte does **not** react to every small feeling Ryan has. The bond is background noise. Only serious emotions warrant visible response.
