# Ryan's Golden Thread – Catch & Release

Ryan was blessed by [[Culture - The Faith of Althena]] with unique divine power. Magic exists nowhere else.

See also: [[The Sensory Link (Ryan & Charlotte)]], [[Charlotte's Silver Manifestation (Shared Power)]].

## The Golden Thread (Mental Tool)
- Visualized in **"the brick room"** – not physical.
- **One loop** = catch a water droplet. **Full thread** = glowing beacon, stop rain, capsize warships (accidental).
- **Eyes-open use** possible for simple manifestations.
- **Multiple discrete catches:** Loops govern material threshold, not object count (6 loops catches 2 pieces of bread – same as 1).
- **Broadcast command:** Command "the water" as single entity for multi-target.
- **Catch & pull:** Feels a "catch"; can adjust mid-action (e.g., catching thrown bread). Targets materials and composite layers.

## Combat Applications
- **Disarm:** Pull weapon from opponent's grip – thread acts on matter, not wielder.
- **Interception:** Catch thrown objects mid-flight.
- **Radiative release (glow):** 10+ loops bleeds excess energy as light/heat (slows resonance; makes him visible).

## Hard Limit: Living Flesh
Ryan has vowed to never pull blood/water from a living person. Non‑negotiable.

## Sharing Power with Charlotte
> **Only Charlotte** – sensory bond loophole. No one else can receive it.
- Wrap thread around mental image of Charlotte → silver light.
- Ryan has killswitch; Charlotte cannot take power.
- **Body-part wrapping for concealment:** Ankle (under dress) hides glow.
- **Baseline tether:** Increased to 2 loops (ankle) – no strain.
- **Parallel circuit:** Matched loops split maintenance cost (doubles stability/duration).

## Summary Key Rules
1. Only Ryan has divine power.
2. Only Charlotte can receive shared power – bond loophole.
3. Thread is mental, not physical.
4. Living flesh off-limits.
5. Loops govern material threshold, not object count.
6. Glow = radiative release (trade: visibility).
7. Matched loops = parallel circuit.
