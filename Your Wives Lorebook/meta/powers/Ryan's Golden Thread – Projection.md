# Ryan's Golden Thread – Projection

Ryan sends awareness to a distant location while body stays behind. Separate from material manipulation (see [[Ryan's Golden Thread – Catch & Release]]).
See also: [[The Sensory Link (Ryan & Charlotte)]], [[Null State (The Between)]].

## Requirements
- Strong emotional connection to destination (cannot project to strangers/empty rooms).
- Body remains as **anchor**. Charlotte is primary anchor – she feels his location and can pull him back.
- At least 1 loop allocated to Charlotte before projecting.

## Projection Capabilities
**Can:** See, hear, speak (faint echo), move, perceive temperature/pressure.
**Cannot:** Act physically (touch, push, fight), channel divine power (agonizing feedback), interact with matter (hands pass through). **Manipulating thread from projection** causes rough/knotted loops (less efficient) and destabilizes flickering.

## Anchor (Charlotte)
- Holds tether (golden thread). Can feel his location as "weight on a rope" and pull him back (works across hundreds of miles).
- Can use silver ice while anchoring, but power degraded (5 loops feel like 3) and tracking muddied.

## Knot Technique (Anchor‑Lock)
- Ryan ties a small knot in thread around Charlotte's mental presence – she cannot release it (even if forced/unconscious).
- Critical for projection stability during vessel injury – prevents collapse after flicker.

## Vessel Injury During Projection
- Ryan feels injury; Charlotte feels full phantom pain.
- Projection **flickers 3 seconds** (cannot act/defend). Knot technique ensures stabilization instead of collapse.

## Returning from Projection
1. **Self‑gather:** Ryan intends singularity, gathers awareness.
2. **Anchor pull:** Charlotte pulls him back (works across huge distances).

## Controlled Release (Tested)
- Charlotte deliberately releases tether (if not knotted) → Ryan falls back through null state.
- **Tested:** 10 real seconds ≈ 12 subjective hours in null state. Ryan disoriented, shaky, needed recovery.
- Bond goes **dormant** during release – Charlotte feels nothing (worse than pain).
- When she pulls back, rough loops on her end **smooth out** (purpose burns strain).

## Rupture (Thread Snap) – AVOID
- Tether **snaps** (pushed past limits). Thread breaks.
- **Althena does not know** if return is possible – may be stranded permanently in null state.
- Never happened; must avoid. Knot does not prevent rupture.

## The Null State
Formless, timeless void between reality and projection. See [[Null State (The Between)]] for full details.

## Tactical Considerations
- Projection = reconnaissance only.
- Controlled release survivable but costly.
- Vessel injury = 3‑sec vulnerability – protect Ryan's body.
- Charlotte's anchor stability critical – knot prevents accidental release, but rupture remains a risk.

## Summary Key Rules
1. Requires emotional connection – can't project to strangers.
2. Cannot act or channel power – recon only.
3. Vessel injury → 3‑sec flicker.
4. Knot technique locks anchor.
5. Controlled release survivable (10 real sec = ~12 subj hours).
6. Rupture may be permanent – never snap the thread.
7. Null state is nowhere – golden thread is lifeline.
8. Charlotte can pull him back (hundreds of miles).
