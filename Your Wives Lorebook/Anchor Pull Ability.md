# Anchor Pull Ability

The anchor pull is a unique manifestation of Charlotte's connection to Ryan through the divine thread. When Ryan projects, Charlotte can physically pull him back to her location, causing water to crystallize around them in silver patterns. This ability is distinct from any known divine power manifestations and has only been observed twice during projection tests. Florence, Charlotte, and Ryan are keeping this ability secret from the royal family as it provides an unexpected strategic advantage in encounters with enemies who think they know the full extent of Ryan's powers.
