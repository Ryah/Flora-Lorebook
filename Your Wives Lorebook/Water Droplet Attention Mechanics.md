# Water Droplet Attention Mechanics

Water droplets exhibit a form of attention when subjected to magical commands, particularly during crystallization attempts. Not all droplets listen at once; some respond immediately when commands are broadcast generally, while others require repeated calls or broader influence. This was observed when Charlotte caught three droplets out of dozens—the ones that answered were already listening. Althena explained the phenomenon as akin to calling a room full of people: some turn around right away, others take time. The technique involves broadcasting a single command to many receivers rather than negotiating with each individually, with success depending on practice and the water's learning curve.
