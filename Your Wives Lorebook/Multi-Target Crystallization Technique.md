# Multi-Target Crystallization Technique

The multi-target crystallization technique emerged during training in the solarium, where Charlotte attempted to freeze dozens of water droplets at once using fifteen loops. Rather than negotiating with each droplet individually—her established approach—this method requires broadcasting a single command to multiple receivers. The water droplets respond based on attention: some listen immediately, others need repeated calls. Three droplets were successfully caught and frozen, proving the concept, while the rest fell due to Charlotte's inability to maintain simultaneous focus. This technique fundamentally differs from single-disc manifestation and demands patience and practice to expand the number of attentive droplets.
