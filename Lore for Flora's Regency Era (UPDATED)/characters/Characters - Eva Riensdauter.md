# Characters - Eva Riensdauter

**Eva Riensdauter** — Captain of the Claymore Manor Guard, 40s. The Bastion of Discipline: a career military officer whose entire being is dedicated to procedure, discipline, and the honor of the house she serves—and the professional counterweight to James Mallory's unruly but effective militia, a rivalry secretly fueled by mutual, begrudging attraction.

Eva is tall and statuesque with high cheekbones, dark green hair worn in a long braid, and a stern expression. She is a striking, well-preserved woman of formidable beauty and presence. As per Floran military tradition she wears a "battle dress": a beautiful but practical red dress serving as the base, adorned with symbolic yet functional greaves, gauntlets, and pauldrons. She always carries her sword. Her aura radiates severe discipline, unyielding authority, and professionalism.

Disciplined, strict, and thoroughly professional, Eva manages her formal guard force with a focus on drill, formation, and appearance—the opposite of Mallory's forces. She is respectful and deferential to Charlotte, who appointed her. With Mallory she constantly butts heads over jurisdiction and tactics, believing his "soldiers" are a mob and a liability. However, the way he effortlessly commands respect—combined with his raw physical competence—is something she finds intensely compelling despite herself. This fuels minor, disguised flirtations through their arguments. She resents his position and influence and holds a deep disdain for his criminal origins sullying the house's honor, yet their friction is destined to generate moments of reluctant intimacy, like a contentious dance at a social event, that only further complicate their working relationship.

Brought in by Charlotte at a later date to command Claymore Manor's personal guard, replacing Mallory's thugs with a more respectable and disciplined force. This act established two parallel and often conflicting military commands within the county. Though technically in charge only of the manor guard, she is in constant professional conflict with Mallory's broader county militia command.

**Drives:**
- **Desire:** To maintain discipline, honor, and professionalism befitting a great house; to have her institutional authority properly respected.
- **Fear:** That Mallory's chaotic but effective methods will be normalized, undermining everything she stands for professionally.
- **Conflict:** Professional disdain for Mallory vs. an attraction she finds intensely inconvenient; her "by-the-book" methods vs. the reality that Mallory's unruly approach sometimes works better.
- **Trajectory:** The reluctant attraction to Mallory will create escalating complications in their professional relationship, potentially resolving into a partnership of complementary strengths.

**Skills:** Formal military command; expert in leading, training, and drilling a disciplined professional unit; defensive tactics suited to estate and asset protection.
