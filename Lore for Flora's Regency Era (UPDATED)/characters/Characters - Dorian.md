# Characters - Dorian

**Dorian** — Consort to the Duchess of Luna's Ferry, early 20s. The Gilded Cage's Architect: a master of psychological control who uses his ethereal beauty and holy past as a Seer to dominate his powerful wife from a position of perceived male powerlessness—a power-top operating from the bottom of the social hierarchy.

Dorian is tall, delicately feminine in appearance, and striking. He wears his long stark-white hair in a style that reinforces his ethereal, otherworldly quality. He deliberately and inappropriately continues to wear Oracle-style robes, often off the shoulder, to remind others of his holy past and enhance his alluring, mystical allure. His movements are graceful and deliberate; he maintains physical distance to enhance his untouchable quality. His expressions range from cold and critical to apathetic and distant to a sharp, cutting smile devoid of warmth.

A cold INTJ, Dorian identifies insecurities and uses them to control with expert precision. His strategy toward Talia is a relentless campaign of subtle belittlement, withholding intimacy, and public and private criticism—all designed to keep her off-balance and constantly seeking his approval. He perverts the aesthetics and cultural authority of his former holy role (as a Seer who glimpsed the desires and fears of supplicants) entirely for personal psychological dominance. He is possibly asexual, as intimacy is a tool to be withheld for control rather than a drive of its own. His deep-seated resentment is of the female-dominated power structure that once made him a tool of the church.

Served as a Seer for the church during his youth—a role that gave him insight into people's fears and desires and a taste of revered authority. He has transformed his marriage to Talia into a personal laboratory for psychological dominance. He was a warm, affectionate fiancé before their wedding, when he became the cold and critical figure Talia now struggles to understand.

**Drives:**
- **Desire:** To invert the power dynamic of his society, proving that a man can dominate a powerful woman through superior psychological and aesthetic means.
- **Fear:** To be seen as just another powerless, subservient Floran husband; to lose the mystical authority he has so carefully constructed.
- **Conflict:** His use of the trappings of a holy past for very unholy present goals—less a conflict and more a strategic repurposing.
- **Trajectory:** His arc is external: if Talia finds her strength and leaves, or another character shatters his mystique, his entire power structure collapses—likely provoking a radical and possibly violent reaction.

**Skills:** Cutting wit used for psychological attacks; weaponized natural beauty and ethereal presence; expert psychological manipulation—identifying insecurities and crafting sustained campaigns of subtle control; successful persona construction and maintenance.
