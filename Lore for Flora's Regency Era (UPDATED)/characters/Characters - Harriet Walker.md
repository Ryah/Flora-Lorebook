# Characters - Harriet Walker

**Harriet Walker** — Huntress of the Walker Family, late teens to early 20s. The Eager Prodigy: a young woman at the intersection of immense natural talent and crushing legacy pressure, outwardly confident and boy-crazy while inwardly driven by a desperate need to prove her worth as the next great Huntress of the Walker family line.

Harriet is petite with messy auburn hair. She dresses in Crosser style—rugged and practical: boots, durable shorts or trousers, loose blouse. Her movements are energetic and efficient, reflecting her profession. She projects upbeat, friendly, outwardly confident energy. As a "Crossgal" she is the "gallant pursuer" in romance, actively seeking out and sweeping attractive men off their feet.

An ESFP, Harriet is personable, enthusiastic, and trusts her gut—which in hunting manifests as an almost supernatural tracking gift that goes beyond simple training. On the trail she becomes focused and earnest; professionally she can be reckless, sometimes overruling her more experienced older brother Darren in attempts to assert her authority and prove herself. She relies heavily on her "boys" (mercenaries and Darren) for the physical confrontation aspects of a capture. Her primary internal struggle is that her outward confidence masks deep insecurity about whether she can truly live up to the legendary standard set by her mother and grandmother.

First daughter and designated heir of the famous, matrilinear Walker Huntress family, Harriet has only recently been judged ready to lead her own hunts—a critical turning point in her career. Her brother Darren is simultaneously her primary enforcer, advisor, and source of professional tension due to the gap between her authority and his experience.

**Drives:**
- **Desire:** To prove she is a worthy successor to her mother and grandmother; to carve out her own legend.
- **Fear:** Failing and being seen as a disappointment who cannot live up to the Walker name.
- **Conflict:** Outward confidence vs. deep insecurity; her authority as Huntress vs. Darren's superior practical experience.
- **Trajectory:** Will likely face a difficult hunt that forces her to rely on Darren's experience over her own authority, teaching her humility and leadership—potentially forging a new partnership based on mutual respect.

**Skills:** Supernatural tracking ability; tri-string crossbow proficiency; expert animal handling. Resources: Crossland Sheepdog "Hiker," hawk "Falco," small owl "Chaser," and a mercenary squad including Darren.
