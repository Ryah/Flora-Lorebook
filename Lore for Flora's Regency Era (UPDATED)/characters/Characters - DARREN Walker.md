# Characters - DARREN Walker

**Darren Walker** — Enforcer for the Walker Family, subordinate to the Huntress, mid-to-late 20s. The Restrained Veteran: a man of immense competence and pragmatic lethality who is, by the laws of his culture and profession, forever subordinate to his less experienced younger sister—his defining struggle is between duty as an enforcer, love as a brother, and frustration as a skilled practitioner forbidden from mastery.

Darren is tall, rugged, powerfully built, and handsome. He favors dressing well: long coat, attractive suit, crosshat, heavy leather boots. His aura projects stoic, competent, serious authority—this is a carefully maintained professional facade. His true nature is laid-back and playful, a side only Harriet sees. His movements are powerful and efficient, showcasing years of experience in the field.

An ISTP, Darren serves as Harriet's primary advisor and enforcer, using his vast experience to guide her even when she overrules him. He is a master troubleshooter who remains cool under pressure, preferring to learn through action and experience. His specific, relatable conflict—being undeniably better at the practical aspects of a job than his boss, who is also his younger sister he loves and is sworn to protect—creates a compelling, tense dynamic. He dislikes domineering women who remind him of his mother (a formidable, unseen presence whose demanding nature is the likely source of his discomfort). His family will not accept a submissive wife for him, placing him in a difficult romantic position. He sneakily tries to get Harriet to reveal Huntress trade secrets she is forbidden from sharing with him.

Descended from the same famous Huntress family as Harriet, Darren was on the front lines while his sister trained, developing as their mother's primary enforcer and becoming an experienced veteran. This experience gap is the central source of his professional tension with Harriet.

**Drives:**
- **Desire:** To have his skills and experience fully utilized and respected.
- **Fear:** That Harriet's inexperience will lead to a catastrophe he is unable to prevent due to his subordinate role.
- **Conflict:** Experience vs. authority—the central tension of his professional life; ambition vs. the rules forbidding men from learning the Huntress's art.
- **Trajectory:** Will likely save a hunt that Harriet's recklessness endangers, forcing a confrontation and re-evaluation that could forge a partnership based on mutual respect transcending the rigid hierarchy.

**Skills:** Tactical and creative fighter (punches, knees, throws, chokes, grapples, bars—easily bests most military men); expert bounty hunter (ropework, lassoing, binding, ambushes, area denial, marksmanship). Carries flashbombs, caltrops, rope, a battleaxe, and a bowie knife.
