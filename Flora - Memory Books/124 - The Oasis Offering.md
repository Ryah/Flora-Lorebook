# 124 - The Oasis Offering
On Wednesday, June 21, 1843, from approximately 10:40 PM to 10:50 PM in the shared lucid dream, Ryan Adame guided Charlotte Claymore to the oasis in his mental labyrinth. He handed her a family drawing he made after his first accidental projection, depicting himself, Charlotte, Florence, and Estra smiling together. Charlotte pressed it to her chest, moved that he had imagined them happy before anyone had said they loved her. She then sensed an older note on the stone pedestal. Ryan revealed it was a suicide letter written two months ago (April 18, 1843) while bleeding in a closet after goading Estra into stabbing him to protect Florence. He had written it believing he would be sent back to his abusive mother Eleanor and preferred death. Charlotte read the letter with analytical intensity, then expressed horror at how close she had come to sending him back, which would have killed him. She made a heartfelt promise never to treat him as collateral again and folded the note with the drawing. After embracing, Ryan wiped tears and thanked Charlotte for saving him. 

Charlotte accepted the nickname 'Mrs. Silver' with dry humor. He manifested a backpack with holding space for the papers. They decided to explore unmapped parts of the labyrinth. At a branching path, Charlotte chose left because the roses carved on the wall grew toward the morning sun in her childhood gardens, the one thing her mother Loren couldn't control. As they walked, the stone walls began to sprout intricate botanical carvings — vines, citrus blossoms, climbing roses — that Ryan had never placed. Charlotte realized her own dreamscape (her glass conservatory) was physically merging with his labyrinth, indicating deeper sensory bond integration. The labyrinth was becoming theirs. The corridor curved into leaf-dappled darkness, the floor softening into a garden path. Meanwhile in the physical world, Mina Adame observed Thomas Greaves completing his midnight crossing to the dead drop in the east kitchen courtyard, counting down the seconds.
