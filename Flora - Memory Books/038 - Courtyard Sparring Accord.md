# 038 - Courtyard Sparring Accord

Friday morning, June 16, 1843, begins with Ryan Adame, Charlotte, and Florence racing through Claymore Manor's corridors toward the breakfast room, where raised voices signal confrontation.

Inside, they find Rachel confronting Callista Venn over a Duranian intruder, while Callista insists the courier brings vital intelligence about the Sea Witch. Estra Hamilton enters from the garden terrace in her Andrea's Cross disguise, spinning a karambit with blood-red eyes from dromamine use, demanding Ryan acknowledge his birthright as Prince Darian and come with her. Instead of submitting to her demands for a date and a kiss, Ryan proposes a sparring match in the eastern courtyard: unarmed combat until submission, witnessed by Howard or Captain Eva. If Ryan wins, Estra must reveal why she accepted the contract to kill Florence and provide the Sea Witch's manifest; if Estra wins, she receives her date and an hour of honest conversation. Estra accepts, adding that she wants to understand why Ryan chose to survive his captivity. 

The match commences in the dew-slicked courtyard with Mina and Cora joining the spectating circle alongside Charlotte, Florence, Rachel, Callista, Howard, and Eva. The fight proves evenly matched, both combatants experiencing exhilaration rather than malice—Estra targets Ryan's reopened back wound while Ryan holds back slightly to prolong the contest, both grinning through blood and sweat. Ryan eventually secures a chokehold from behind, forcing Estra to yield. Victorious but bleeding from torn stitches, Ryan helps Estra up and agrees to meet her at 8 PM in the Sion Quarter's Miyazakan district, clarifying it is not a date but an alliance discussion, though he acknowledges their mutual enjoyment of the spar and suggests future matches when healed. Estra reveals that Loren ordered Florence's assassination because killing the "weak link" would shatter Charlotte, annulling the marriage and leaving Ryan available for extraction. She provides a manifest sealed with blue wax, detailing crew lists, supply routes, and dead drops, confirming the Sea Witch arrives in twenty-one days. 

In Charlotte's study, while Dr. Mercer prepares fresh sutures, Charlotte confronts Ryan with trembling hands and legal precision, furious that he risked his life but ultimately acknowledging his strategic victory and expressing that she trusts his judgment, loves him, and fears for him. She provides a brass whistle for the evening meeting—one blast for aid, two for extraction—while Florence supports him, affirming that despite his recklessness, he belongs to them. The scene ends with Ryan's shirt removed for treatment, revealing the extent of his scars from three years in chains, as Charlotte holds the lamp and recognizes his strength forged in darkness.
