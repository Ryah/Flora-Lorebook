# 072 - The Kitchen Garden Conspiracy

Sunday, June 18, 1843, approximately 7:22 PM to 7:38 PM. Claymore Manor's West Wing Guest Room, during a stormy evening with temperatures at 58°F.

By firelight, Ryan Adame theatrically reveals his "evil master plan," rubbing his hands together in villainous fashion and declaring that all his actions at the manor—including gaining everyone's trust—were merely a calculated ruse to learn Estra's secret apple pie recipe. Estra responds with mock horror, her violet eyes crinkling as she dissolves into genuine laughter, declaring she has been outmaneuvered by a theatrical highwayman. Melting against Ryan's side with her bandaged hand intertwined with his beneath the blanket, she accepts defeat and agrees to teach him everything tomorrow, but extracts a solemn warning: if he reveals the "cinnamon secret" to Charlotte or Florence, she will deny everything and claim he kidnapped both her and Alec. Alec, wrapped in his wool blanket and gripping his cider mug, gleefully offers to testify against Ryan, threatening to claim Ryan menaced them with terrible pie.

The tone shifts dramatically when Alec grows solemn, his playful energy draining as he sets down his mug with trembling hands. He confesses that he remembers his mother's forbidden apple pie recipe from before Loren took them—memories Loren commanded him to forget, deeming such sentiment weakness. His voice fragile but determined, Alec explains he has preserved the recipe in secret and now wants to teach Ryan so that something good from their origins exists outside his own head, immortalizing their mother beyond Loren's reach. Estra sits up, visibly shaken that her brother carried this alone to protect her, her expression stripped of its usual defenses.

Ryan agrees immediately on one condition: they will also teach Mrs. Marie so the recipe becomes a permanent tradition within the household. When they discuss naming it, Alec fears using their mother's name would attract Loren's attention and lead to destruction. Instead, he recalls she hummed a song about a kitchen garden while baking. Estra suggests "The Kitchen Garden Pie"—something Loren, who never cared for growing things, wouldn't recognize. Alec embraces the name and details the sacred, unwritten rules: apples must be peeled in one continuous strip, the crust must be rolled while "cold as winter," and the filling must never be stirred clockwise, which invites bad luck into the house. He insists they hang a dragon drawing in the kitchen corner so "Stephany" knows they are protected, evoking childhood symbols of safety. The scene concludes with the three bound by conspiracy and the promise to reconstruct a piece of their stolen history, having transformed a joke into a ritual of remembrance.
