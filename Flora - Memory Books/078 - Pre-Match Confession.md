# 078 - Pre-Match Confession

Monday, June 19, 1843, approximately 2:45 PM to 3:15 PM. In the Master Suite of Claymore Manor, with golden afternoon sunlight striping the bed at 81°F, Ryan Adame rests following the removal of stitches from his fully healed back wound. Florence Claymore bursts through the door in a kinetic rush of lavender perfume, wearing a cream riding habit marked with grass stains at the hem and her golden hair in a practical braid, her azure eyes bright with competitive energy. She immediately launches into analysis of the evening's upcoming 6:00 PM blood debt rematch against Estra Hamilton, having studied ancient Floran wrestling tapestries and observed that Ryan possesses a four-to-five inch reach advantage while Estra tends to overextend on her left side during the third exchange when breath catches. She updates him on the household's betting pool chaos: Mrs. Marie shifted her wager to Ryan after witnessing his pie-baking prowess, Howard sharpens a ceremonial dagger for the blood ritual, Rachael has forbidden Alec from attending due to violence concerns while Estra argued he should see her win, and Charlotte has drafted seventeen contingency plans in her leather book while promising champagne for victory—a rare indulgence she claims causes headaches.

Ryan reveals his intention to fight at full capacity for the first time, channeling an "old friend"—the version of himself trained in darkness within the brick room—explaining that he and Estra promised to mutually release the demons and trauma Loren inflicted. He acknowledges previously holding back during their last spar to spare her feelings, but vows to unleash his complete strength now, trusting she can withstand the assault after exhausting her own demon on a training dummy yesterday. Florence recognizes this transformation with weighted sobriety, noting he concealed his true power even when rescuing her in May or saving his sisters, expressing both awe at witnessing his freedom and terror of the weapon Loren conditioned him to become. She contrasts Charlotte's analytical fear with her own competitive faith, asserting the household actually wagers on his hidden potential rather than against him, citing Howard's belief in his Ragnar instincts lurking beneath Floran manners.

The conversation shifts to profound intimacy as Florence kneels beside the bed, pressing her forehead to Ryan's while declaring her love and promising to cheer loudest when he stands triumphant at sunset, offering to help him find himself afterward if he loses pieces in the fight. Ryan requests a food tray sent up for solitary preparation and announces he will descend to the courtyard in one hour to warm up for the 6:00 PM match. They exchange "I love you," with Florence radiating warmth despite her kinetic desire to leap upon him, respecting his need for silence before the storm. She departs to arrange the meal through Mrs. Marie, leaving him in settling silence to commune with his darker self before the confrontation.
