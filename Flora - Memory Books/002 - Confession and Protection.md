# 002 - Confession and Protection

Wednesday, June 14, 1843 AD, approximately 2:47 PM to 3:07 PM. In the parlor of Claymore Manor, Rachael Blakeney's expression transforms from warmth to granite-like fury as Ryan confesses the full extent of his abuse by his mother, Eleanor Adame—details of chains, basement imprisonment, and starvation. Rachael's hands curl into white-knuckled fists as she listens without interruption, the mantle clock counting the heavy silence. She declares that Howard will visit Eleanor and that she will not stop him, then confronts Charlotte with cold fury for missing the abuse during her investigation. When Florence chokes back a sob near the fireplace, Rachael crosses the room in two strides and embraces Ryan fiercely, her vanilla scent enveloping him as she cradles his head and promises that nobody will ever chain him again, declaring him her son. Ryan, experiencing his first remembered hug, provides detailed instructions for locating the hidden torture chamber: an orange brick on the eighth row from the bottom of a basement wall, which requires three hard presses to open a secret door. He describes a hidden cubby under the floor containing evidence and a journal, and begs for the recovery of a locket given by his sister Mina. Rachael commits the instructions to memory with mechanical precision and vows to retrieve the locket personally. She immediately mobilizes resources, commanding Charlotte to freeze Eleanor's accounts through contacts in the Amber Isles and petition the Crown if necessary, citing influence in Tor Veiloth. When Ryan defends Charlotte and Florence—explaining that Eleanor spent decades hiding the evidence and that their purchase unknowingly saved his life—Rachael's anger softens with wonder at his protective instinct. She promises to arrange for him to confront Eleanor after capture and to protect his sisters. After a kiss to his forehead and Florence's tearful embrace from behind, Henry the butler escorts Ryan through the main corridor. Henry reveals the household's own dark history under the previous mistress, Loren Claymore, who wrapped cruelty in scripture and abused both him and Charlotte as children. 

He warns Ryan never to call himself 'cattle' again. As they reach the Lord's Quarters, Ryan observes the fresh wood and repaired wall where an assassin broke through three weeks prior during the attempt on Florence's life that left Ryan with a knife scar across his back. Left alone in the bathing chamber with a steaming porcelain tub scented with lavender and comfrey, Ryan soaks his travel-weary muscles until Fiona, a pale-haired bath attendant, enters nervously with medical supplies to tend his healing wound as a guard watches from the newly replaced window.
