# 025 - Ragnar Vessel Transfer

Thursday, June 15, 1843, between 10:40 AM and 11:20 AM, aboard a longship in coastal waters transitioning to the open sea aboard the Ragnar vessel. Immediately following the successful extraction from the Adame household via the root cellar tunnel, Ryan assesses the condition of his rescued sisters while ignoring his own reopened back wound. He notes that Fiona's salve allowed him to run despite the pain. Finding Cora severely malnourished and dehydrated, he provides her with water from Florence's pouch and a granola bar, guiding her to take small sips to avoid vomiting. Cora, barely conscious, recognizes Ryan and whispers that he kept "the blanket" warm, referencing their shared past. Florence reveals to the sisters that Ryan carried both her and Cora up the hill while bleeding, and had been relentless in planning their rescue after receiving Mina's letter.

As Howard pilots the longship toward a headland to escape Eleanor's potential archers, Ryan moves to comfort Mina, who is overwhelmed with guilt for failing to stop Eleanor from selling him into marriage. She notes he has grown taller and confesses she believed Eleanor had sold him to die. She observes Florence carrying Cora despite her own injury, realizing Ryan married into a family that actively rescues its own. Ryan explains that Rachael Claymore immediately mobilized Howard, Callista, and his wives upon learning of the situation, considering Mina and Cora her daughters already. He introduces Florence as his most cherished family, a competitive wrestler who carried Cora up the hill despite her ankle being bent wrong, and describes Howard as a calculating but trustworthy father-in-law. He warns them to expect Rachael's overwhelming affection and suffocating hugs upon their arrival at Claymore Manor.

Florence introduces herself to Mina, and the two share an emotional embrace. Ryan requests splints for Florence's severely swollen, purple-black ankle. Howard confirms the Ragnar vessel carries a full medical chest with splints, bandages, and supplies from the Skaag campaign. The group transfers to the larger vessel via a rope ladder against the barnacle-crusted hull. Ryan climbs first, his reopened wound bleeding through his bandages, followed by Mina. Cora, too weak to climb, is hoisted in a harness. Howard carries Florence piggyback up the ladder, addressing her as "daughter" and overriding her protests about pride.

Once aboard the Ragnar ship in open sea, with the Amber Isles receding from view, Ryan jokes about needing his back treated before Rachael worsens it with concern. Florence insists he receive treatment immediately, negotiating that she will only accept a splint for her ankle after he is seen. Callista prepares a field kit containing needle, catgut, and strong alcohol. Surrounded by his family, Ryan removes his shirt to reveal the bleeding injury as the ship sets course for Cassia waters and home.
