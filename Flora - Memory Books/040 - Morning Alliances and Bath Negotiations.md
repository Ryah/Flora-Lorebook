# 040 - Morning Alliances and Bath Negotiations

Friday, June 16, 1843, between 9:25 AM and 10:00 AM. In the cooling waters of the Mistress Bath at Claymore Manor, Ryan Adame finalizes a tense alliance with Estra Hamilton, an assassin previously employed by the antagonist Loren. Ryan promises to use his full resources to extract Estra's brother Alec from Loren's custody in exchange for Estra's proven loyalty to his wives. Demonstrating mutual respect for combat craft rather than exploiting her vulnerability, Ryan requests to inspect her karambit after their evening meeting, offering to show his own dagger in exchange and expressing genuine curiosity about whether the curved blade suits his style better than straight steel. Estra agrees to demonstrate the proper draw, reverse grip, and hooking techniques in the courtyard, explaining that the weapon punishes arrogance and demands uncomfortable intimacy with one's target. Charlotte Claymore immediately imposes conditions: the exchange must be supervised with Howard present, reflecting her suspicion that Estra remains a calculated threat. Florence Claymore, nursing a splinted ankle, counters with competitive interest, wanting to learn the karambit's physics to understand angles she has never faced in tournament combat.

Following Estra's departure—where she adopts a provincial bumpkin persona with wide eyes and naive questions to maintain her cover, and thanks Ryan for seeing her authentic self beyond the assassin identity—Charlotte warns that Estra will inevitably betray them when Loren applies pressure, framing the alliance as temporary leverage. Florence suggests an alternative path: converting Estra to family through genuine belonging, citing their own transformation from Ryan's captors to his partners. Ryan reassures both wives by explaining his rapid trust stems from recognizing shared violent upbringing in Estra's physicality, a language of truth written in fighting that cannot lie, unlike spoken words. He declares absolute loyalty to Florence and Charlotte equal to his blood sisters Mina and Cora, stating he would relinquish his Duranian birthright entirely to keep them safe.

The trio relocates to the solar for breakfast, where Charlotte clarifies they gather today for strategic planning since Ryan is on a 48-hour ban on physically intense heroics, imposed by Dr. Mercer for his stitches to heal: decoding the cipher tube in Cora's possession, investigating William Adame's murder, managing the twenty-one-day deadline Loren imposed, and determining information security regarding Ryan's princely identity. While Florence feeds Ryan buttered bread and insists he focus on recovery rather than self-sacrifice, Charlotte admits that without him they would merely manage—drafting clauses and organizing tournaments— but would diminish into isolated, smaller versions of themselves, never having learned what real family means. Ryan ends the scene by teasing them about questioning him in his sleep, accepting their physical care while acknowledging the weight of the 8pm meeting with Estra and the day's pressing conspiracy-related tasks.
