# 009 - Family Declaration

Wednesday, June 14, 1843, between 6:30 PM and 6:44 PM. In the oak-paneled main corridor of Claymore Manor, illuminated by wall sconces casting amber light across oil portraits, Florence Claymore waits barefoot on the dark wood floor. Having sat for twenty minutes against the wainscoting in a pale yellow dress creased at the waist, her honeysuckle scent lingering, she rises as Ryan emerges from a closed-door intelligence discussion with Charlotte, using a severe-looking matriarch's portrait for balance. Florence immediately observes Ryan's genuine exhaustion versus his earlier performed confidence, noting the distinct way his shoulders settle when he is being real rather than brave. Ryan explains his psychological tendency to mirror the emotions of those around him, revealing that he required Charlotte's stoic "stone wall" demeanor to process the traumatic day without emotional contagion, whereas Florence's openness would have amplified his distress. 

He takes her hand, thanking her for guiding him through the day and affirming his reliance on her for the impending extraction mission to rescue his sisters, calling it the most important day of his life. Florence admits that the twenty minutes of silence behind the thick door was worse than hearing conflict, as the walls stopped all sound and she imagined worst-case scenarios. Her gaze drops to the tarnished silver locket at Ryan's throat. She reveals deep insecurity about her role: Charlotte serves as confidant, Rachael as protector, Howard as instructor, while she fears being reduced merely to physical labor—carrying his sisters out "like grain sacks." As she attempts to transition to washing up before dinner, signaled by the kitchen bell, Ryan stops her, grasping her shoulders and tucking a loose golden hair strand behind her ear. He delivers a profound declaration: Florence represents "home" to him, is the sole reason he feels comfortable in the manor, and would be the first and only person he names as "family" despite having known her merely eight hours and despite her complicity in his procurement. He notes that she and Rachael remain the only individuals who have offered him physical comfort through hugs. 

Florence reacts with stunned silence, tears forming as she processes that Ryan could call her family despite her role in purchasing him. She reveals that while Charlotte whispers "family" into her hair at night when she believes Florence sleeps, no friend or teammate in her entire life has ever explicitly claimed her as family. Resting her forehead against his collarbone and gripping his shirt fabric, she absorbs the weight of his words before composing herself to avoid Rachael's scrutiny at the evening meal. Reaffirming her commitment to the extraction mission with renewed emotional stakes, Florence departs down the east corridor toward the east wing, leaving Ryan alone in the hallway.
