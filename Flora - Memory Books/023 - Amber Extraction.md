# 023 - Amber Extraction

Thursday, June 15, 1843, approximately 9:48 AM to 10:32 AM. The scene opens with Ryan and Florence aboard the fishing vessel Sea Witch approaching Cove Landing at Amber Isles. Following a kiss that leaves Florence flushed with adrenaline, they disembark into knee-deep surf, navigating moss-slick rocks to reach a narrow fisherman's path carved into the cliff face and marked by a white quartz stripe. By 10:12 AM, they reach a divergence point where Callista's tested route leads left toward the cove, while Fiona's intelligence reveals a right-hand drainage channel from an abandoned cistern. Ryan chooses Fiona's path, reasoning that Edmund leaked their original plan and the tested route is likely guarded, expressing trust in Fiona's access and knowledge. Florence accepts the decision and leads the way through the narrow, claustrophobic channel, emerging into the kitchen garden behind the house perimeter by 10:18 AM.

They enter the basement and locate the secret entrance to the hidden room by pressing an orange-red brick in the eighth row three times, triggering a pneumatic mechanism. Inside, they find Mina and Cora Adame imprisoned and emaciated—Mina with hacked short dark hair, Cora at sixteen with hollow eyes and rag-wrapped hands. Mina immediately warns that Eleanor moved them there yesterday knowing Ryan would come, declaring it a trap. As predicted, the brick door seals magnetically from the outside, trapping the four in darkness. Ryan lights an oil lantern and reveals a tunnel he dug years ago along the east wall, concealed behind a facade that Eleanor never found (the reason he was previously chained). Using Florence's silver hairpin to scrape away mortar, they breach the tunnel entrance.

The group crawls through the narrow, dark passage while Eleanor's distinctive limping footsteps pass directly overhead, checking the sealed room. Ryan reassures his sisters that Howard's forces wait outside with contingencies, scheduled to breach the house by noon if they don't return, remarking that the Claymores are "more caring and thorough than mother expected." They reach the abandoned root cellar by 10:28 AM, exit through a rotted wooden door into the kitchen garden among overgrown rosemary and morning glory vines, and flee toward the northern path. Florence carries the weakened Cora in her arms while Ryan guides Mina, escaping the grounds as the supply cart continues to distract the household staff.
