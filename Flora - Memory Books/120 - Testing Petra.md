# 120 - Testing Petra
On Wednesday, June 21, 1843, from approximately 7:52 PM to 8:20 PM in the Great Hall of the Palace of Loth Logan, the formal dinner with Queen Maya and the Duranian court continued. Ryan Adame noticed a female servant near the north entrance staring at him persistently throughout the meal. Howard Adame prompted Ryan to observe and report, testing his training. Florence's arm remained around Ryan's shoulders; Estra's hand tightened on his knee; Charlotte tracked the servant from her position at the far left of the high table. Ryan identified that the servant had not served a single glass, her grip changed when Queen Maya spoke to him, and her position was for observation, not service. Howard confirmed the observation was correct. Estra identified the servant as Petra Voss, a palace staff member for six years who transferred from the east wing to the great hall three months ago — approximately when Loren would have begun planning for Ryan's extraction. Charlotte calculated this predated their flight from Claymore Manor, the wedding, and the divine manifestation in the harbor, concluding Loren had been planning for Ryan's return longer than they had been planning to escape her. Florence suggested Petra could simply be curious about the lost prince. Howard established the rule: assume nothing, verify everything, and asked Estra to obtain Petra's shift schedule after dinner. Queen Maya revealed she personally recommended Petra for the great hall rotation and expressed guilt at the possibility of unknowingly welcoming one of Loren's agents into the palace. Charlie defended her; William Adame reassured her from experience with Loren's network; Rachael suggested using Petra to feed misinformation to Loren, a framework Charlotte confirmed she was already planning. Charlotte called for the family to appear relaxed as the court was watching. Florence volunteered to steal Ryan's food and make a scene about the custard as a tactical objective. Alec asked what a tactical objective was; Mina explained stealing food was now a mission. Cora sketched a diagram of the great hall with servant positions marked. Queen Maya called for the pheasant course to proceed. Ryan deliberately winked at Petra Voss, testing her reaction. Petra held eye contact for two counts before breaking contact — a trained, disciplined response, not curiosity, as Estra and Howard confirmed. Florence loved the provocation. Alec asked why Ryan winked; Mina explained it as 'a diplomatic gesture,' which Charlie eagerly adopted for future trade negotiations, drawing laughter from the table. As the pheasant course arrived and the table settled into warm banter, Ryan proposed a plan: after dinner, he would project himself into a disused study while Charlotte and Florence provided an alibi in another room. Estra would lead Petra to the study under the pretext of a mundane errand about kitchen linens. Queen Maya offered the east corridor study as a location and promised to occupy the steward with inventory reports until nine o'clock. Howard advised keeping the projection in partial light so Petra would fill in details herself. Estra agreed to appear flustered and harried as a new wife still learning the palace layout. Charlie asked what they would do if Petra was innocent; Charlotte responded that a confused servant is significantly less dangerous than a trained one. Alec accidentally revealed Estra's 'hurry up and wait' philosophy, which the table adopted as universal wisdom for spying, mothering, ruling, and marriage — sparking a brief argument between Florence and Charlotte about whether the pheasant was dry. At the end of the pheasant course, Ryan called Queen Maya 'mom,' thanking her for the meal. Maya froze, her knife stopping mid-cut, eyes bright but controlled. She acknowledged he had called her that twice now — once on the dock and once here — and she had waited fifteen years to hear it, grateful it came from him. Florence squeezed Ryan's hand under the table. Charlie defused the emotional moment with a joke about calling for custard early. Ryan admitted he had never had custard, and Charlie declared it a culinary crime he intended to rectify. Queen Maya promised an extra portion; Florence demanded two for baseline comparison; Estra admitted she had never had custard either, prompting theatrical horror from Charlie. William shared a memory of Charlie declaring a diplomatic frog as a boy, drawing warm laughter from the table. Throughout, Petra Voss remained at the north entrance, still watching, but her grip on the wine pitcher had loosened — she was now watching the family around Ryan. Twenty-five minutes remained until custard, twenty until Estra moved.
