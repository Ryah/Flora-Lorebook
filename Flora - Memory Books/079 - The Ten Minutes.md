# 079 - The Ten Minutes

On Monday, June 19, 1843, between 3:15 PM and 3:35 PM in the Master Suite of Claymore Manor, Ryan Adame reveals to Estra Hamilton that he had already intended to train her brother Alec in self-defense—exactly as she wished in her blood debt—both to prepare for Loren Claymore's looming retaliation and to sever Loren's psychological grip on the Hamilton siblings. Estra, dressed in charcoal trousers and a sleeveless linen shirt with bandaged knuckles, stands rigid with tension despite having discussed wedding plans that morning. Ryan invites her to sit, arguing they should pretend to be normal before they must fight at 6:00 PM, and suggests that protecting Alec and teaching him alongside Mina was always his plan.

Estra repeats his revelation with flat surprise, realizing she did not need to win a debt to secure her brother's safety. She acknowledges that Ryan never waits to be asked to protect people, yet she hesitates to sit, explaining that lowering her guard makes her feel weak and that Loren's house taught her to be invisible, silent, and hopeless—making Claymore Manor's warmth feel like learning to breathe underwater. Ryan extends his scarred palm—the hand scabbed from the blood vigil—and confesses he already sees her as his wife and partner, not a stray to be kept but an equal. He cites specific memories as proof: her saving his life at the monastery by breaking Loren's formation to attack a guard strangling him with a flail, the time he sat motionless for six hours while she slept on his shoulder, and his vigil in a hallway chair so she could rest without fear.

He tells her she is the left hand to his right, two halves of the same coin, and that their upcoming fight has a 50-50 betting pool because they are perfectly matched partners. Estra, stunned that he noticed her defiance at the monastery, admits she knows only how to be useful, not precious, and does not understand how to accept care without price. Nevertheless, she slowly takes his hand, settles onto the bed's edge, and agrees to try learning how to be cared for, though she insists she does not drool.

Ryan mentions dreaming of baking apple pie with her and Alec, reinforcing their potential future as a family. Estra reveals she trained at dawn with Mina, who pushed her through exhaustion and accused her of softness from Loren's "coddling"—a term she finds absurd—while she observed her brother sketching survival maps of the manor exits to protect himself, a paranoia she blames herself for instilling. Ryan notes Mina's own brutal training methods in dark rooms.

After Ryan steals a kiss and reaffirms he regrets nothing, Estra analyzes the tactical situation for their 6:00 PM rematch in the eastern courtyard, noting the western sun could blind her. She confesses that when they fight, she will see him as the only person who has ever fought beside her rather than against her, which will make her hesitate and potentially lose. She recognizes they share identical trauma responses—the flinch before striking, the stillness that masks waiting for blows—and theorizes that victory will go to whoever hesitates first.
