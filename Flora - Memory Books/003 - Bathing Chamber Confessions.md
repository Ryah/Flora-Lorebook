# 003 - Bathing Chamber Confessions

On Wednesday, June 14, 1843 AD, beginning at 3:07 PM in Claymore Manor's Lord's Quarters Bathing Chamber, Henry counsels Ryan that mental wounds heal slower than physical ones before withdrawing. Ryan enters the chamber where a porcelain tub awaits filled with steaming water scented with lavender and comfrey, the temperature just shy of scalding to ease travel-weary muscles. Fresh clothes rest on a wooden bench: cream linen shirt, dark trousers, and a forest green waistcoat matching the canopy in the next room. Ryan settles into the water, feeling the tightness of the three-week-old scar tissue across his back where a blade had missed his spine by the width of a finger.

At 3:10 PM, Fiona, a pale-haired bath attendant in a black swimsuit with white ruffles, enters carrying a basket of medical supplies. She tends to Ryan's wound, applying honey-colored salve to the raised scar tissue while revealing she formerly served at Ashcroft (the Blakeney estate) under Lord Harry. She confesses that Harry disciplined her through spanking and bondage, that she hated how much she needed such treatment to sleep, and that she felt abandoned when he married another for alliance and left without goodbye. She admits she waits for someone to see her as more than a servant, to stop asking what she needs and instead take control and decide for her.

Ryan reveals that during the assassination attempt three weeks prior, he had wanted to die, not from fear of death but from fear of failing to protect Florence and dooming his sisters. He admits that only twenty-four hours ago he was chained to a wall by his mother, Eleanor Castor, and still struggles to see himself as a noble. Fiona, moved by his vulnerability, agrees when Ryan tells her he will call her to his chambers soon to "work something out" regarding her needs. She departs at 3:19 PM after arranging for Claire to bring towels.

Claire, a brunette maid, enters at 3:35 PM with fresh towels. She reveals that Lady Florence visited Ryan's room every day during his recovery, reading to him through fever and weeping when she thought no one watched. Claire also mentions growing up with Charlotte and watching Loren Claymore make her cold. She informs Ryan that Lady Rachael waits in the parlor with "someone important" who is eager to meet him.

Ryan proceeds to the parlor at 3:52 PM, where he meets Howard Blakeney Ragnar, Lord Consort of the Black Forest and Florence's father. Howard is a massive, scarred man with ice blue eyes and a military bearing, wearing a forest green doublet. When asked what he would do to Eleanor, Ryan describes entombing her alive in bricks to watch her rot, which Howard approves, calling it either the stupidest or bravest thing he has heard. Howard reveals that Loren Claymore arranged the marriage before her exile, making Eleanor her creature, and declares that he and Ryan are now "family by enemy." He also reveals that Florence carries guilt over marrying without love and wept for three days when Ryan was wounded.

Ryan responds that he intends to earn his title through action, to be the best husband he can be, and to repay Florence for saving his life regardless of the circumstances. Rachael, present throughout, insists Ryan does not need to earn his place, while Howard declares that Florence needs a partner, not a lord. Howard schedules combat training for dawn the next morning in the eastern courtyard, stating the next assassin will not miss.

At 4:03 PM, Ryan tells Rachael about his sisters Mina and Cora—how Mina taught him to fight and Cora snuck him food, and how they tried to help him escape but he refused to endanger them. Rachael devises a plan to extract the sisters using Howard's northern contacts, but requires Ryan to write a carefully coded letter tonight after dinner, revealing only that he is alive and safe while withholding all specifics to prevent interception by Eleanor.
