# 007 - Locket and Legal Deadline

Wednesday, June 14, 1843, 4:55 PM - 5:45 PM. At Claymore Manor's Main Hall by the dying fire, Ryan Claymore insists on participating in the ground extraction of his sisters Mina and Cora from the Amber Isles household despite his recent head wound and back knife injury. He reveals the existence of a hidden brick room absent from all blueprints, describing the entrance mechanism: a brick on the eighth row from the bottom that is slightly less red, which must be pressed three times to release a hidden door. Charlotte Claymore challenges his physical capability, arguing his injuries make him a liability, while Florence Claymore supports Ryan, asserting that the abused sisters will not trust strangers and require a familiar face to follow. Rachael Blakeney requests tactical details about the room and tests Ryan's resolve regarding his ability to hold a rope.

Ryan argues that his intimate knowledge of the house's blindspots and his sisters' psychology makes his presence non-negotiable, declaring he would walk through fire naked to protect his family. Charlotte concedes his point about trust but warns that adrenaline will eventually crash. Rachael assigns roles: Ryan and Florence will infiltrate via the smuggler's path during Thursday's supply delivery while Charlotte manages the legal defense against Eleanor Adame's competency petition, which declares Ryan mentally incompetent and demands his return to custody.

Ryan asks Florence to promise to abandon him if the plan fails, arguing Eleanor will kill her but only torture him, buying escape time. Florence furiously refuses, declaring that Claymores do not leave family behind, a principle Rachael confirms. Charlotte then reveals a critical obstacle: the petition requires Ryan to submit to a physical examination by court-approved physician Doctor Aldous Mercer by Friday morning; absence constitutes evidence of incompetence. Henry the butler reveals Mercer treated Charlotte and Florence as children and owes Rachael a favor. Rachael dispatches Henry to Millhaven with a sealed letter requesting Mercer examine Ryan Thursday morning instead, creating an 18-hour window between examination and extraction.

Henry produces Mina's tarnished silver locket, found in the courier's satchel. Ryan opens it to reveal an eight-year-old photograph of himself with his sisters. Charlotte, examining the hinge construction, discovers a hidden second compartment containing a faded photograph of young Eleanor Adame. Ryan is shaken, whispering unanswered questions to his mother's image, before closing that compartment forever and wearing the locket. Ryan then requests a private audience with Charlotte to finish their interrupted conversation from the blue room. Florence leaves with masked disappointment but understanding, and Rachael departs to check on dinner, leaving Charlotte alone with Ryan. Charlotte admits his gratitude destabilizes her and asks what he needs to tell her.
