# 020 - Tower Confession

On Thursday, June 15, 1843, between 8:20 and 8:40 AM, Florence Claymore leads her husband Ryan through Claymore Manor toward the North Tower to prepare for the extraction of Ryan's sisters from Amber Isles. Dressed in grey trousers, a charcoal waistcoat, and her golden hair braided back with a leather cord, Florence carries a canvas pack while nursing a fading yellow-green bruise on her forearm.

While ascending the spiral staircase, Ryan stops Florence to reveal a critical secret: the carriage driver who originally brought him to the manor, Edmund Girard, is the mole in Eleanor's intelligence network. Ryan explains that his initial memory loss was intentional, caused by a too-small carriage, and that he and Charlotte had discovered this discrepancy together but maintained absolute secrecy to ensure focus remained on rescuing his sisters. He confesses that Eleanor now knows they are coming—a deliberate part of their plan to ensure the sisters remain at the location—but apologizes for excluding Florence from the initial discovery, explaining he needed to wait until Edmund sent a memo to Eleanor for this plan to work.

Taking Florence's hands, Ryan solemnly vows as her husband never to keep secrets from her or Charlotte again, acknowledging that marriage means shared secrets and promising to include her in all private conversations going forward. Florence processes the revelation with focused intensity, repeating the name 'Edmund Girard' as confirmation. She accepts his reasoning and his apology, emphasizing that his oath goes both ways and that trust requires transparency with both wives. She is upset he kept her out of the discussion, but notes that his timing—telling her in a safe, private space before they reach the carriage and before walking into danger blind—matters significantly.

They proceed to the third alcove in the North Tower, a narrow stone room lit by morning light through an arrow slit. Florence displays the extraction gear: fifty feet of braided hemp rope capable of holding two hundred pounds, twelve iron pitons of varying lengths in a leather roll, a hammer, two water skins, dried meat, hard bread, and a small boot knife for Ryan. She also reveals dark wool shirts and trousers packed for Mina and Cora to replace any dresses before climbing.

After Ryan changes into grey climbing trousers and a dark wool shirt, checking that his figure-eight bandage allows full mobility, he initiates his first-ever embrace with Florence. He pulls her close, expressing profound gratitude and acknowledging her guilt about purchasing him for marriage, assuring her that her immediate willingness to join the ground mission proves her character. He tells her she is the first person he thinks of when he hears the word 'family,' declaring that he would want no other wives.

Florence goes still in the embrace, then returns it with trembling hands, deeply moved that Ryan chose to reach for her voluntarily rather than out of obligation. She acknowledges she has been waiting for him to want her freely, and promises to hold him to every word of his gratitude and his vow of transparency. With renewed determination, she redirects focus to the mission, reminding him they have twenty minutes until the carriage departs.

They pack the gear into two canvas rucksacks—Ryan carrying the rope while Florence takes the pitons and hammer—and descend the spiral staircase. Florence establishes that during the carriage ride, Ryan must explain exactly how he and Charlotte identified Edmund, because Eleanor's foreknowledge changes their tactical situation.

They exit the manor and board the carriage driven by Thomas Greaves, maintaining the cover story of visiting the Hartwell textile merchant in Cassia County. The scene concludes as they depart at 8:40 AM, armed and prepared for the extraction, with Florence having secured Ryan's promise of complete honesty and Ryan having affirmed Florence's place as chosen family.
