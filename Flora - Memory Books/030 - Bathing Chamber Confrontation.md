# 030 - Bathing Chamber Confrontation

Thursday, June 15, 1843, approximately 5:57 PM to 7:00 PM. In the Lord's Quarters Bathing Chamber at Claymore Manor, Ryan and Florence linger naked in a cooling copper tub following their first intimate encounter. The water has turned cloudy and lukewarm, scented with lavender and the evidence of their pleasure. Florence, her golden hair plastered wet against her shoulders and her splinted ankle propped awkwardly on the tub rim, washes Ryan's stitched back wound—injured during a recent assassination attempt on her life—using gentle circular motions with a slippery soap bar. Ryan reaffirms his love for both wives and insists they honor their "no secrets" vow by telling Charlotte about their intimacy immediately, rejecting the idea of hiding Florence as a shameful secret. Florence, initially anxious about Charlotte's reaction but fiercely determined to avoid choosing between them, agrees to face her wife together.

Around 6:05 PM, Charlotte arrives outside the locked chamber door, demanding entry to assess Florence's ankle before the evening assembly. Her voice carries controlled suspicion, sharpened by Florence's trembling replies. Despite Florence's plea for five minutes, Charlotte produces a master key and barges in at 6:12 PM, catching them still naked and flushed. Charlotte confronts the scene with rigid composure that rapidly fractures—she catalogues the evidence of their intimacy with mechanical precision before her voice cracks with hurt and betrayal. She accuses Ryan of taking her wife and Florence of choosing him over her, referencing the legal documents she drafted to protect Ryan's autonomy and her own sacrifices during his recovery.

Ryan defends himself by asserting this is their first time together and that he was moments away from telling her, criticizing her use of the master key as a violation of his safety and privacy. Florence intervenes physically, stepping out of the tub naked and dripping to grasp Charlotte's gloved hands, begging her not to leave and pressing Charlotte's palm against her own heart to prove her loyalty remains intact. After a moment of rigid resistance, Charlotte collapses into a possessive embrace, crushing Florence against her burgundy velvet gown and declaring her hatred for her own vulnerability while asserting dominance over the situation.

Charlotte commands that both Ryan and Florence will sleep in her bed tonight to "sort this out" after the assembly, refusing to be excluded from the arrangement she created. She exits to prepare for the evening's political maneuvering—exposing Edmund Girard as the mole—leaving Ryan and Florence alone to process the confrontation. Ryan commits absolutely to staying and fighting for the triad relationship, referencing how he reopened his wound carrying Florence to safety and how she was the first face he saw upon waking, cementing her place in his concept of family. Florence, relieved and resolute, accepts his commitment and swears to fight for their arrangement despite her fear of hurting either party. They agree to face Charlotte together tonight, dressing quickly for the assembly where Edmund will be exposed and Ryan's mother confronted, the atmosphere shifting from volatile confrontation to determined unity as they prepare to navigate the complex dynamics of their new triad marriage.
