# 114 - The Sealed Hall
On Wednesday, June 21, 1843, from approximately 2:24 PM to 3:12 PM in the Great Hall of the Palace of Loth Logan, Ryan Adame introduced Rachael, Howard, and William to Queen Maya during the family lunch. Ryan called Rachael 'mama,' crediting her for teaching him family means accepting anyone with open arms and giving him his first hug after the brick room. He praised Howard as the father he never had, a trusted combat leader whose gruff exterior hides a soft heart, and referenced the 'prettiest person in the manor' bet from his early days at Claymore Manor. Howard's ears turned red with embarrassment, and Florence gleefully reminded everyone the bet was never properly settled. William was acknowledged as 'the risen man' whose fifteen years of tunnel-digging enabled their escape from Loren's assault. Maya thanked Rachael for being there when she couldn't be and welcomed William back to the living. Charlie entered and demanded explanations for the divine events, learning about Althena's bathtub appearance and wedding officiation. Mina teased that the goddess 'likes hot water.' Maya requested a demonstration of Ryan's power after lunch. Ryan then demonstrated publicly for the first time with eyes open, using four loops to bend a stream of tea from Maya's kettle into his own cup. Maya froze, processing that she had prayed unsuccessfully for fifteen years while Ryan received power after a bathtub negotiation. Howard immediately asked tactical questions about range and volume limits. Ryan threw bread across the table and caught it mid-air with six loops, testing resistance and adjusting mid-catch. He cited Loren's potential agents as justification for keeping secrets, revealing they found two moles in Claymore Manor. Maya confirmed Loren had fifteen years to plant spies in Duran's court and acknowledged Ryan's caution was justified. Charlie asked for threat assessment parameters. Ryan requested the room be cleared of all servants. Maya ordered all staff out, Charlie sealed all entrances, and Howard swept for listening devices. Charlotte proposed a controlled public narrative revealing only 'lost prince with divine blessing' with no specifics about demonstrated power. Ryan then demonstrated steel interception using fifteen pre-wrapped loops, pulling Estra's karambit from her combat grip across the room. Howard confirmed the power bypasses physical possession to act on matter directly, meaning Ryan could disarm an attacker mid-strike. Charlie asked about arrow interception. Maya touched Ryan's face and asked whether his power can protect him from non-physical threats—manipulation, betrayal, and the things that hurt in ways blades cannot. Charlotte provided anchoring support through the bond. Cora documented Trial 49.
