# 055 - Bench Vigil and Departure

Late night on Saturday, June 17, 1843, at Claymore Manor. Rather than return to his quarters, Ryan Adame chooses to sleep on a hard oak bench in the East Wing corridor twenty paces from Howard Blakeney Ragnar's door, positioning himself to protect Charlotte and Florence Claymore while monitoring for intruders. The unforgiving wood presses against his partially-healed back wound, a gash that has closed but left stiff scar tissue, while the cold mist seeping through the manor chills his thin slippers. Inside Howard's room, the group finalizes plans to rescue Alec Hamilton from Valheim monastery before Loren arrives Tuesday at dusk. Charlotte and Florence separately debate who should bring Ryan a blanket; Florence wins the silent contest, emerging to cover him with rough-spun wool smelling of lavender and pipe smoke, her fingers carefully avoiding the scab on his palm. Half-conscious, Ryan thanks her, murmuring his need to stay close "just in case," before descending into exhausted sleep.

After roughly twenty minutes to an hour, Charlotte rouses him at approximately 3:45 AM with news that the plan is ready. Despite the bench's brutality and his wounds pulling tight, Ryan rises more agile than expected—attributing his resilience to past trauma training his body to sleep on hard surfaces. The group mobilizes for a 4:00 AM sharp departure: four geldings saddled in the stable courtyard, provisions packed for three days, and Thomas Greaves holding the west gate. Howard outlines the extraction route: six hours hard riding to reach the monastery by dawn prayer when chanting monks provide cover, entering through the western approach with better cover but limited escape routes, then evacuating through the western pass to waiting Ragnar longships on the coast if necessary.

Ryan questions Estra Hamilton's absence, finding her inside refusing to ride and threatening to kill her horse rather than be separated from the household. He insists she join, declaring that the blood vigil has made her a blood-sister to the Claymores and legally family; more pragmatically, she possesses unique identifying knowledge of Alec (a freckle on his left earlobe, his habit of biting his lip when frightened) and understands Loren's tactics. Estra emerges suspicious but pragmatic, her amber eyes flecked with red from dromamine use, karambit spinning in her fingers. Charlotte coldly warns the group that she will leave behind anyone who slows them, injury or family bonds notwithstanding—a pointed glance shared between Ryan's scarred back and Florence's concealed discomfort. Florence, hiding a splinted ankle that trembles beneath her riding leathers, competes with Ryan's stoicism, insisting she can scale walls regardless of her injury. Eva Riensdauter reports the mist is thickening to reduce visibility to twenty yards on the ridge, warning of ambush vulnerability, but Charlotte overrides the tactical concern: they cannot wait for clearer weather without missing the window to intercept Alec before Loren's Tuesday dusk visit. The team stands at the stable threshold, mounting up as the cold settles into Ryan's scar tissue and Florence's hidden sprain, committed to the ride despite deteriorating conditions.
