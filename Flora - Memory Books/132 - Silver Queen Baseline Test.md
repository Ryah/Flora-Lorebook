# 132 - Silver Queen Baseline Test
On Thursday, June 22, 1843, at approximately 6:22 AM in the East Kitchen Courtyard of the Palace of Loth Logan, Ryan Adame proposed inviting Mina and Cora to observe a practice session focused on Charlotte's abilities rather than his own. Charlotte stiffened at the casual mention of her secret silver ice power in mixed company, her fingers tightening on his waistcoat. Florence supported the idea, noting Mina was already Charlotte's anchor's sister and Cora had been documenting everything. Mina, still lying on the grass, deduced this was not merely about anchor holds. 

Charlotte then formally revealed her secret to Mina: when Ryan shares divine power through their bond, it manifests as silver light rather than gold, allowing her to crystallize water and create ice formations—a harmonic accident Althena herself called unintended and the only variable Loren does not know exists. Mina accepted the secret with steady gravity, promising to protect it as she had protected Ryan in the dark for eight years. Florence added her own commitment to secrecy. Charlotte agreed to include Cora for documentation and Claire, who had already witnessed the power. Mina quipped about eighteen pages of documentation including footnotes, earning a near-smile from Charlotte. 

Ryan then playfully teased Mina about checking palace walls and revealed that Howard had noticed the silver glow around Charlotte's fingers during the projection demonstration but chose not to pry, earning Mina's immediate respect. Ryan added that Queen Maya had stationed extra guards and likely deduced Charlotte is the anchor, choosing to protect rather than interrogate. Charlotte processed this as unexpected strategic validation. Florence reframed it warmly as family protecting family, noting Charlotte now had two mothers—one who wanted to weaponize her and one quietly stationing guards. Mina shifted to tactical questions about ice crystallization speed and combat applications. Ryan proposed a methodical testing protocol: find maximum safe loop capacity first, establish minimum thresholds, then explore combat applications—mirroring his own training progression. He expressed concern about the sensory link's unknown transmission limits and noted even Althena was curious about this unprecedented phenomenon. Charlotte acknowledged the link was never designed to carry divine power and stressed finding its limits before Loren forces the issue. As they approached the solarium, Cora was visible through the glass door, already documenting with ink-stained fingers beside a basin of water. 

Mina asked the critical unvoiced question: what happens to Charlotte if the bond breaks under strain. Ryan's face grew serious as he described the bone-rattling white-noise pain he experienced when pushing divine power past safe limits during projection—an experience so dangerous Althena personally intervened to stop him. He warned that if the sensory link has a similar threshold, Charlotte might suffer pain severe enough to make her unwilling to continue, clarifying this was not weakness but a mortal limit no one else should ever experience. Mina shifted from tactical assessment to protective concern, demanding immediate stoppage at the first sign of feedback and threatening to physically remove Charlotte from the basin if necessary. Florence echoed the concern tenderly. Charlotte reaffirmed her commitment to controlled testing, extended her hand to Mina in trust, and Mina accepted, holding her grip. Charlotte argued it was better to find the threshold in controlled conditions than when Loren had a blade to her throat. 

Ryan then instructed Mina not to intervene physically if something went wrong, as they can do nothing physically to help, and he could feel Charlotte's state through the bond and release the thread faster than anyone could react. Mina's job was perimeter security, observation, and brainstorming. He appreciated the mental image of her dragging a "water-logged ice queen" across the floor. 

Charlotte expressed cautious intrigue about Ryan's long-term proposal to maintain a one-loop connection throughout the day using the knot technique to test sustained resonance, fatigue rates, and emotional bleed-through. Florence joked about Charlotte glowing during dinner to unsettle the Duranian court. Claire Whitehill arrived with a breakfast tray of bread, tea, and butter, flustered at nearly missing the start. Cora had prepared a documentation grid with columns for loop count, water response, physical state, and feedback. Estra contributed dryly from her post near the basin wall, noting that documentation preceded winning. Charlotte stepped to the basin, her reflection mirrored in the still water, extended her hand toward Ryan, and declared readiness for the first one-loop baseline test.
