# 100 - Pre-Wedding Preparations

On Tuesday, June 20, 1843, between 7:30 PM and 8:40 PM, in the Lord's Quarters and East Wing of Claymore Manor, preparations unfolded for the 10:00 PM church gathering and the 10:45 PM wedding. The scene began with Ryan tickling Charlotte in their bedroom, breaking her administrative composure and making her laugh uncontrollably on the floor. Althena, the divine presence reclining in the copper bathtub in the adjacent bathroom, observed with scholarly amusement, noting Charlotte's 'Tuesday sensitivity' and her 'snort.' Charlotte, disheveled and flushed, eventually surrendered to the moment, asking Ryan to make her laugh once more before they had to be strong. Ryan then rose to dress for the evening, asking about attire. Charlotte recommended a green waistcoat with silver threading for mobility and knife concealment, and black trousers with reinforced-toe boots. Althena announced she would manifest as 'radiance'—golden light with specific density—rather than wear fabric, having not done so since the Third Age. Charlotte noted this would terrify the church elders sufficiently for distraction. 

Ryan then mischievously theorized about testing their new Sacred Bond, which shares sensation, by pleasuring himself during Charlotte's future formal meetings. Charlotte reacted with threatened administrative precision, attempting to negotiate contractual boundaries and penalties for violation, while flushing crimson and gripping his wrist desperately. Ryan cited Article 3 of their marriage contract, which grants him absolute autonomy over his physical person, using her own legal precision against her. Charlotte, after a spike of arousal-terror, acknowledged his legal correctness but negotiated for discretion, offering deferred 'payment' through restraints and her imagination if he disrupted her sessions. She named herself 'Charlotte' without title as a vulnerability offering. Althena observed the recursive nature of their courtship through constraints. 

Florence then burst into the room with updates on banners and blue silk for Estra, helping Ryan into his waistcoat. Ryan revealed Althena was still in the bathtub, and Florence, with delighted awe, approached the bathroom despite Charlotte's attempts to maintain the schedule. Althena invited Florence in, observing the contrast between Charlotte's 'controlled architecture' and Florence's 'kinetic energy.' Ryan fled the room after jokingly warning Florence that Althena was a 'menace' who turns people into shrubs. Charlotte, feeling Ryan's amusement through the bond, propelled him downstairs, admitting on the landing that she knew he could feel her panic. In the main hall, Howard, Mina, and Rachael coordinated security. Florence appeared at the top of the stairs to announce Althena had agreed to officiate the wedding. Charlotte released Ryan's hand to manage the security briefing.

Ryan went to check on Estra in the east wing, covering his eyes to avoid seeing her wedding dress. Estra, panicked and pacing in her heavy blue dress, admitted she wasn't okay, fearing she couldn't fight in her attire. Alec, her brother, calmly reported she'd been drawing escape routes. Ryan reassured her by noting Loren's dominant arm was injured from his dagger strike, extending potential breach time, and that Althena's presence and Ragnar perimeter security offered protection. Estra grounded herself by pressing Ryan's hand to her racing heart and attempting breathing exercises. She moved behind him, touched his jaw, and asked him to look without seeing the dress. Ryan opened his eyes, called her 'beautiful,' and expressed excitement. Estra, flushed and emotional, admitted she didn't know how to be a bride. Alec then presented her with a small, rough wooden ring he had carved for the ceremony. Estra accepted it, overwhelmed. Through the Sacred Bond, Charlotte, in the main hall, felt Ryan's affection for Estra, experiencing a complex lurch of jealousy and relief while forcing administrative focus. The group had approximately eighty-five minutes remaining until the church elders' arrival.
