# 053 - Wedding Night Vigil

Late Friday night bleeding into Saturday, June 17, 1843, at Claymore Manor. Following the Oracle-sealed triadic wedding, Ryan finds Estra Hamilton lingering in the Great Hall's periphery, damp from the mist and guarding with her karambit. He reassures her regarding the extraction of her brother Alec from Valheim, confident in a 48-hour timeline despite Loren's eleven-day deadline, and references the "fresh bread" code emphasizing that family comes first. Charlotte observes from a distance with rigid anxiety, her legal mind calculating risks even as jealousy strains her composure; Ryan pulls his wives close, whispering promises of exclusive privileges and reassuring Charlotte that while Estra may be interesting, Charlotte and Florence alone hold his heart.

The celebration continues with the cutting of the rosemary cake adorned with candied violets and gold leaf, performed with three hands upon a pearl-handled ceremonial knife under Rachael's tearful supervision. Once the festivities conclude and the household retreats, the trio makes for the master suite, but Estra intercepts them on the Grand Staircase. She requests the Hamilton Blood Vigil, a Duranian ritual to bind her as blood-sister to the marriage before the door closes. Despite Florence's frustrated exhaustion and Charlotte's recognition of the manipulation's timing, they agree. With Rachael, Mina, and Cora as witnesses, Ryan, Charlotte, and Florence each cut their palms with a steel blade, mingling blood with Estra's to seal the sanctuary bond, ensuring Loren cannot claim Estra as a tool without violating Claymore protection.

With Estra taking her vigil outside the master door, the wedding night proceeds in the master suite where a copper tub steams near a lit fire. Charlotte, shedding her black velvet gown and controlled mask, assumes dominant command at Ryan's explicit urging to break him without restraint. Florence, naked and still sporting her splinted ankle and bandaged hand, eagerly submits to Charlotte's direction. Charlotte binds Ryan's wrists to the four-poster bed with a silk sash, rendering him immobile beneath their dual attentions. The scene escalates with Charlotte exercising cold, sadistic authority—commanding Florence to impale herself and remain perfectly still while Charlotte torments them both with friction and denial, establishing the power dynamic of the marriage through silk, command, and the threat of release withheld.
