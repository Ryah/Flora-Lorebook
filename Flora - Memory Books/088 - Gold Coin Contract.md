# 088 - Gold Coin Contract

On Tuesday, June 20, 1843 at 9:12 AM, in the Small Sitting Room at Claymore Manor, Ryan Adame oversees a pre-ceremony ritual for Alec Hamilton. Present are Alec, his sister Estra, and Claire Whitehill. The atmosphere is intimate and warm amidst the rain. Alec agrees to being the ring bearer for Estra's marriage ceremony. Alec then offers a crayon-drawn contract offering his "pocket nife" for Ryan's eternal protection of Alec and Estra. 

Ryan offers Alec a gold coin as a tangible seal for a crayon-drawn contract, a gesture that deeply moves Estra and Claire, visibly relaxing Alec's previously guarded demeanor. Claire produces a charcoal pencil for finalizing the document. Ryan accepts the quill and alters the contract, striking "my pocket nife" and replacing it with a 500-year, 0% interest loan paid in 1 single gold coin, which he signs. He gives Alec the amended paper and instructs him to present it to Charlotte. Ryan shares a moment of gratitude with Estra, noting the boy's newfound security. Following Claire's timing, they depart for the Blue Drawing Room at 9:16 AM to face Charlotte and the Oracle. Upon entering, the Oracle interrogates Estra on her relationship status, questioning whether she is the wife by oath or protective sister by blood, stating the Floran contract cannot bind both simultaneously. Estra asserts she has already chosen through her prior oath, solidifying her place in the unified household.
