# 129 - We Need Therapy
On Thursday, June 22, 1843, at approximately 5:44 AM in the East Kitchen Courtyard of the Palace of Loth Logan, Ryan Adame lay on his back in the grass, having dismissed Mina's offer to retrieve the dead drop package under the thornless rose bush, arguing that the risk of exposing surveillance wasn't worth the low-priority intelligence and that his projection ability's vulnerability window was acceptably short. Mina, who had been lying beside him, sat up abruptly with a fierce, trembling fist and told him not to talk about himself as an acceptable loss. Her voice cracked as she referenced the eight years she had brought him bread in the dark, saying she wouldn't let Loren have him for three seconds or anything. Estra quietly noted from her post near the east gate that Ryan was expressing trust, not expendability. Charlotte, against the stone wall, gave a calm tactical assessment agreeing with the logic. Ryan then clarified he didn't think himself expendable, but was realistic and prepared to kill Loren if she touched his family, adding that he hadn't yet felt the weight of his first kill during Alec's rescue and remarked it was good they were getting therapists later. Florence, cross-legged beside him, made a half-laugh half-exhale at the casual mention of therapists, and Charlotte confirmed she was already drafting a list of candidates for Florence to vet for bedside manner. Estra shared that the first kill often doesn't feel heavy right away, but noticing its absence is a good sign. Mina's anger softened into something more complicated; she called him a stubborn, compartmentalizing idiot but said she'd hold him to his promise not to let Loren take him. She then lay back down on the grass beside Ryan, close enough for her hand to rest near his, and noted there were fifteen minutes until Marten arrived. The sky had turned fully blue, pink clouds faded, and the group settled into a still, family peace after having said hard things and survived them.
