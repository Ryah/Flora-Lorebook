# 076 - Blue Dress Blood Debt

Monday, June 19, 1843, 9:05 AM to 9:20 AM at Claymore Manor. In the dining room, Ryan Adame signs a blood debt document proposing a wager: if he wins the rematch, Estra Hamilton must choose a dress color (blue), though he will not force her to wear it. Estra, stunned that he mentions dresses publicly while Howard Blakeney Ragnar and Charlotte Claymore observe, realizes he is offering her agency she never had under Loren. She signs the document after selecting blue for a potential wedding—"the color of the sky after a storm" and Ryan's eyes when reckless—accepting the nickname "Estie" for the first time without correction. Charlotte snatches the parchment to seal it with blue wax, insisting on strict parameters for the 6 PM match given the looming Loren deadline. Howard establishes Ragnar rules: no strikes to throat, eyes, or groin; no biting or weapons; first to yield or incapacitation with a ten-second count; and he will stop the match if permanent injury threatens. A betting pool forms with Rachael placing ten gold on Estra's spirit, Florence betting ten gold on both combatants, Ryan wagering five gold on Estra (citing uncertainty and bragging rights), and Alec contributing his single copper coin on his sister. Cora calculates the odds as statistically even, noting Ryan's unpredictability versus Estra's technical precision. Mina declares she will serve as witness—not seconds—to ensure both walk away whole, acknowledging she trained Estra to strike hard and fast. Mrs. Marie interrupts with calls that her kitchen garden pie grows cold, herding the group toward the kitchen with maternal authority. As they relocate, Ryan reveals he spent twenty years in literal chains and complete darkness, maintaining silence to prove value to his mother, and that only Mina and Cora have seen his unmasked self—the side that will surface at 6 PM. Estra responds by acknowledging the cost of becoming small to survive, promising she will not look away when his darkness surfaces, accepting the role of witness to his trauma. Alec asks how kindness survived such captivity; Mrs. Marie observes that some grow thorns while others grow roots. Howard offers the Ragnar forge metaphor: the fire that breaks the blade also teaches it to cut. The scene closes with the group gathered around the steaming cinnamon-scented pie, the blood debt signed and sealed, the match set for 6:00 PM under Ragnar rules, and both combatants committed to fighting without masks or restraint.
