# 035 - Assassin Identified in Parlor

Thursday, June 15, 1843, from 8:40 PM to 9:50 PM at Claymore Manor. The scene opens in the Blue Drawing Room where Ryan Adame and Charlotte Claymore strategize against Countess Loren's looming threat. Having decoded Loren's cipher revealing a 21-day extraction deadline via the ship Sea Witch, they discuss building a legal and political fortress to protect Ryan. Ryan asserts his role as Charlotte's partner rather than a subservient puppet, declaring he will fight for the Claymore family regardless of his true origins.

When Florence Claymore, Cora Adame, and Mina Adame arrive, the group shares critical intelligence: Loren conditioned Ryan as a child to create loyalty through rescue trauma, planning to use him as a diplomatic bridge between Flora and Duran. Ryan reveals he remembers the face of the assassin who wounded him three weeks prior and believes she remains hidden within the household. They proceed to the Parlor, where Ryan delivers a speech to the assembled household, apologizing for recent chaos and affirming his loyalty to the Claymore family while covertly scanning faces. He identifies the assassin—Estra Hamilton, a servant in a grey dress standing by the grandfather clock—and signals Charlotte by touching his left ear.

Returning to the Lord's Quarters with Howard Blakeney Ragnar, Rachael Blakeney, and Henry Whitmore, Ryan reveals his identity as the kidnapped Prince of Duran, missing for fifteen years, and notes that his brother Prince Charles has been searching for him. The group realizes this revelation fundamentally alters their political strategy, as Loren intended to rule through his bloodline. Rather than immediate capture, they formulate a plan to recruit Estra as a double agent, leveraging potential Duranian patriotic loyalty or personal conflict evident in her pained expression during the original attack. Ryan vouches for Henry's loyalty when questioned by Howard.

Tactical preparations are established: Henry reports Estra retires to the attic at ten bells and reads Duranian texts in the east wing library. The group plans a covert "accidental" encounter for Ryan in the library the following night, with Howard and Mina watching from concealment while Charlotte and Florence monitor from servant passages. Cora and Mina are assigned adjacent quarters in the east wing. The scene concludes with Charlotte and Florence declaring they will guard Ryan through the night, maintaining a protective watch as he rests before the dangerous recruitment attempt.
