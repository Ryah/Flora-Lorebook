# 037 - Sleep Confessions and Morning Alliance

Late on the evening of Thursday, June 15, 1843, in the candlelit Lord's Quarters of Claymore Manor, Ryan Adame declares his intention to renounce his position as Prince of Duran rather than leave Charlotte and Florence, stating he would rather remain their husband than command a nation. Charlotte challenges this devotion, fearing it stems from gratitude-based loyalty engineered by Loren rather than genuine love, confessing that she has been conditioned to suspect poison in every sweet thing and cannot trust absolute declarations of love. Florence defends Ryan, citing his choice to carry her up a hill while his back split open as evidence of authentic devotion beyond conditioning. Ryan admits he cannot distinguish between love and gratitude but commits to following his heart regardless. Charlotte demands he prove his devotion through consistent daily choice, not words, extracting a promise that he will confess immediately if he ever discovers his feelings are manufactured.

The scene transitions to dawn on Friday, June 16, 1843. Charlotte and Florence discover that Ryan talks in his sleep and answers questions truthfully. They conduct a sleep-interrogation, documenting his unfiltered responses in a leather notebook with charcoal. He unconsciously reveals that he considers Cora smarter than Charlotte but acknowledges Charlotte's tactical superiority; when asked who is prettier, he refuses to choose, describing Florence as sunlight and Charlotte as moonlight, declaring both perfect. He admits to sexual fantasies involving both women simultaneously in the bath and against walls, expressing desire for a dynamic where Charlotte commands while Florence submits (and vice versa). Most critically, when asked if his love is real or conditioned, he confirms it is genuine, claiming them as "mine" and "my wives" without the influence of gratitude.

Upon waking to their teasing, Ryan finds they have documented every confession. He deflects their demand to choose who is prettier by joking that Howard is the prettiest person in the manor, reminding them to later ask Dr. Mercer to sew his mouth shut before sleeping. When Howard Blakeney Ragnar arrives early for the war council, Ryan fulfills his bet by calling the scarred Valheimr warlord "pretty" to his face. Howard accepts the compliment with stoic amusement, declaring that in his homeland such words require blood or marriage, but settles for alliance, noting that Ryan's madness makes the family interesting.

The group discovers that Ryan's surgical stitches from his June 15th operation have reopened during the night from thrashing in his sleep. Charlotte performs emergency field dressing, assessing that three stitches pulled but no arterial bleeding occurred, while Florence insists he seek proper medical attention after the council. Despite the reopened wound and the looming 21-day extraction deadline established by Loren, the triad shares a moment of domestic intimacy as Ryan offers his arms for support and they walk together toward the breakfast room, planning to face the war council as a united front.
