# 084 - Threefold Embrace in the Parlor

On Monday, June 19, 1843 at approximately 8:30 PM, Charlotte Claymore suffered a dissociation episode triggered by a fire-related sound in the Claymore Manor parlor. Ryan Adame responded by physically positioning himself in Charlotte's lap, offering weight and touch to anchor her. Florence Claymore remained present, pressing against Ryan's legs in a protective triangle of intimacy. Ryan reassured Charlotte that comfort cost him nothing, highlighting his devotion despite his physical injuries from a prior blood debt confrontation with Loren Claymore. Charlotte, usually armored by legal precision and contract, allowed herself to be vulnerable, acknowledging that she had never given herself permission to feel safe. She clung to the moment for 'five minutes' before returning to her analytical mind, though she admitted she did not want to push Ryan away. Florence gently urged Charlotte to accept this comfort, reminding her that Ryan was paying for everyone. After Ryan promised to stay until Estra Hamilton returned, the wife reunited them in a shared moment of grounding. Estra entered quietly after putting Alec to bed, noting his drawings of guardian dragons watching over the manor. She declined to sit on the furniture, instead sitting on the floor beside Charlotte to maintain the intimate circle. The scene ended with Florence drifting toward sleep, and the group suspended in a shared, tranquil silence within the firelight.
