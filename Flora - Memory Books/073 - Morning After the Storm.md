# 073 - Morning After the Storm

On Monday, June 19, 1843, between 6:45 AM and 7:00 AM, morning breaks clearly over Claymore Manor's West Wing Guest Room following the previous night's storm. The temperature rests at 62°F with clear skies, rain having washed the windows clean. Ryan Adame awakens after ensuring the Hamilton siblings slept peacefully through the night—a rarity for both. Estra Hamilton remains in deep sleep, her body relaxed against Ryan, blonde hair tangled across the pillow, wearing wrinkled linen clothes from the previous day, bandages on her knuckles loosened from wear. Alec Hamilton stirs in the armchair where he slept, emerging slowly without his usual fear, stretching contentedly as his stomach growls loudly enough to break the morning silence.

Alec approaches the window barefoot, observing the vanished storm, then turns to Ryan with whispered excitement about making the promised Kitchen Garden Pie. Ryan suggests Alec wake his sister so they can begin. Alec climbs carefully onto the bed, gently touching Estra's shoulder, whispering that morning has come and the storm is gone. Estra merely mumbles "Five more minutes," her voice thick with sleep, confusing the present for her past trauma: "The patrol does not come until dawn."

Distressed, Alec explains to Ryan that Estra sometimes believes she is still guarding his cell at the monastery, thinking him locked away by Loren. He kneels beside her, shaking her gently while grounding her in their new reality: "We're at Claymore Manor. We're safe. You rescued me. Remember? Ryan helped. We're going to make pie today. Apple pie. With the recipe Mama taught us."

Estra's eyes snap open wild and unfocused, her body tensing for combat before recognizing Alec's worried face and Ryan's solid presence beside her. Breathing hitching, she cups Alec's cheek with trembling fingers, confirming he is truly there. She sits up slowly, color rising to her cheeks as she notices her slept-in clothes and shared bed, then expresses wonder at having slept through the entire night without nightmares or vigilance—something she cannot remember experiencing since before Loren took them.

Rising to stretch, her linen shirt rides up briefly to reveal scarred skin on her lower back. She embraces the plan for Kitchen Garden Pie, listing required ingredients: apples, cinnamon, butter, flour, and sugar, noting the kitchen will be busy with breakfast. Alec excitedly suggests seeking Mrs. Marie, who had given him extra bacon the previous day and commented on his thinness needing remedy. Estra laughs genuinely, agreeing he is too skinny and ruffling his hair.

Turning to Ryan with softened intimacy, Estra quietly thanks him for staying and being present upon her awakening, admitting she had not expected it to matter so much, yet it did profoundly. The scene closes as the household below begins stirring with slamming doors and distant voices, the trio poised to descend together into a day promising flour, apples, and the possibility of healing.
