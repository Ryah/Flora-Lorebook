# 033 - Kitchen Confrontation and Message Extraction

Thursday, June 15, 1843, 7:25 PM to 7:42 PM. In the Main Corridor of Claymore Manor, Ryan Adame confronts Edmund Girard after severing his Achilles tendon, explaining his philosophy of "an eye for an eye" and asserting that the injury serves as punishment for Edmund harming Florence and as a reminder of loyalty. Ryan promises to petition for Edmund to retain his employment with carriage accommodations, identifying himself as Charlotte's blade before following her toward the kitchen.

By 7:28 PM, the group enters the kitchen where Margot stands preparing a pigeon courier. Margot reveals devastating intelligence: Ryan is allegedly the kidnapped Duranian prince, taken fifteen years ago and raised by Eleanor Castor as a "planted" husband for Charlotte specifically designed to create a bloodline bridge between nations through gratitude and loyalty engineered by abuse. As Margot threatens that the message is already written and will fly to Loren Claymore, Ryan instinctively throws his dagger, pinning the pigeon's wing to the brick wall without killing the bird, trapping the copper message tube. In a private whisper to Florence, Ryan confesses the throw was not skill but pure uncalculated instinct.

Cora Adame analyzes the tube, identifying a sophisticated double-wax seal (paraffin over beeswax) containing a hair-trigger filament mixed with silver nitrate that would destroy the message if opened incorrectly. Charlotte Claymore experiences a severe panic attack, attempting to seize the tube from Cora out of fear that failure will allow her mother Loren to destroy everything she has built. Ryan embraces Charlotte to calm her, acknowledging he values both her calculated precision and Florence's passionate sacrifice.

Cora successfully extracts the tube using Ryan's dagger with surgical precision, calculating a seventeen-degree angle to peel the outer wax without snapping the inner seal. A tense standoff follows between Charlotte and Cora over who should decode the message, with Charlotte demanding control as Countess and Cora asserting her right as the extractor. Ryan mediates by proposing collaborative work, requiring Cora to explain her chemical methods (using lemon juice to reveal heat-sensitive ink rather than flame) while Charlotte shares her authority and resources.

Ryan instructs Florence, Howard, Mina, and Rachael to remain in the kitchen guarding Margot. He then escorts Charlotte and Cora to the Blue Drawing Room, where Charlotte grants Cora one hour to decode the message using supplies including lemon juice, white vinegar, a candle, and a clean glass plate. Charlotte demands step-by-step explanations of the process, declaring, "We begin as equals, or we do not begin at all," establishing a tense partnership to uncover Loren's secrets and the truth of Ryan's parentage.
