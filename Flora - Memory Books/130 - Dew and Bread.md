# 130 - Dew and Bread
On Thursday, June 22, 1843, at approximately 6:07 AM in the East Kitchen Courtyard of the Palace of Loth Logan, Ryan Adame teased Mina about her earlier vulnerable statement regarding children, asking Charlotte to document it. Mina threatened him with seventeen ways to make it look like an accident (eighteen counting the flour sack). Charlotte dryly recited a mock documentation of the moment. Florence demanded to hear about the flour sack but was refused. Ryan then turned to Marten Voss and offered to show him "something cool," mentally wrapping one loop of golden thread around his finger. Marten, a fourteen-year-old scullion with flour-dusted hands and a burn scar on his knuckle, agreed with cautious curiosity. Florence squeaked with excitement. Estra advised keeping it small to avoid attracting attention. Ryan then demonstrated a new application of his divine power—pushing instead of pulling, catching multiple dew droplets simultaneously. He gathered morning dew from the grass into a single floating sphere, then drank it casually while reclining. Marten witnessed his first miracle: water floating in the air. His reaction was visceral awe—cracked voice, trembling hands. Charlotte noted the technical advancement (pushing, multiple targets). Florence beamed with delight. Mina called him a show-off but her voice betrayed warmth. Estra watched from her post with professional softening. Ryan then confessed the technique was unintentional, having felt multiple droplets instead of one. He playfully told Mina that God gave him powers so he had a right to show them off. He then demonstrated the same power on bread, catching a piece from the basket and pulling it to his hand. He made a shushing gesture, telling Marten the bread trick was a big secret. Marten, rambling in disbelief, promised to keep the secret, even from Petra, if Ryan wished. Mina continued to tease him about being a show-off. Florence praised Marten for being good at keeping secrets, noting his immediate instinct to protect what he saw. Marten, ears red, explained that his sister Petra said Ryan and the group were the good guys, and he was starting to understand. He then asked about the dragon-drawing kid, whether he knew about the power. Estra, without turning, answered that Alec knew some of it but not all, and warned Marten that Alec would probably ask about the bread. Marten, calmer and steadier, affirmed he would keep all the secrets, stating he was good at keeping secrets. The group settled into a quiet moment of trust and connection, the bread basket between them and the sky fully blue.
