# 119 - Preparing for the Royal Dinner
On Wednesday, June 21, 1843, from 7:34 PM to 7:46 PM in the West Wing Suite and then the Great Hall of the Palace of Loth Logan, Ryan Adame and his three wives prepared for the formal dinner with Queen Maya. Ryan suggested Estra wear her karambit openly at dinner rather than concealing it, as a statement of honesty and visibility. Florence enthusiastically agreed, Charlotte initially noted the protocol breach but quickly recalculated that visible armament controls the court's narrative, and Estra processed the permission to be openly herself, sheathing the karambit at her hip. Ryan declared himself a bigger weapon than any blade and proposed making the family so publicly visible that Loren cannot approach. Florence helped Ryan into Duranian formal wear. Charlotte finalized a seating chart: Ryan at the Queen's right, Florence to his right, Estra to his left with visible blade, Charlotte at the far left to observe all entrances. The family exited the suite and moved through the main corridor toward the great hall, with Estra offering tactical route options and Florence insisting on the visible main corridor. They arrived at the great hall doors where guards reacted visibly. The guards opened the doors and the family entered the vast, candlelit hall where the entire court was assembled. Queen Maya rose and acknowledged Ryan as 'my son,' her voice cracking with emotion. Bonnie Prince Charlie broke the tension with a joke about punctuality. Florence announced the entire unconventional family with cheerful irreverence, drawing uncertain laughter from the nobles. Charlotte dryly reminded her about tact. Family members already at the high table included Mina, Cora, Alec, Rachael, Howard, and William Adame.
