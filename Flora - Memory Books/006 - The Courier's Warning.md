# 006 - The Courier's Warning

Wednesday, June 14, 1843, between 4:30 PM and 4:55 PM. Ryan enters the main hall to find Rachael rigid beside the fireplace, holding a sealed letter with black wax bearing Eleanor's crude thumbprint seal. Courier Callista Venn, sun-darkened and mud-spattered from three days' hard riding, stands nearby having delivered correspondence from the Amber Isles. Ryan breaks the seal and reads Eleanor's manipulative letter on cheap parchment, which references his past abuse ('the basement,' 'the chains'), mentions his sisters with thinly veiled threats, and reveals Eleanor knows of Rachael's inquiries. Rachael produces her own letter revealing Eleanor has arranged to sell sixteen-year-old Cora to a Durant merchant family by month's end.

Callista provides critical eyewitness intelligence from the Adame household: she saw Mina (tall, dark-haired, with faded finger-shaped bruises on her forearm) and Cora (younger, light-haired, with raw red knuckles from scrubbing stone floors). The house smelled of lye suggesting recent blood cleaning, and featured a padlocked door with knee-height scratches. Cora secretly passed Callista a folded linen note containing Mina's coded warning: 'She knows Claymore is moving. She has a buyer in Durant. Cora. Before July. Please hurry. We are still alive.' Additionally, Callista witnessed a second rider leaving the estate carrying documents bearing the Durant diplomatic seal, suggesting the sale may already be finalized.

Ryan requested to fetch Florence, and Florence arrives breathless and barefoot, demanding immediate action. Ryan negotiates Callista's retention as a household consultant at quadruple retainer pay with equal privileges and freedom of movement, arranging a replacement courier for her pharmaceutical delivery to avoid raising suspicion. Callista then reveals tactical intelligence: a narrow fisherman's path leads to a cove half a mile from the Adame property, and Thursday (the following day) is the weekly supply delivery when the household is distracted, creating a sixteen-hour extraction window. The group confronts the tactical reality that the narrow cliff path limits the team to two or three people, requiring a surgical strike. Rachael reveals Eleanor has filed a petition claiming Ryan mentally unfit, raising the stakes that the extraction must succeed before legal and diplomatic entanglements escalate.
