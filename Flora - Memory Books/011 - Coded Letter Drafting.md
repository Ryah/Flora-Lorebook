# 011 - Coded Letter Drafting

Wednesday, June 14, 1843, from 7:48 PM to 8:14 PM across Claymore Manor's Dining Hall and Blue Drawing Room. As dinner concludes, Florence Claymore subtly calms Ryan's anxious bouncing knee beneath the table with a gentle palm press and slow thumb circle before withdrawing without a word. Rachael Blakeney redirects Howard from his fourth bread roll with a single raised eyebrow, then invites Ryan to draft a coded letter to his sisters. She leads him through corridors lit by low-burning sconces to the blue drawing room, where a stoked fire, trimmed quill, uncapped ink pot, and cream-colored paper await.

Rachael instructs Ryan to write authentically without considering security first. Ryan composes a three-section letter: an opening expressing his feelings and describing his sweet new wives and family; a middle paragraph disguised as childlike wonder about household conveniences ("the ice is cold," "the heater is warm," "turning on lights," "fluffing pillows") that actually serves as a private code to verify his identity to Mina and Cora; and a closing section mentioning his memory loss, the assassination attempt on Florence, his recovery, and thanking Mina for self-defense training. He ends with the phrase "Please keep the blanket warm for me."

Ryan insists the middle coded paragraph remain word-for-word intact, explaining that any alteration would change the meaning entirely, though to outsiders it appears as mere excitement over appliances. Rachael reviews the draft by firelight, reading the coded section three times and recognizing its deliberate construction. She approves keeping it unchanged, promising to defend this choice to Charlotte if necessary, while cautioning that mentioning the assassination and recovered memories may accelerate Eleanor's timeline against him, though it will reassure his sisters of his lucidity.

As Ryan prepares to depart, he positions himself with his back to the window, verifies the door is closed, and requests a family meeting after tomorrow's extraction to discuss something important related to his private conversation with Charlotte about the extraction plan. Rachael observes his security-conscious behavior—checking doors and avoiding windows—and notes that he treats the household as potentially compromised by unseen ears, but she agrees to the meeting without pressing for details. Ryan explains that fresh eyes see details others overlook due to normalcy, bids her goodnight, and departs for his drawn bath and rest before the dawn extraction.
