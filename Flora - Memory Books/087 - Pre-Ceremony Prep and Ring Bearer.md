# 087 - Pre-Ceremony Prep and Ring Bearer

Tuesday, June 20, 1843 at 8:40 AM, in the Master Bedroom of Claymore Manor. Ryan urges his wives to prepare for the Oracle, acknowledging the importance of their triumphant marriage contract. Charlotte asserts that their success stems from her meticulous legal preparation and drafting of ironclad amendments, shifting from emotional intimacy to the strategic role of Countess. Florence encourages Estra, noting Charlotte's concealed enjoyment of Ryan's attention, and instructs Estra on composure for the ceremony. Estra struggles with her identity, admitting she only knows how to be a servant or a killer, not a wife. Charlotte forces Estra to meet her gaze, declaring her worth lies in her choices and courage rather than domestic skills. Claire enters with tea and reports the Oracle is asking about the blood debt clause. Florence answers the knock, telling the Oracle the blood debt was paid and Estra chose her family freely. Claire observes the group with deep insight before retreating. Charlotte hands Ryan the beneficiary clause, designating equal inheritance rights for their children, and prepares for the arrival. Estra anxiously asks if their younger brother Alec can attend to prove they are safe; Ryan insists on his presence, deciding Alec will serve as the ring bearer. Charlotte advises Alec needs a task to manage his anxiety and includes a guardianship provision for him in the amendments. Florence enthusiastically suggests a blue pillow embroidered with a dragon. Estra, moved by the inclusion, vows to explain to Alec that Ryan chose him and that he matters, finally believing she has found a true family.
