# 024 - Coastal Extraction

Thursday, June 15, 1843, between 10:28 AM and 10:40 AM. The scene opens as Ryan, Florence Claymore, Mina Adame, and the semi-conscious Cora Adame emerge from a root cellar tunnel onto the Adame House grounds. The morning is clear and 58°F, with gray light flooding the kitchen garden where rosemary bushes tangle with dead vines. Florence, her golden hair completely fallen from its braid and grey trousers mud-stained at the knees, carries the emaciated sixteen-year-old Cora—who has been locked in a closet for three days without food or water—in her arms. Cora is limp as a doll, ribs visible through her torn dress, consciousness flickering. Mina, barefoot with wild dark hair snarled by cobwebs, urges immediate flight, having heard floorboards creaking overhead where Eleanor Adame searches the house.

Ryan hands his waterskin to Florence for Cora, then takes the lead sprinting down Callista's path toward the northern tree line and the coastal cove. The group runs on extreme adrenaline, damp clothes clinging to their bodies. At approximately 10:32 AM, Eleanor's scream cuts through the morning air—she has discovered the empty room and the tunnel entrance. Pursuit begins, boots pounding the path behind them.

On the descent to the cove, where Howard Blakeney Ragnar and Callista Venn wait beside a longship pulled up on the shingle, an alarm sounds from the Adame manor. Florence, startled by the alarm, doesn't notice the wet algae and steps in it. Her ankle rolls with a sickening pop, joint twisting sideways, and she pitches forward, dropping Cora onto the stones near a driftwood log. Cora is uninjured but remains limp, while Florence collapses, her left ankle swelling and discolored, unable to bear weight. She insists Ryan leave her and carry only Cora, sacrificing herself to buy time.

Instead, Ryan lifts Cora over his left shoulder and heaves Florence over his right, ignoring his reopened back wound which bleeds heavily through the bandage with each jarring step. He runs the final stretch carrying both women while shouting for Mina to reach the boat. Mina breaks onto the beach first, alerting the crew. Ryan reaches the longship, handing Cora to Callista and lowering Florence to the gunwale despite her protests. The group scrambles aboard as Eleanor's orange-haired figure appears at the tree line screaming commands, her pursuers too late to intercept.

Howard bellows orders to push off; the crew shoves the longship into the surf, hull scraping against shingle before catching the gray water and current. By 10:35 AM, the boat rows toward a distant headland that will block Eleanor's view. Onboard, Callista checks Cora's vitals—pulse weak, skin translucent and blue-tinged at the extremities from malnutrition and dehydration, requiring immediate sugar water and warmth. Mina, hollow-eyed, clutches her sister's hand and reveals the three-day closet imprisonment.

Florence collapses against Ryan's shoulder, admitting her ankle is likely sprained and her shoulder pulled, but declaring she is "better than okay" because they survived. Ryan's emotional mask cracks; he pulls her into a tearful embrace, thanking her profusely and whispering, "We fucking did it, Flor." Florence notes he carried them both up the hill while bleeding, calling him insane. Howard commands the crew to row toward the larger Ragnar vessel waiting beyond the headland, where doctors, warm beds, and hot food await for the journey back to Claymore Manor.
