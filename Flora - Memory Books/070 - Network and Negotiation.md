# 070 - Network and Negotiation

On Sunday, June 18, 1843, from approximately 6:05 PM to 6:35 PM, Ryan Adame meets with his wives Rachael and Mina in the Blue Drawing Room of Claymore Manor. Ryan absolves Rachael of blame for not rescuing him sooner, asserting that her rapid response after his confession showed more love than his mother ever had. Rachael, overcome with tears, expresses her guilt for not acting on intelligence reports of chains in Eleanor’s basement years ago. Mina argues that Loren would have simply built another cage, regardless of timing, and confesses her own failure for ignoring whispers about a boy being broken. She tells Ryan he is allowed to be angry and to hate them both, but they are not asking him to carry their guilt. Rachael reveals Loren is wounded but not stopped, with ten days until her deadline to take Ryan by force. She offers a sealed list detailing Loren's entire network: three financial channels, four ships (The Sea Witch, Frost Maiden, Black Albatross, and the Grief), and twenty-four agents across three nations. She presents Ryan with the choice of arresting, turning, or using the agents for misinformation, warning that any action will accelerate Loren's timeline. Ryan asserts that the transaction of his marriage was Loren's critical weak point, as it required Eleanor to interact with the outside world, exposing her to Charlotte's scrutiny. Rachael agrees, noting Loren over-engineered Charlotte’s paranoia, which ultimately backfired and led Charlotte to recognize Ryan's true nature. Ryan decides they will wait until after the next day's sparring match (June 19) to fully assemble and decide on the network, wanting to give Alec, Estra, and Charlotte a day of normalcy. He argues that ally retention hinges on their emotional wellbeing, revealing he saw Charlotte's deeply regressed, vulnerable state behind closed doors. Mina reluctantly agrees to the delay, vowing to keep the investigation quiet and make Estra feel she has earned her place. Rachael seals the agent list and agrees to verify safehouse locations discreetly. The scene ends with the group parting to prepare for dinner, with Ryan and Mina reaffirming their strategic alliance and the need for caution.
