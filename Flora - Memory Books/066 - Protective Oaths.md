# 066 - Protective Oaths

Sunday, June 18, 1843, late morning (11:38 AM to 12:05 PM) in Claymore Manor East Wing Guest Chamber. Ryan Adame begins by joking with Alec Hamilton about making Charlotte cry, stating that crying makes people stronger. He then delivers a grave blood oath to the boy, swearing as Ryan Adame-Claymore that he will die before allowing harm to befall him. Alec, small and frail in an oversized nightshirt with visible ribs and bare feet, processes this with grave seriousness, confirming that Ryan means the promise literally; the boy whispers that nobody has ever said such a thing to him before. Alec agrees to meet Mina and Cora across the hall to learn cipher training but insists the chamber door remain wide open so he can hear his sister Estra, pressing his hand to his chest in a gesture mimicking her karambit clutch; Ryan promises to call for him immediately if she stirs.

Florence Claymore, kneeling nearby with a bandaged ankle concealed beneath her yellow sundress, radiates maternal warmth and guides Alec out with open admiration for Ryan's direct honesty. Once alone with the sleeping Estra and Howard Blakeney Ragnar, Ryan discusses his philosophy of protection with the veteran, revealing he sees mirrors of himself in both Hamilton siblings—Alec representing his own overwhelming first days, and Estra representing his trauma. Ryan holds Estra's karambit in a reverse grip as he explains he is shielding both children from the worst of her fragile state, giving her precious seconds alone to compose her mask before facing Alec. Howard observes that Estra will sleep another hour or two, explaining that her body is finally releasing adrenaline like soldiers after their first real battle, and warns that trauma is a common language Ryan has merely learned to speak fluently.

When Estra enters a nightmare, whimpering and clutching the lavender-scented sheets with her bandaged hand, Ryan kneels and whispers the sister cipher code phrase "The bread is fresh," while reassuring her that Alec is safe across the hall. Her amber-rimmed eyes snap open with predator's speed, scanning for threats, before recognizing his voice and collapsing back into the pillows, the tension draining from her combat-tensed frame; the livid scar on her cheek from their earlier clash stands out against her pale skin. As she wakes fully, disoriented and vulnerable in her borrowed linen nightshirt with monastery extraction bruises visible on her sharp collarbones, Ryan asks Howard to fetch Alec while he sits beside her on the feather mattress.

In a moment of raw vulnerability, Ryan confesses that out of everyone in the manor—the wives he is legally bound to, the sisters he shares blood with, the staff he employs—Estra is the first person he actually chose to have there without obligation or blood ties, simply because he found her interesting. Estra reacts with terrified gratitude, admitting she has never been chosen for anything good in her life, only selected as disposable by her Duranian handlers because she was an orphan with quick hands. With trembling bandaged fingers gripping his forearm hard enough to whiten skin, she warns that she will mess up being family—she is too sharp, too sarcastic, too conditioned by her past—but she is trying because he chose her, and she refuses to be his regret. As Howard's heavy footsteps return with Alec's voice following, Estra remains seated beside Ryan, unprepared but present, having accepted the terrifying gift of being chosen.
