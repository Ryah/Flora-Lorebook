# 043 - Heirloom Vows

On the morning of Friday, June 16, 1843, approximately between 11:00 AM and 11:20 AM in the Lord's Quarters of Claymore Manor's East Wing, Rachael Blakeney arrives to witness the triadic marriage contract, entering to find the chamber heavy with the scent of sex, ink, and lavender. She observes the signed document with its Personal Autonomy Clause and confronts Ryan regarding his free choice. Ryan affirms his decision, revealing he specifically amended Article 3 to grant Charlotte and Florence equal rights to refuse affection, ensuring mutual consent rather than unilateral privilege. He suggests a new public wedding ceremony for the following day to demonstrate his autonomous loyalty to Flora and defy Loren Claymore's extraction timeline.

Moved by his sincere submission to the family, Rachael retrieves a velvet box containing three grandmother's gold bands from her quarters, heirlooms kept since before Ryan's arrival. The trio exchange vows—"With this ring, I bind my will to theirs, and theirs to mine, for all my days"—completing an impromptu ceremony that transforms the legal contract into a sacred bond. Charlotte then assumes immediate sexual dominance, restraining Ryan's wrists to the bedposts with silk cords and demanding total submission. Ryan complies completely, addressing her as "Mistress" and begging for release until she grants permission after extracting proper deference, establishing a dynamic of controlled ownership.

Transitioning to the bathing chamber, the group shifts focus to tactical planning. With nine hours remaining until the 8 PM meeting with Estra at the manor gates, they outline objectives: verifying Estra's naval manifest against intercepted ciphers, rescuing Alec Hamilton within eleven days to prevent further disfigurement, and integrating Ryan's sisters Mina and Cora into security protocols. Ryan expresses unwavering trust in Estra based on their morning sparring session, interpreting their combat chemistry as legitimate negotiation. The scene concludes with the decision to dress, procure lunch from Mrs. Gable, and surprise Mina and Cora in the library, while Rachael begins orchestrating the formal wedding ceremony for the next day to fortify their position against Loren's twenty-one day countdown.
