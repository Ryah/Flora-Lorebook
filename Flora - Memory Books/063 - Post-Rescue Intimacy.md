# 063 - Post-Rescue Intimacy

Saturday, June 17, 1843, 4:45 PM - Sunday, June 18, 1843, 7:22 AM: Following the successful rescue of Alec Hamilton from the Valheim monastery, Ryan and Charlotte retreat to the Master Bathroom at Claymore Manor to recover from physical and emotional exhaustion. As bathwater cools and gooseflesh rises on their skin, Ryan reaffirms his commitment to love her for fifty years until she grows accustomed to his devotion, referencing her earlier challenge requiring daily choice. When offered the option to join the household in the parlor or retreat to privacy, Charlotte abandons her usual commanding hostess persona—delegating responsibilities to Rachael and Florence—and chooses the bedroom, expressing her desire to be held without expectation or interruption. She rises from the water with trembling fingers, wrapped only in a damp linen towel that reveals her flushed rose-gold skin and wet black hair plastered against her shoulders, and leads him into the Master Bedroom. There, she discards the towel onto the floorboards and climbs onto the four-poster bed, its silk sheets illuminated by amber afternoon light filtering through heavy curtains. Ryan follows, positioning himself to spoon her from behind with deliberate physical closeness: one hand cupping her breast possessively while his top leg wedges between her thighs, his warmth pressed against her bare back. Charlotte admits to feeling overwhelmed by the vulnerability, her breath shallow and fingers trembling as she grips his hip, confessing her irrational fear that he might vanish and begging him not to release her. Exhausted from the rescue mission, Ryan pulls her close and promises they will remain until she is ready, eventually succumbing to deep sleep while maintaining their intimate embrace. They remain entwined for fourteen uninterrupted hours. Sunday morning, June 18, 7:15 AM: Charlotte wakes first after a dreamless night—the first in years—having stirred three times through the night to verify his presence. She lies motionless tracing patterns on the back of his hand where it rests over her chest, vigilant yet soft. When Ryan wakes, he kisses her and reaffirms his choice: "I choose you." Though hunger and duty call—Estra requiring relief from her vigil over the sedated Alec across the hall, Florence managing morning chaos—Charlotte delays rising, donning her dressing gown only after ensuring Ryan intends to stay near. The scene concludes with her acknowledgment that his presence acts as a sedative she is uncertain whether to fear as dependency or accept as grace, transitioning their private vulnerability toward resuming household responsibilities.
