# 013 - The Closet Confession

Wednesday, June 14, 1843, between 9:28 PM and 9:55 PM. In the Lord's Quarters Bedroom of Claymore Manor, Ryan enters after bathing, damp and bandaged, wearing only a towel around his waist. The room is lit by a single low oil lamp, with burgundy velvet curtains drawn against the moonlight. A tarnished locket rests on the writing desk beside a four-poster bed with white military-folded linens.

At 9:28 PM, Claire enters bearing two folded deep green wool blankets sent by Lady Rachael, ostensibly because the nights run cold in this wing. Dressed in her black maid uniform with white ruffled lace visible at the hemline, she moves with cautious curiosity, her bare feet padding across polished floorboards. She immediately notes Fiona's tearful, hurried departure from the corridor moments before, fishing for explanation while feigning casual delivery.

Ryan questions why nobles treating staff with respect shocks everyone, citing his own treatment of Harvey and Fiona. Claire's demeanor shifts from playful to grave. She confirms such kindness is rare, citing Harry's abuse of Fiona as a toy he was allowed to break, Loren's treatment of staff as experiments in suffering, and Ryan's own mother's chaining of him.

At 9:37 PM, Claire reveals she discovered Ryan in the closet three weeks prior, finding him lying in blood that had soaked through the floorboards and stained the ceiling of the room below—the stain that revealed his location. She recounts his specific words: "Please do not save me. I cannot go back to her," followed by him ceasing to breathe, prompting her to scream loud enough to summon Captain Eva with drawn sword.

Ryan, seated on the bed edge, apologizes and admits he retains no memory of the event—only entering the closet and waking days later. His attempt to joke about blood loss falls flat.

Claire then confesses she sat vigil beside his unconscious form for two nights, holding his left hand while his right bore an IV needle. Around 3 AM on the second night, while she spoke of Loren, Charlotte, and her recurring suffocation nightmares, his fingers squeezed hers once. Physicians dismissed it as involuntary reflex, witnessed also by Florence, but Claire has carried the uncertainty of whether he heard her for three weeks.

By 9:48 PM, Claire concludes her confession, standing and reassembling her professional composure. She declares she owns her choices to sit with him and care whether he lived, then retreats with promises of Thursday breakfast service and extra jam. She exits through the corridor, her footsteps fading.

Alone, Ryan lies back on the bed and speaks into the empty room, resolving to tell Claire when next they meet that he does remember—the squeeze was real. The oil lamp gutters low as the manor settles into silence around 9:55 PM. Ryan drifts toward sleep ahead of Henry's 5:30 AM wake-up call for dawn combat training with Howard, the green wool blankets left folded at the foot of the bed.
