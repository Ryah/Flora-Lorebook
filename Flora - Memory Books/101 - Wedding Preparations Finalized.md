# 101 - Wedding Preparations Finalized

On Tuesday, June 20, 1843, between 8:40 PM and 9:15 PM, final preparations for the wedding and evacuation were made at Claymore Manor. The scene began in the East Wing Guest Chamber, where Ryan Adame comforted Estra Hamilton, who was deeply moved and clutching the rough wooden ring Alec had carved. Ryan assured her God would be between them at the wedding, smiling, and that he was marrying her, not a concept of a bride. Estra, emotionally overwhelmed, ordered him to leave to preserve the surprise of her blue wedding dress. Alec, standing guard at the threshold, announced eighty minutes remained until the church gathering. Through the active Sacred Combat Bond, Charlotte Claymore, in the main hall, felt a phantom warmth of Ryan's affection and openness without context, causing her to lose focus on security maps for three seconds. Mina Adame called from the corridor, urging Ryan downstairs. 

Ryan exited with eyes closed to avoid seeing the dress, joking about Mina being bossy. In the corridor, Mina guided him while Florence Claymore intercepted them to adjust his green waistcoat and silver cravat pin. Ryan entered the main hall, intentionally making his heart flutter so Charlotte would feel included through the bond. Charlotte briefed him on verified tunnel routes and compromised entrances while fighting distraction from the sensory link. 

Ryan then prayed aloud to Althena, asking her to wake the unconscious Oracle. Almost immediately, the Oracle staggered into the hall, claiming Althena had kicked him awake with a threat to turn him into a shrub if he didn't witness the union. Althena's voice echoed from upstairs, denying the kick but confirming the threat. Ryan argued for including the detained Crown Princess Cornelia vi Relani as an unpredictable variable against Loren, embracing chaos. Charlotte, feeling Ryan's earnest trust through the link, agreed under heavy Ragnar escort. 

Florence then eagerly led Ryan, Mina, and the Oracle to the Great Hall to see the completed banners: three panels of blue silk with the Althena symbol embroidered in dimensional gold thread, inscribed 'Four vessels. One covenant.' Florence's competitive mask cracked into vulnerability, asking if it was 'too much,' but Ryan validated her work as perfect. He kissed her and whispered about negotiating a 'reward' from Charlotte, which Florence vowed to earn. The group moved to the chapel, where Ryan finalized the wedding processional and security layout. 

He would stand at the altar; Estra and William would walk the aisle first, followed by Florence and Charlotte flanking the sides. Mina and Cora would form a protective box on opposite flanks. Alec, hidden behind a silk screen with Estra, called out geometric calculations about sight lines and glass shatter angles. Florence departed to fetch Charlotte for final approval, leaving Ryan in the chapel with Mina, the Oracle, and the hidden siblings as the rain continued outside.
