# 032 - The Achilles Severance

On Thursday, June 15, 1843, between 7:05 PM and 7:27 PM in Claymore Manor's Main Parlor, Ryan confronts Edmund Girard, the household carriage driver exposed as a spy, who is physically restrained by Howard against a window frame. Charlotte presents legal documents proving Edmund dispatched pigeons to Eleanor Adame reporting Ryan's head injury during transit from the Amber Isles, facilitating a premature competency petition. Cora reveals childhood memories of Edmund visiting her family home bearing letters regarding 'Loren' from the cold north, causing Charlotte's rigid composure to shatter as she realizes her exiled mother orchestrated the conspiracy from Valheim. Mina corroborates by referencing their late father's letter mentioning Loren's assistance with escape plans. 

Edmund, having soiled himself in terror, confesses that Loren Claymore kidnapped Ryan fifteen years ago as the missing Duranian prince, planting him with Eleanor to be raised subservient for a strategic marriage to Charlotte that would create a personal union placing Loren's bloodline on the throne. He reveals Loren intends to spark war between Flora and Duran to weaken both nations, then invade with mercenary forces, and has planted false evidence framing Ryan as a traitor to both nations, rendering him stateless. When Ryan exposes Edmund's lie about having children—a previous claim contradicted by their earlier carriage conversation—Edmund breaks completely. 

After Charlotte grants permission for non-lethal punishment and Ryan sends the Adame sisters from the room to spare them trauma, Edmund offers desperate apologies to Charlotte and Florence for the treachery that resulted in Florence's splinted ankle and Cora being dropped. In a swift motion, Ryan severs Edmund's right Achilles tendon with his dagger, the distinctive POP of the snapping tendon echoing through the parlor as arterial blood sprays across the marble floor. Despite his agony, Edmund reveals Margot, a kitchen spy from Valheim with a branded wrist, operates a pigeon coop behind the stillroom and prepares to send messages that evening. Ryan offers accommodations for him and petitions to continue working, stating this punishment wasn't meant to ruin his life but instead serve as a reminder. The scene concludes with the household preparing to storm the kitchen to apprehend Margot before she can alert Loren.
