# 127 - Dragon and Fist Bump
On Wednesday, June 21, 1843, at approximately 5:14 AM, Ryan Adame threw on the green waistcoat, grabbed his boots, and took Estra Hamilton-Claymore's hand in the West Wing Bedroom corridor of the Palace of Loth Logan. He called Estra 'slowpoke' teasingly as they left. Estra retorted she was not slow but waiting, which implied patience not inefficiency. She noted Alec had been awake for twenty minutes, had likely finished the dragon drawing, and might have started another one. Florence called after them with a forty-five-minute warning demanding a full report if Ryan carried Estra again. From inside the bedroom, Charlotte reminded Ryan about a loose flagstone three doors past the linen closet in the east wing corridor, warned not to let Alec derail the timeline with dragon anatomy questions, then quietly admitted the green waistcoat was the correct choice, prompting Florence's delighted laughter as the bedroom door clicked shut. Walking down the corridor, Estra fell into step beside Ryan, her karambit at her hip, but her free hand swinging at her side. She observed Alec draws when he's anxious because the lines make sense when everything else doesn't. Ryan joked about giving Estra 37 years to get comfortable before pushing her at 38. Estra responded with dry humor calling it 'poor resource management' since 37 years is longer than she's been alive, but acknowledged she would waste a few minutes letting him see Alec's dragon, reporting Alec added more scales to fifty-three and has wing membrane theories that aren't aerodynamically sound but creative. She quietly urged Ryan to let Alec explain the whole thing even if it makes them late. Her hand stayed in his, grip loosening deliberately. Ryan squeezed her hand tighter, promising to listen to every word Alec says. Estra's breath caught briefly; she deflected with dry humor about circulation loss and documentation, warning not to laugh at Alec's hypotheses. She thanked Ryan for taking Alec seriously. The door to Alec's room was ahead, candlelight spilling beneath it. At approximately 5:19 AM, Ryan entered Alec's room with Estra, having kissed the back of her hand before entering. He announced he heard about a dragon and delivered Charlotte's line that dragons without wings are just glorified lizards. Alec Hamilton, a seven-year-old boy cross-legged on the bed surrounded by papers with charcoal dust on him, lit up and declared that was exactly what he'd been trying to tell Estra. He showed his dragon drawing with fifty-three individually rendered scales, two sets of wings labeled 'REDUNDANCY PAIR,' and a breath weapon still undecided. Estra settled into the chair beside his bed, watching Ryan interact with Alec, her small smile present. Alec smiled for the first time since Ryan entered—tentative but real. At approximately 5:20 AM, Ryan held out his fist for a fist bump and called Alec 'brother-in-law.' Alec stared in confusion and looked to Estra for guidance. Estra dryly explained it's a greeting and that Ryan did it to Howard once. Alec set aside his drawing and made an imperfect fist with thumb tucked wrong, extending it toward Ryan with trembling determination. He asked if dragonflies have tail spikes since they have two sets of wings and 'dragon' in their name, suggesting he could add spikes if accurate. At approximately 5:21 AM, Ryan lightly took Alec's fist, corrected his thumb out and wrist straight, then tapped knuckles. He demonstrated the fist bump with an explosion sound at the end—pulling his hand back fast and opening it with a quiet explosion noise. Alec repeated the motion with a whispered explosion, already holding his fist out for another try. Ryan seriously declared the dragon could demand tail spikes if it's cool. Alec nodded, pulled his drawing back, and declared with gravity that 'the dragon demands tail spikes,' planning three or four after consulting the dragon. Estra's smile finally reached her eyes. She warned Ryan that Alec will now fist bump everyone including the Queen, noted thirty-eight minutes until the Marten meeting, and suggested Ryan teach Alec what to do if someone doesn't know the handshake, observing that Alec will wait patiently with his fist out indefinitely.
