# 050 - River Walk Codes

Friday, June 16, 1843, 8:25 PM. At the River Walk Embarcadère in Tor Veiloth, seated on a damp stone bench beneath flickering gas lamps, Ryan Adame reveals the existence of a cryptographic code system designed by his sister Cora at age thirteen. He explains the conceptual framework: vague phrases where context provides specificity. Examples include "bread is fresh" meaning "family is first," "the water is clear" meaning "the truth is clear," and "how clear is the water?" functioning as "are you okay." Estra Hamilton, her professional mask cracked, expresses profound admiration that such a system endured fifteen years against Eleanor Adame's scrutiny. When Ryan jokes that she should stab him now while he is defenseless, Estra reacts with fierce anger and vulnerability, forbidding such jests after her attempt on Florence Claymore's life—which Ryan intercepted, taking the blade himself. She confesses she expected abandonment, yet Ryan orchestrated her infiltration from the start, planning the library rendezvous at 10 PM that she accelerated by appearing that morning. 

Ryan recounts his earlier confrontation with Loren Claymore, describing an ice manifestation in the wine cellar bearing floating text reading "GRATITUDE IS CHAINS." He dismissed Loren as an urban legend and myth to her face, rejecting her philosophy of manufactured destiny and conditioned obedience. Estra warns that Loren has operatives embedded in the Sanctum Matrice and will know of the midnight wedding plans, but Ryan maintains that Loren needs him alive and is growing desperate as her plan unravels.

By 8:50 PM, they enter a River Walk stew shop for eel pie and ale, where Ryan reveals Henry Whitmore watches from a window table. Estra fully abandons her Andrea's Cross disguise, admitting Ryan outmaneuvered her. As they discuss strategy, Estra detects three sets of heavy footsteps approaching with military gait and synchronized pacing—professional soldiers, not city watch. They escape through the kitchen, where Ryan pauses at the alley exit to surveil the pursuers before physically throwing Estra into the waiting carriage and commanding Henry to drive. He refuses to leave her behind, noting the agents likely targeted her as a defector rather than him.

During the carriage ride back to Claymore Manor, Estra confirms that if Loren knows she has turned, both she and her brother Alec—held in a Valheim dungeon—are dead within eleven days. Ryan reveals his reopened back wound is bleeding through his bandages, and they arrive to find Charlotte and Florence Claymore in nightclothes by the entrance, having received Henry's warning. Charlotte threatens to imprison Estra in the cellar; Ryan counters that he would share the cell and insists they relocate to the Blue Drawing Room for security, rejecting the exposed Solarium.

In the Blue Drawing Room, Henry retrieves Mina and Cora Adame. Mina and Cora utilize the family code: Mina stating "the bread is fresh but the kitchen is crowded" and Cora noting "the water is clear in the cellar but the cistern needs checking," confirming family unity while acknowledging infrastructure threats. Henry testifies that the three agents displayed paramilitary bearing and were positioning to intercept Estra specifically, not Ryan—evidence she is now a "loose end" to be terminated. Charlotte reluctantly agrees to trust but verify, while Rachael Blakeney mandates that Estra will teach karambit technique to Ryan, his wives, and his sisters in the courtyard immediately, supervised by Howard. If Estra betrays them, Rachael vows to have her killed instantly. Estra accepts, acknowledging that Ryan changed the math of her loyalty by trusting her first. The scene concludes with the family preparing for an accelerated timeline: twenty days until Loren's extraction deadline, eleven days until Alec's scheduled mutilation, and a wedding that must occur immediately to secure Church protection via Althena's Oracle.
