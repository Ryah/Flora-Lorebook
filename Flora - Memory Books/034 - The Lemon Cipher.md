# 034 - The Lemon Cipher

Thursday, June 15, 1843, 7:42 PM - 8:35 PM. In Claymore Manor's Blue Drawing Room, with doors locked and blinds drawn, Ryan Adame observes while Charlotte Claymore and Cora Adame collaborate to decode a message from a copper tube extracted from a carrier pigeon. Cora, wielding cryptographic authority, insists on fresh-squeezed lemon juice poured onto a crystal coaster rather than porcelain, explaining that glass transparency allows backlight to reveal iron-gall ink as the acid penetrates paper fibers. The message, compressed and hidden via complex geometric substitution using prime number sequencing, slowly materializes in the lamplight—shifting mid-text from Floran to Duranian script, indicating Loren communicates with someone raised across both cultures.

The revealed text devastates: Loren confesses to engineering Ryan's entire psychological profile through fifteen years of conditioning—"gratitude-based loyalty," submissive tendencies toward female authority, and fear of abandonment—designed to make him cling to protectors. The marriage to Charlotte was calculated to create a "bloodline bridge" merging Claymore military resources with Duranian legitimacy for Loren's war scheme. Cora was designated bait to be sold to Durant merchants to trigger Ryan's protective response, ensuring his capture (not killing) for extraction via the ship "Sea Witch."

Charlotte confronts Ryan with trembling vulnerability, demanding to know if his love is genuine or manufactured programming, noting his scarred back may have been orchestrated to bind him through blood-debt. Ryan kneels on the marble floor, places her gloved hand over his beating heart, and submits a framework for collective defense: he serves as her blade, Cora as intelligence confidant, Florence as her rock, Howard as general, and Rachael as emotional backbone. All while reaffirming that they are family, and encourages Charlotte to let family help them. Charlotte, accepting both his devotion and command, pulls him upright with interlaced fingers.

Together they decode a secondary cipher layer embedded in Charlotte's childhood memories—willow tree spirals representing cryptographic keys, the wine cellar's third rack, and the north tower's Duran-facing silhouette—which Loren carved into the code using her daughter's trauma. The final revelation establishes Loren's extraction timeline: twenty-one days hence, the last day of June, when Parliament recesses distracts the capital. As Cora departs to fetch Florence, Charlotte and Ryan remain, resolved to construct legal, political, and physical fortifications against the approaching conspiracy.
