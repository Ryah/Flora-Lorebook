# 116 - Two Hours of Softness
On Wednesday, June 21, 1843, from approximately 5:00 PM to 5:30 PM in the West Wing Suite of the Palace of Loth Logan, Ryan Adame led his three wives into their private chambers for mandated decompression. Florence immediately drew the curtains and lit the hearth before self-flicking her forehead for catching herself thinking about security. Charlotte stood frozen in the center of the room, confessing she didn't know how to stop strategizing or what 'soft' looked like. Estra hovered near the doorway with her hand on her karambit, volunteering to take first watch before catching herself and admitting she had no protocol for stillness. Florence settled on the hearthrug and invited everyone to join her, suggesting they simply exist for two hours. Ryan lay spread-eagle on the floor and announced decompression was mandatory, offering sex as an equally valid alternative. Charlotte struggled to lie down, admitting she didn't know what to do with her hands. Ryan flicked both Charlotte and Estra's foreheads for security thoughts, calling them his 'ditzy wives.' Florence laughed and joined him on the floor. Charlotte surprised herself by inviting Estra to join them for body heat. Ryan mentioned he hadn't relieved himself in three days due to the sensory link, and when Charlotte realized the bond would transmit intimate sensation, Florence gleefully proposed finding out together 'for science.' Ryan initiated sensory link testing through self-stimulation, and Charlotte received tactile input through the bond—rhythmic pressure and building warmth without visual context—her spine arching off the floor and her analytical mask fracturing as she requested 'the full data set for documentation purposes.' Ryan stopped mid-test, telling Charlotte she'd have to complete the data collection herself. Charlotte's body continued responding to the interrupted input while her mind tried to rationalize continuing. Ryan then leaned in close to Charlotte, telling her she needed to unwind and granting her permission to take control, using the safeword 'Black' and suggesting she also unwind Estra. Charlotte accepted, her focus sharpening into methodical hunger. She instructed Florence to remove Ryan's shirt while she began guiding Estra through unfamiliar intimate territory. Estra expressed uncertainty about her role and lack of coordinates for intimacy. Florence reassured Estra that the only coordinates were family and permission to want things. Charlotte declared they would finish the experiment 'thoroughly' with 'all of us' for documentation purposes, her voice steady and commanding as she took control and began to teach Estra where to put her hands.
