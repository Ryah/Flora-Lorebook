# 081 - The Bridal Carry

On Monday, June 19, 1843, from approximately 6:15 PM to 6:45 PM, the aftermath of the blood debt rematch unfolds in the Eastern Courtyard of Claymore Manor, transitioning to the Dining Hall. Ryan Adame, bearing a split lip and bloody nose, accepts Estra Hamilton into the family, joking about her loss of control while acknowledging his own feral state during the combat. He extends a side hug to Mina, who reciprocates stiffly, patting his back with awkward concern while admitting he terrified her and demanding warning before he channels such violence again. Cora Adame, analytical despite the emotional atmosphere, requests measurements and baseline vitals for post-traumatic data, noting the need to document cortisol clearance and motor function, though she concedes he should bathe and cease bleeding on the flagstones first.

Florence Claymore, her ankle splint visible beneath her riding habit and face streaked with tears, erupts into hysterical laughter, declaring Ryan's tendency to bleed on surfaces his signature move. She enthusiastically plans Estra's wedding attire: a blue silk dress with ivory lace trim, insisting the wedding party will be the most ridiculous in Flora. Charlotte Claymore, controlled in her burgundy evening gown but revealing fear beneath her mask, interrupts to stress the legal necessities and Oracle consultation required for the marriage, yet prioritizes immediate medical attention, bathing, and food, refusing to discuss contracts while anyone remains hypovolemic. Estra, trembling and exhausted in her torn charcoal fighting trousers and bandaged knuckles, denies being hypovolemic but admits to exhaustion and possible concussion, her usual assassin demeanor crumbling into vulnerability as she presses against Ryan.

Alec Hamilton, bouncing with joy, asks if they truly get to stay forever, prompting Mrs. Marie to command the group to the table with kitchen authority, threatening ladles, rolling pins, and a meat tenderizer that has seen things to hurry the wounded. Howard Blakeney Ragnar, reverent and hoarse, declares they have earned their place in the clan by the Ragnar way: blood first, then meat, then mead, then sleep, offering heavy paternal approval.

At approximately 6:25 PM, Ryan stands and lifts Estra bridal-style, carrying her toward the dining hall despite her dramatic protests about being an assassin with eleven kills who does not get carried like luggage. She squirms weakly, her scraped knee and bare feet dangling, but her arms find their way around his neck, her face flushing as she hides her gratitude. During the procession, Mina dryly supplies the word "bride" for Estra's predicament, while Cora statistically calculates the low probability of Ryan dropping her. Mrs. Marie leads with threats of kitchen implements, and Florence limps alongside, wiping tears of delighted laughter. Howard confirms carrying the wounded is honorable in Valheimr culture. Alec teases his sister about liking the carry, observing her red face and lack of kicking, prompting Estra to threaten trading him for beans and a mediocre chicken. Charlotte advises Estra to admit defeat gracefully, to which Estra whispers to Ryan that she has merely postponed her victory until after dinner, thanking him for not making her walk or be strong just yet.

Upon entering the dining hall around 6:35 PM, Rachael Blakeney, elegant in an emerald silk gown, presides over the table from the head seat, welcoming her "future daughter" and commanding the exhausted group to sit before the food grows cold. Estra hesitates to sit at the head table, citing her unofficial status pending Oracle blessing, but Florence vehemently insists she earned the chair through combat excellence. Charlotte quietly affirms that the blood vigil has already made Estra family, acknowledging her fit within their structure. Rachael raises a glass toasts Ryan for fighting for his family and to Estra for choosing to stand with him, declaring the house a haven for those who fight to find one. Throughout the meal of beef stew and bread, Mina maintains a hovering, protective watch, Cora mutters about elevated oxytocin levels and protein requirements, and Howard tears into his meal with gusto, having formally recognized Ryan's warrior status. The scene concludes with the group united around the oak table, candlelight flickering as sunset bleeds through the windows, marking the integration of a new member into the household through blood and acceptance.
