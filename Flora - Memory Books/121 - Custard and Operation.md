# 121 - Custard and Operation
On Wednesday, June 21, 1843, from approximately 8:18 PM to 8:23 PM, the formal dinner in the Great Hall of the Palace of Loth Logan concluded with custard service. Ryan Adame blew a kiss to Charlotte Claymore across the table, which she dismissed as 'ridiculous' and 'not protocol,' though Florence gleefully noted she was blushing. Ryan declared the custard heavenly. Queen Maya revealed the berry compote came from bushes planted by Ryan's father, the late king, with William Adame adding the bushes were stubborn like their owner. Charlie and Florence continued a betting pool on how many annotations Charlotte would write about the custard, with Charlotte insisting she would not annotate it. Ryan waved over servant Petra Voss to refill his wine, deliberately moving up the timeline for their planned operation. He casually invited Petra to meet him in his room in ten minutes, claiming he needed help with a project and that Estra would also be present. Petra accepted with professional composure, offering to bring wine. Queen Maya smoothly covered the interaction and directed Petra to the kitchen for late trays. Ryan spooned custard and gave final instructions: he, Florence, and Charlotte would go to another room to project, while Estra would walk the projection to meet Petra. Queen Maya formally ended the dinner. Florence pulled Ryan toward the west door under the pretext of a walk. Charlotte rose with calculated calm, scanning the room. Estra checked her karambit and fell into step behind them. Howard and Mina departed to 'check window seals' in the east corridor, ready as backup. Cora slipped away to document Petra's exit gait. Rachael kept William and Alec occupied at the table. Alec continued stacking spoons. Claire cleared dishes with trembling hands, still watching Florence. Henry opened the west door for the group. They moved into the private corridor, then entered the east corridor study—a small, disused room smelling of old parchment and dust, with a locking door and a single interior-facing window. Florence checked the lock and positioned herself to guard. Charlotte moved the chair to the center of the room and insisted on physical contact to anchor Ryan's projection, referencing the feedback dissonance from the last attempt. Estra reviewed her route to intercept Petra: kitchen corridor, past the linen closet, through the servant's passage—seven minutes. She planned to keep Petra talking about mundane topics to search for tells. Florence joked about the plan; Charlotte called her logic 'not entirely unsound.' The group prepared, with Estra poised to leave and intercept Petra. Six minutes remained until Petra's arrival.
