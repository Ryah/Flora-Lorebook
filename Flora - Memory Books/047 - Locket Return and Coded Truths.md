# 047 - Locket Return and Coded Truths

On Friday, June 16, 1843, between 12:40 PM and 1:50 PM at Claymore Manor, Ryan Adame pulls back with his sister Cora in the corridor to return a tarnished brass locket containing a family photograph from his youth, revealing he intentionally preserved it through extraction and trauma despite zero-point-three percent probability of retention. Cora accepts it with trembling fingers, admitting through statistical analysis that she "likes" Ryan's wives—Florence and Charlotte—but vows to calculate their destruction if they harm him. Mina joins them and recounts Eleanor's erasure of Ryan after the wedding: dismantling his room within four hours, sealing the chain behind plaster, and attempting to sell the house in Tor Veiloth. The sisters confess they preserved Ryan's indexed journals against maternal denial and trained in violence and mathematics to survive.

At 1:02 PM in the West Solarium, Rachael Blakeney embraces Mina and Cora with maternal ferocity, claiming them as daughters and vowing to give them everything Eleanor denied. The group plans Ryan's wedding to Charlotte and Florence for June 17, intending to demonstrate Ryan's free will against Loren Claymore's extraction timeline. When Ryan raises concerns about public backlash against an equal triad marriage in Floria, he produces the signed Article 4 contract proving his autonomous choice. References to "Durian Influence" and Estra alert Mina and Cora to Ryan's hidden Duranian heritage.

Ryan dispatches his sisters to "fetch drinks," employing their childhood doublespeak code: "water" signals "act normal," "figuring out logistics" warns of listening ears, and "bread" confirms truth. In the kitchen (1:15-1:35 PM), Mina and Cora establish coded phrases through doublespeak while Mrs. Marie provides refreshments: "fire is low" means safe to speak; "window is closed" means secrets remain hidden; "bread is fresh" confirms Ryan is Duranian royalty; "kitchen is busy" indicates multiple threats; "wine is old" references the fifteen-year-old secret of his identity; and "door is open" grants permission to speak freely. They agree to monitor the situation without immediate action, acknowledging the twenty-day extraction threat from the north (Valheim/Loren).

Returning to the Solarium at 1:40 PM, the sisters confirm understanding through code while maintaining cover before Rachael, Charlotte, and Florence. The group finalizes plans for a sunset wedding with an Oracle's blessing from the Sanctum Matrice to provide Church legitimacy against Loren's challenges, intending to hold a proper Dowry ceremony where all parties kneel as equals in the east garden rose trellis. Mina and Cora pledge to witness the ceremony legally and guard Ryan against both word-blades and steel, accepting the wives as expanding family while maintaining protective surveillance.
