# 068 - Winner Takes All

On Sunday, June 18, 1843, beginning around 2:10 PM in the Claymore Manor dining room, Ryan Adame formally challenges Estra Hamilton to a rematch scheduled for June 19th at 6:00 PM in the Eastern courtyard. Referencing their previous spontaneous bout and his now-healed back, Ryan proposes an unarmed match fought until one yields. Estra accepts with fierce determination, declaring that without the distraction of her brother Alec being held hostage, she will prove her skill is no fluke and referencing her experience fighting in Duranian fish markets.

The household quickly becomes invested in the event. Florence Claymore, nursing a wrapped ankle, requests front row seating and expresses desire for training from whichever fighter wins. Charlotte Claymore assumes administrative control, directing Henry to prepare the courtyard boundaries and medical supplies while establishing household rules prohibiting permanent injury and requiring chapel attendance regardless of outcome. Howard Blakeney Ragnar volunteers to judge under "Ragnar rules" (no biting or eye-gouging), recommending the tradition of the loser buying the winner a drink. Rachael Blakeney offers to prepare refreshments and medical supplies while suggesting protein-heavy celebration dinners. Alec Hamilton enthusiastically attempts to wager his two gold coins as the fight's banker, though Estra refuses and Charlotte designates Henry to hold official wagers.

The discussion shifts to stakes beyond drinks. Charlotte proposes the loser perform the winner's least favorite household task for one week—specifically stables duty for Ryan and mending for Estra. Florence suggests the winner train the loser for a month, which Estra counters with one week of morning sessions plus the right to train Florence if Estra wins. Howard proposes a blood debt—one task within reason pledged without complaint. Rachael adds that the winner should select the celebration dinner menu. Ryan escalates the stakes to "winner takes all," combining the household labor, training schedule, dinner choice, social function attendance, and the blood debt into a comprehensive wager. Estra accepts with a rough handshake, her bandaged hand rough against his palm, while Charlotte establishes parameters ensuring the blood debt cannot compromise security, health, or Ryan's dignity as Count of Cassia. Estra specifies that if she wins, Ryan must commit to full-contact training without holding back due to her gender or their "blood sibling" relationship.

Immediately after formalizing the bout, Ryan summons Mina Adame from the east wing library where she was reviewing monastery maps with Cora. Begun around 3:15 PM in the Eastern courtyard, Mina subjects Ryan to an intensive thirty-minute training session focused on "fighting in the darkness"—using auditory cues and physical sensation rather than sight. She emphasizes listening to footfalls and weight shifts, testing Ryan's ability to counter her strikes through instinct rather than visual reaction. During this training, Charlotte observes anxiously from the dining room window planning recovery measures including bath oils and liniments, while Florence studies Ryan's footwork patterns for future application. Estra watches from a kitchen archway, silently planning to employ "dirty tricks" including pressure points and targeting vulnerable areas that Mina's honorable training doesn't address.

Concluding as afternoon transitions to evening around 3:45 PM, Mina advises Ryan that tomorrow's fight will differ fundamentally from his dark-room training—he will fight in full daylight with spectators judging, requiring him to avoid becoming lost in spectacle while maintaining awareness of distance, breathing, and ground position. She orders him to rest and recover before dismissing him to prepare for the next day's match.
