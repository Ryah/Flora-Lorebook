# 054 - Midnight War Council

Saturday, June 17, 1843, shortly after 12:48 AM: Following the consummation of their triadic marriage and the sealing of the Hamilton blood vigil, Ryan Adame walks with Countess Charlotte Claymore, Countess Florence Claymore, and Estra Hamilton through the moonlit Kitchen Corridor of Claymore Manor toward a midnight snack. The group, dressed in disheveled night clothes and bearing minor wounds (Ryan's healing palm laceration and Charlotte's back stitches, Florence's splinted ankle), encounters Mrs. Marie blocking the path to the kitchen. The housekeeper carries a silver tray bearing roast beef, cheese, apples, and a letter bearing an unrecognized crimson seal.

Mrs. Marie presents the letter, explaining it arrived by night-rider bearing black sealing wax. Estra immediately identifies it as Hamilton correspondence, snatching the paper with trembling hands. The smuggled intelligence reveals Alec Hamilton is held at an abandoned monastery on the Valheim border, twelve leagues north of Frostwatch. The letter details security vulnerabilities: four guards rotating six-hour shifts with a seventeen-minute window between rotations, a crumbling northwest wall overgrown with ivy offering climbing handholds every three feet, and a critical window on June 18 when Loren visits at dusk to commune alone with a "shattered mirror" in the chapel crypt, leaving her vulnerable from dusk until midnight.

Charlotte immediately accelerates the timeline from Loren's eleven-day deadline to a dawn departure in four hours, reasoning they must strike before Loren discovers their intelligence advantage. Ryan confirms his physical readiness, explaining his back wound has healed at forty percent the normal rate per Mercer's assessment and Fiona's earlier treatment, leaving only residual scar tissue. He reminds Estra of his promise to extract Alec within forty-eight hours, asserting the house works fast.

The group relocates to the kitchen, where Mrs. Marie provides a war council meal of cold beef, dark bread, apples, and strong coffee. Ryan challenges Florence's readiness for the ride, demanding verification of her splinted ankle capability. Florence demonstrates her fitness by standing and weight-bearing despite the injury, insisting she will prove her capability on horseback and refusing to be left behind. Ryan approves her determination, acknowledging her proven value to the team despite previous attempts to hold her back.

Estra provides detailed tactical intelligence from three prior surveillances: the monastery sits on a bluff above the Frostwater River with an exposed southern approach, the chapel houses Loren's communion site in the crypt, and the forge on the eastern side likely contains Alec, though the expanded cellar extending under the entire structure poses the greatest risk for his actual location. Ryan utilizes his knowledge of Floran monastery architecture (specifically the Sanctum Matrice in Tor Veiloth) to sketch an estimated interior layout, identifying three entrances, two staircases, and probable cellar access points.

Charlotte orders Howard and Eva awakened to provide guard rotation maps and orchestrate a distraction at the main gate using fire arrows and noise to extend the seventeen-minute infiltration window. Mrs. Marie prepares provisions including hardtack, tinned coffee, and water skins filled from the spring rather than the well (noting Valheim water runs bitter), while the stable master readies two sure-footed geldings accustomed to foul weather.

Florence departs to change into riding leathers after testing her ankle, acknowledging she will need tight binding for stirrup work. Charlotte concedes Florence will slow the ride but values her competitive drive for wall-scaling speed. The party agrees to two hours of sleep before the dawn departure. The scene ends with the mist pressing against kitchen windows as horses whinny in the stable yard, sensing the coming weather and conflict.
