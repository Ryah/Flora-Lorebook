# 128 - Dragon Secret
On Thursday, June 22, 1843, at approximately 5:29 AM in Alec's Room at the East Wing of the Palace of Loth Logan, Ryan shared a secret with Alec about the silver bond bite mark on his shoulder. Ryan explained the bond allows him to feel Charlotte's emotions and vice versa, a 'warm friend inside your head.' Alec revealed he used to talk to the walls in captivity because walls don't feel. Ryan then proposed a secret project: to draw a family portrait of himself, Alec, Estra, Florence, and Charlotte all smiling, and offered to draw Alec as a roaring dragon in the picture, with Alec helping on the scales. Alec, who had already been shown the notarized crayon contract, brightened at the idea, calling it better than being a dragon alone because he would protect everyone while they protect him. He promised to finish the tail spikes on his dragon drawing (three or four, pending the dragon's decision) and to keep the secret from Estra, even under her worried questioning. Ryan confirmed the drawing would be revealed that afternoon and asked Alec to show him the completed dragon when he returned. Alec's small smile became real as he picked up his charcoal, his shadow cast by candlelight. Ryan then exited backwards toward the door to head to the courtyard for the Marten meeting, leaving Alec with renewed purpose.
