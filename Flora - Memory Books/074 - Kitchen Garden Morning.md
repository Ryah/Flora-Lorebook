# 074 - Kitchen Garden Morning

Monday, June 19, 1843, approximately 7:45 AM to 8:05 AM. Claymore Manor Kitchen, 64-66°F, clear weather. Ryan settles at the scarred worktable between Estra and Alec to peel apples for the Kitchen Garden Pie, while Florence is briefly present before descending to the cellar. Alec instructs them in proper technique—thumb atop the fruit, blade underneath, pulling toward oneself to avoid waste—while Mrs. Marie observes from her dough-kneading station. Estra struggles initially, her bandaged knuckles and scarred hands trembling as she treats the apple like a moving target, admitting she keeps expecting it to move. Mrs. Marie counsels her to respect the apple rather than conquer it. Alec shares his late mother's wisdom: long unbroken spirals of peel indicate patience, while short pieces reveal a rushed heart, adding that assassins make terrible bakers because they desire swift resolution over slow care. After several attempts, Estra successfully removes a long spiral of red-gold skin without breaking it, experiencing a moment of genuine wonder at the domestic achievement. Ryan praises her progress and rubs his shoulder against hers; Estra allows the contact and presses back slightly, an unusual display of physical comfort for the normally guarded bodyguard. Florence returns from the cellar with a wrapped block of butter, revealing that the household has begun wagering on the evening's rematch—Howard maintains a chalkboard in the courtyard taking even odds, with Florence betting ten gold on Estra to see Ryan scrub stables while Charlotte wagered twenty gold on Ryan to see Estra assigned kitchen duty. Alec declares his intention to bet his two gold coins (a gift from Rachael) on his sister, extracting a promise that she will not harm Ryan too badly, declaring Ryan "family now" who should not be hurt. Estra agrees, acknowledging that Ryan taught her true strength means knowing when to stop before unnecessary harm. Mrs. Marie interrupts the philosophical moment, rapping her wooden spoon to redirect the group toward preparing the pie crust before the morning wastes away.
