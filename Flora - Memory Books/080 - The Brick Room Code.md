# 080 - The Brick Room Code

On Monday, June 19, 1843, from approximately 4:47 PM to 6:00 PM in the Eastern Courtyard of Claymore Manor, Ryan Adame prepares for his scheduled rematch with Estra Hamilton using an elaborate coded language shared with his sister Mina. Ryan informs Mina that "the bread is in the oven" and instructs her not to "let it burn," signaling his intention to mentally regress to his traumatic "brick room" persona and fully unleash his combat capabilities, with Mina serving as failsafe to stop him if Estra proves unable to handle his intensity. While Estra observes the exchange without fully comprehending the code, recognizing only that something significant passes between them, Ryan continues speaking in metaphor about cold water providing necessary clarity, melted ice working to his advantage, dark rooms without light, and a lantern out of oil. Mina translates these cryptic statements for Estra, explaining that Ryan will not fully regress but needs to silence his inner critical voice, trusts Estra completely to "catch him when he falls," sees her clearly as his opponent rather than an enemy, and is drowning under psychological weight that requires the fight as a release mechanism. She relays his explicit declaration of love, framed within the code as the underlying truth behind his request. Estra accepts this vulnerability, acknowledging that Ryan is showing her his unmasked self and promising to be the "wall" he needs and to catch him when exhaustion takes him. Ryan confirms this connection by touching her hand and speaking final code about "clear water" and "attached chains," affirming he stands before her without pretense before retreating indoors at 5:45 PM to complete his mental preparation. As 6:00 PM approaches, a sizable crowd including household staff, visiting Ragnar clansmen, Claire Whitehill, and Mrs. Marie gathers around the chalk circle, having created the largest betting pool in Claymore history exceeding three hundred gold, while Howard Blakeney Ragnar prepares to officiate and Mina goes to retrieve Ryan from his internal state.
