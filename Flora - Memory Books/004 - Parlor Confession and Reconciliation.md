# 004 - Parlor Confession and Reconciliation

Wednesday, June 14, 1843 AD, 4:05 PM - 4:20 PM. At Claymore Manor's parlor, Rachael Claymore exits to speak with Cook about dinner arrangements, leaving her daughter Florence alone with Ryan. Florence, wearing a pale yellow day dress with golden hair loose past her shoulders, enters expressing tearful relief that Ryan's memories have returned—she had feared the carriage accident caused permanent damage and that he would never remember them. Ryan asks her to sit beside him on the settee and takes her trembling hand, confronting her about the guilt she carries regarding their arranged marriage. He reveals he learned from Claire that Florence read to him daily and wept over his unconscious form during his recovery from the assassination attempt. Florence confesses she knew "buying a husband like a piece of furniture" was wrong but let Charlotte convince her it was practical and necessary, then reveals the depth of her guilt: Ryan had thrown himself between her and a blade when he barely knew them, yet she feared he would hate her upon waking. Ryan confesses his own secret—that after the assassination attempt, he hid bleeding in a closet for three days without calling for help because he believed he had failed them and feared being sent back to his abusive mother Eleanor, choosing to die rather than face that humiliation. Florence reacts with horror, her face going pale before she embraces him fiercely, promising to have Charlotte write him into every legal protection—wills, estates, and trusts—so he never doubts he belongs there. She presses her palm over his heart and kisses his forehead, extracting a promise that he will call for help immediately if ever injured again, even if it wakes the entire household. She reveals that Charlotte also sat by his bed bargaining with Althena and fate for his survival, though Charlotte processes emotions differently and will want her own conversation. Florence then leads Ryan through oak-paneled corridors lined with disapproving portraits of Claymore matriarchs to the Blue Drawing Room, where writing materials await. She shares that at the wedding, Ryan's eldest sister Mina boldly asked Florence and Charlotte if they would treat him well, risking their mother Eleanor's wrath, while his younger sister Cora wept throughout the ceremony and refused to sign as witness. She mentions Rachael has begun inquiries about extracting Mina and Cora from the Amber Isles. At the drawing room entrance, Ryan thanks Florence genuinely, acknowledging she married him to be with her lover Charlotte yet calling her a good wife regardless. Florence laughs tearfully, calling it the most honest thing anyone has said about their marriage, then promises to send Claire to fetch him ten minutes before dinner. She departs leaving Ryan to write letters to his sisters, having pledged to spend every day earning the love he offers.
