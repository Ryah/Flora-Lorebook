# 058 - Post-Rescue Vigil

Saturday, June 17, 1843, approximately 6:15 AM in Claymore Manor's Second Floor Corridor. Following the successful extraction of Alec Hamilton from Valheim Monastery, Ryan Adame carries an exhausted Charlotte Claymore—her black hair tangled across his shoulder, burgundy riding habit stained with salt and grime—while Florence Claymore hobbles behind with a linen splint wrapped around her left ankle, her white blouse translucent with dampness. They encounter Estra Hamilton clutching her younger brother Alec with white-knuckled intensity, her dark mantle dripping onto the floorboards. Estra tests the window latch and threatens to gut anyone who touches Alec without her permission, her amber-red eyes tracking Ryan for traps, but her aggressive posture gradually softens into exhaustion as Ryan offers resources: physician Mercer (who previously healed Ryan's spinal injury) and Fiona's healing salve for any cuts. Howard Blakeney Ragnar confirms Eva has locked down the grounds with three checkpoints before departing to verify the perimeter.

Ryan enters his quarters and settles Charlotte onto the bed, her long limbs sprawling diagonally across the mattress with one boot half-removed. He sits beside her, stroking her hair while holding Florence's hand as she collapses beside him, her splinted ankle propped on a folded blanket. He remains motionless until both women drift into genuine exhausted sleep—Charlotte's breathing deepening to a slow rhythm, Florence's competitive tension draining from her jaw—leaving the door open at Charlotte's request so they can hear if Estra calls. Rachael Blakeney arrives with a silver tray bearing honey cakes, strong tea, and fresh bandages, settling into a chair with her needlework to watch over the sleeping women while humming a Valheim lullaby.

At approximately 6:25 AM, Ryan moves to the East Wing Guest Chamber to check on his sisters Mina and Cora Adame, who have been awake all night waiting. Mina fills the doorway with frizzy dark hair escaping its braid, her rigid posture breaking into trembling relief as she grips Ryan's ice-cold wrist; she admits they were preparing to storm the monastery themselves by 7 AM if he hadn't returned. Cora stands behind her with charcoal-stained fingers and a discarded notebook, immediately analyzing Ryan for injuries and noting his limp. Ryan confirms Alec's rescue but admits to complications: Loren Claymore was present in the monastery sub-cellar, and he lost his dagger embedded in her arm during the confrontation. He reassures them he slept during the return ride and used Cora's encryption code successfully against Loren.

Despite his assurances, the sisters insist on inspecting his spinal scar. Mina lifts his shirt with trembling fingers to verify the raised ridge of tissue shows no fresh bleeding or tearing, only tightness from the cold ride and stress. Cora provides clinical assessment that the scar tissue contracts in cold weather and requires daily stretching to maintain range of motion. Throughout this reunion, Estra maintains vigilance from the guest room across the hall, visible through the cracked door with her knife glinting under the pillow, one eye cracked open to watch the corridor while Alec sleeps curled against her chest.
