# 123 - Dream Sanctuary
On Wednesday, June 21, 1843, at approximately 9:55 PM, Ryan Adame and Charlotte Claymore met in a shared lucid dream, the null state that Ryan enters when pulled back from projection. The dream began as an endless twilight water beneath a star-filled sky. Ryan revealed this is his serenity, but in waking it is black nothingness; he learned lucid dreaming through years of sensory deprivation in the brick room. 

Charlotte asked if they could choose to return here. Ryan transformed the scene into a classical ballroom with marble floors, crystal chandeliers, and a frescoed ceiling of moving constellations, inviting her to dance. Charlotte confessed she never danced properly—only studied steps, and Florence gave up after four stepped-on feet. They attempted a Viennese waltz, both beginners stumbling repeatedly: wrong turns, bumped knees, stepped-on toes. Each mistake drew laughter rather than frustration. Over time they found the rhythm, their movements grew surer, and the ballroom expanded around them. Charlotte’s Countess mask dissolved completely, replaced by unguarded joy. The dreamscape responded with brighter candles and pulsing silver-gold cord. 

After the waltz, Ryan told her the shared dreamscape is a permanent feature of the sensory bond, a gift from Althena—a private space free from spies. He called her queen and the most beautiful woman he has ever seen. Charlotte confessed she never had a safe space; Loren always listened and twisted everything. She asked Ryan to teach her how to simply be, step by step. 

Ryan covered her eyes and asked her to imagine her childhood mental sanctuary. Charlotte described how at age seven she tore pages from a book called The Gardens of the Southern Counties and created three imaginary gardens—the third being a glass conservatory with citrus trees, bookshelves, a bronze sundial, and a window seat overlooking snow-dusted mountains. Loren burned the book page by page, but Charlotte hid the torn illustrations. As she spoke, the dreamscape transformed into that conservatory: crystal walls, honey-colored wooden floors, citrus trees in terracotta pots, and mountains visible beyond the glass. Charlotte was shocked to realize she had manifested this herself, proving she has agency in the dream. 

She led Ryan on a tour: the sundial that tells whatever time she needs, the orange tree imagined from an encyclopedia Rachael gave her, the worn green book on the shelf, and finally the window seat where she read adventure stories as a child. She revealed she never imagined another person in this room in twenty years—Ryan is the first. She confessed she lacks words to articulate what sharing this sanctuary means. The dreamscape held steady as dawn approached in the waking world. Below, the physical bodies remained asleep: Florence curled against Ryan's left side, Estra on his right oriented toward the door, while Mina was awake in the east kitchen courtyard counting footsteps and awaiting Greaves's midnight crossing.
