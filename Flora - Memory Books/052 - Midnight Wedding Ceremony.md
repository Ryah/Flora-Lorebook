# 052 - Midnight Wedding Ceremony

On Friday, June 16, 1843, beginning at 10:25 PM in Claymore Manor's Eastern Courtyard, Ryan Adame concludes accelerated karambit training with Estra Hamilton and Howard Blakeney Ragnar, demonstrating proficiency sufficient to impress his mentors. Howard agrees to procure Duranian steel blades—sharpened for Ryan and an unsharpened trainer for Florence Claymore—while Estra accepts temporary ally status and household hospitality despite her street-hardened instincts, declaring she does not run from debts. The group moves indoors as midnight approaches, with Florence frustrated by her physical limitations (bandaged hand and splinted ankle) but eager to prove herself in combat she has yet to master.

By 10:30 PM, walking the main corridor with Charlotte and Florence, Ryan teases Florence about future sparring matches with Mina or Cora, prompting Charlotte to insist that Florence will train properly once healed, acknowledging that documents and prayers cannot stop blades. At 10:35 PM, Ryan requests the romantic tradition of separate preparation, insisting on bathing apart from his wives so his first sight of them occurs at the altar. Despite Charlotte's anxiety about the 52-minute deadline until the Oracle's arrival, both wives agree to honor his desire for tradition and emotional impact.

In Ryan's private quarters from 10:40 PM to 10:58 PM, maids Claire Whitehill and Fiona assist his preparations. Fiona applies healing salve and fresh bandages to Ryan's reopened back wound, noting the near-healed scar tissue. Claire, wearing a black swimsuit uniform with emerald lace, engages in open flirtation, employing a warm towel in intimate, lingering contact under the pretense of drying him. With Fiona departing to check arrangements, Claire performs intimate acts on Ryan as a "secret" to be remembered during the ceremony, ensuring he is both prepared and trembling before assisting him into black velvet wedding attire with silver threading and adjusting his cravat.

At 11:00 PM, Ryan exits to find Rachael Blakeney, whom he embraces and addresses as "mama" for the first time, then receives protective well-wishes from sisters Mina and Cora. He learns the midnight ceremony will be the first equal-rights triadic marriage in Flora's history, conducted publicly in the East Garden beneath an ancient oak within a sacred circle of white stones, with approximately sixty citizens witnessing—an unusually open display for a society typically female-led. Mina and Cora provide security assurances while Estra watches from the north hedge.

The processional begins at 11:15 PM with Ryan entering first, facing east as instructed, the Oracle—a blindfolded young man in white and gold robes holding a carved silver staff with descending ring chains—presiding. As harp and flute music swells, Charlotte walks the aisle in black velvet with silver threading, followed by Florence in cream silk with gold embroidery (limping on her splinted ankle). At 11:28 PM, upon the Oracle's command to turn, Ryan faces his wives, tears forming as he whispers endearments and takes their hands.

The vows emphasize mutual choice and equality, rejecting Flora's traditional conditioning. Charlotte binds herself through choice rather than obligation or property, sliding a gold band onto Ryan's finger while acknowledging her fears. Florence follows with competitive urgency, demanding "no take-backs" and promising permanence. Ryan reciprocates, citing that his wives taught him family is worth living and fighting for, acknowledging Rachael as his only maternal support outside of his sisters. The Oracle invokes Althena to bless what is freely given, pressing his staff to each forehead before pronouncing the triad sealed.

The ceremony concludes with passionate kisses—Charlotte's fierce and desperate, Florence's open and possessive—observed by tearful family and a visibly moved Estra who brushes away unauthorized emotion. With tensions lowered but security maintained (Estra accepting Florence's insistence that allies join the feast), the party proceeds to the great hall where a three-tiered wedding cake with white icing and silver leaf awaits, marking the transition from warriors to spouses and the dawn of a legally protected, chosen union against looming threats.
