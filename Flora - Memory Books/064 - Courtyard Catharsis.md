# 064 - Courtyard Catharsis

Sunday, June 18, 1843, 8:35 AM to 9:30 AM. At Claymore Manor's Solar, the morning after extracting Alec Hamilton from Loren's custody, Ryan crouches before Alec to reassure him, swearing to watch over Estra and revealing he spent six hours sleeping motionless with her on his shoulder to finally grant her rest. Alec whispers in disbelief that Estra never sleeps near others, a fact she confirms with her clipped Duranian accent, acknowledging Ryan's stubbornness. The group proceeds to breakfast, Ryan holding Charlotte and Florence's hands while Rachael Blakeney threatens to spoon-feed Ryan herself with her wooden serving spoon if he doesn't eat. The table is laden with bacon, porridge, and fresh bread. Ryan briefly rests his head on Florence's bandaged shoulder, murmuring 'missed ya,' before eating. Afterward, Ryan sketches a deliberately crude stick-figure rendition of Loren's face on parchment, folds it, and pockets it.

He leads Estra to the courtyard where they previously trained karambits, producing a weathered training dummy once used by Howard and Eva. He nails the drawing to the dummy's face and commands Estra to imagine it is Loren and kill her. Estra initially hesitates, then releases traumatic revelations: Loren chained Alec, touched his face while he screamed, promised to make him love her before breaking him, and threatened to send Estra his fingers in a box if she disobeyed. At Ryan's command, Estra attacks the dummy with wild, uncontrolled slashes unlike her training, targeting eyes and throat while screaming 'Die!' The paper face tears, wood splinters, and sap bleeds from the oak. She sobs that it isn't enough, that Loren isn't bleeding or screaming.

Ryan bellows 'YOU'RE JUST NOT STRIKING HARD ENOUGH. KILL HER AGAIN!' in raw motivation. Estra launches a second frenzied assault, kicking the oaken post and driving her karambit deep into the wood with savage grunts, her gray linen tunic drenched in sweat. From the courtyard archway, Florence, Charlotte, and Howard arrive to observe silently; Florence grips Charlotte's arm fearing injury, while Charlotte analyzes with clinical detachment and Howard nods grim approval of the emotional purging. Exhausted and trembling, Estra begs Ryan to tell her Loren feels the pain, that she's burning, begging 'Tell me I'm killing her,' as she stands weeping amidst splinters and shattered oak.
