# 060 - Manor Recovery

Saturday, June 17, 1843, approximately 1:15 PM to 2:05 PM. Following the rescue of Alec Hamilton from Valheim monastery and the completion of the blood vigil, the group gathers in Claymore Manor's kitchen to recover from post-adrenaline fatigue. Ryan Adame teases Estra Hamilton about drooling on his shoulder during rest, jesting that his shoulder is wet with "holy water," causing her to flush crimson and sputter with indignant denial while suppressing a smile. Ryan suggests arranging a second bed for Alec in Estra's room, though Rachael Blakeney notes the traumatized boy will likely need to remain close to his sister for some time. Mrs. Marie commands the group to eat with maternal ferocity, slamming platters of glistening beef and bread onto the oak table and insisting the emaciated Estra eat first, wiping the "murder" off her face. Mina Adame admits this is her first proper sleep in years without ship hammocks or screaming, while Cora Adame reveals she woke confused by the softness, expecting chains. Estra expresses wonder at simple luxuries like curtains, having forgotten such comforts existed.

Around 1:45 PM, the satiated group moves to the parlor, a room smelling of beeswax and old books with burgundy velvet curtains and sunlight streaming through tall windows. Florence Claymore rests on a chaise with her wrapped ankle, having helped orchestrate the rescue. Rachael settles the heavily sedated Alec onto a velvet settee, arranging blankets with infinite care while knitting nearby. Mina collapses onto a settee, kicking off her boots and declaring she could die content there, while Cora curls in a wingback chair, cataloguing the room's three exits but allowing herself to relax. Estra stands overwhelmed in the center of the room before cautiously sitting on furniture as if she might break it, admitting she is not used to such safety.

Ryan inquires about Charlotte Claymore, recalling she was "dead weight" when he carried her to bed after her traumatic reaction to seeing "you know who" (the demon Loren). Florence confirms Charlotte remains unconscious upstairs as of roughly 1:30 PM, curled tightly under blankets after days of insomnia and stimulant powders caused her body to crash. Rachael notes she will need soup and company when she wakes. Estra identifies Charlotte's condition as acute shock, warning she will likely wake screaming or resist waking entirely, and should not be alone. The group resolves to rest while the world waits, basking in the rare moment of safety and found family bonding as the fire pops and afternoon sun warms the room.
