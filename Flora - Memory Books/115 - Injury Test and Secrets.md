# 115 - Injury Test and Secrets
On Wednesday, June 21, 1843, from approximately 3:54 PM to 4:20 PM in the Great Hall and later the Council Chamber of the Palace of Loth Logan, Ryan Adame, still projected after his morning test, revealed to the gathered family that Loren Claymore had been physically present at Claymore Manor during his projection and had left a sealed letter on the parlor mantle. The group processed the tactical implications—Loren may suspect projection capability, the letter could be bait or intelligence, and Loren's agents may be embedded in Duran's court. Ryan proposed testing what happens to his projection if his vessel body is injured. He tied a small knot in the golden thread around Charlotte's presence as an anchor-lock technique, ensuring she could not release the connection. Charlotte, suppressing her fear, agreed to proceed. Estra, with professional reluctance, made a shallow two-inch cut on Ryan's vessel forearm. Through the sensory bond, Charlotte felt the full phantom pain of the cut, screaming silently. Ryan's projection destabilized and flickered between solid and translucent for three seconds before solidifying. The knot technique held and prevented full collapse. Cora documented Trial 52, confirming vessel injury transmits sensation to the anchor, the projection remains stable but flickers for three seconds during injury response, and the knot technique is a viable stabilization method. Howard identified the three-second window as a critical tactical vulnerability. Queen Maya witnessed the test and recognized that the anchor is now a secondary target alongside Ryan. Ryan returned to his vessel body via Charlotte's anchor-pull, then embraced Estra, kissing her forehead and swearing on his life never to ask her to cut him again. He thanked her for being the only person he trusted with the task. He then returned to Charlotte, squeezed her hand, apologized for the pain she felt, and promised to find a way to suppress or control the sensory bond's pain transmission, suggesting they ask Althena about a toggle switch. The group relocated through hidden servant passages to the secured Council Chamber. Ryan wrapped two golden loops around Charlotte's presence, offering her the choice to reveal her silver ice power to the family. Charlotte chose to keep the secret, stating it stays within the inner circle. Ryan directly refused Maya's request for disclosure, citing operational security and the primacy of protecting the hidden variable over nation politics. Maya accepted his refusal, acknowledging he called her 'mom' twice and valuing that trust. The council debated retrieving the sealed letter from Claymore Manor versus leaving it as potential bait. William offered knowledge of his escape tunnels for a potential retrieval mission. The group acknowledged they must protect both Ryan and Charlotte as dual targets while Loren's agents remain embedded in the Duranian court with unknown identities.
