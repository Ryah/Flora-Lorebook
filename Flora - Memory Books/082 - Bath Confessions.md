# 082 - Bath Confessions

On Monday, June 19, 1843, between 7:25 PM and 7:35 PM, in the Lord's Bath at Claymore Manor, Ryan Adame and Estra Hamilton share an intimate aftermath following their blood debt rematch. Both bear visible injuries—Estra with skinned knuckles trembling as she unwraps bloody linen bandages, Ryan with a split lip and purple bruising across his ribs and torso. Estra admits feeling relieved when her fist finally connected during their fight, acknowledging that even when trying to win, she had been holding back from truly hurting him. She reveals she selected blue silk for her wedding dress, seeking something solid and steady when everything else felt unstable.

Ryan confesses he was also holding back because he has grown fond of her, revealing his intentions to marry her. After both undress—Estra removing her blood-and-grass-stained sleeveless linen shirt and torn charcoal fighting trousers, Ryan peeling off fabric stuck to dried blood—Estra hesitates at the tub's edge, warning that she will sit where she can watch the entrance, though she admits she will be looking at him instead. Ryan enters the steaming water first, followed by Estra, who reveals a body mapped with violence: silver scar lines across her ribs, a jagged mark above her hip, bruises flowering from the fight, and knees abraded from courtyard stones. She confesses she is not clean but wants to be with him, touching his swollen lip and marveling that he still wants her in his marriage despite the damage she inflicted.

Ryan cups her face and tells her she is beautiful, prompting Estra to confess her history as a killer for Loren Claymore and the terrible things she has done. She admits her body is a weapon that has been used, but expresses her desire to be something other than a blade—to be beautiful and worth keeping. Moving closer through the water until their bodies press together, her bandaged hand resting over his heart, she asks him to touch her not to heal or check for injury, but to claim her as his own.
