# 015 - Dawn Combat Assessment

Thursday, June 15, 1843, 5:58 AM to 6:40 AM. In the misty Eastern Courtyard of Claymore Manor, Captain Eva Riensdauter intercepts Ryan moments after he completes dawn training with Howard. Dressed in Claymore red-and-black battle dress with steel greaves and a Duranian-made sword at her hip, her light green hair braided over one shoulder, Eva confronts Ryan with professional skepticism. She dismisses Howard's assessment that Ryan is at seventy percent readiness, criticizing his reliance on pain tolerance and citing his history of suicidal ideation as liability rather than credential. When Ryan insists his limitations are only physical, Eva challenges him to an unarmed combat demonstration to test his actual capabilities versus his claims.

Ryan squares up using techniques learned from his sister Mina during years of training in darkness, revealing far greater proficiency in hand-to-hand combat than with blades. Eva attacks his injured left side repeatedly, testing his rotation limits and defensive instincts. Ryan's footwork proves exceptional—fast, agile, and precise—allowing him to evade and block her military strikes despite his healing knife wound and exertion fatigue. Eva notes that his body knows the confined-space fighting his sister taught him, and that his hands move faster without the encumbrance of a sword.

Lady Florence Claymore arrives wearing a fitted white blouse and dark trousers, her golden hair braided for action. She insists on joining the assessment as Ryan's extraction partner. Eva immediately tests the pair by attacking Florence, throwing her to the flagstones twice to probe her reactions. The first throw reveals Florence's wrestling instincts and Ragnar temper—she rises furious rather than frightened, grass stains blooming across her back. Eva identifies Florence's weakness: committing fully to attacks without planning exits, crossing her ankles on recovery, and losing her weapon in the opening seconds of engagement.

When Eva drives between them to test their coordination, Ryan and Florence demonstrate instinctive teamwork without verbal communication. Ryan sidesteps right to clear Eva's reach while Florence holds the front line, absorbing the captain's momentum. Ryan redirects Eva from behind while Florence drives forward, leveraging Eva's own velocity to throw the captain onto the wet stone. Eva rises with mud on her hip and a reassessment in her eyes, acknowledging that while their technique is rough, their timing and mutual awareness are undeniable.

Eva explicitly compares Florence to her father Howard, noting she hits like a Ragnar—no technique, all stubborn aggression—but declares this an asset for extraction missions where plans inevitably fail. She contrasts this with Howard's emotional judgment, establishing her own empirical assessment as the standard. Despite identifying Ryan's telegraphed rotations and Florence's defensive gaps, Eva grants qualified approval for both to join the extraction team to rescue Mina and Cora from the Amber Isles, contingent on Doctor Mercer clearing Ryan's tissue for climbing. She promises further training in sword retention and blade work upon their return, noting that Ryan must learn to make the sword feel like an extension of himself rather than a borrowed tool.

The scene concludes with the morning sun clearing the treeline, the kitchen bell ringing for breakfast, and Eva departing to report to Charlotte while Ryan and Florence share a moment of mutual recognition—they move together without speaking, anticipating each other's positioning in combat. Florence, still burning with competitive fury at being thrown, bumps her shoulder against Ryan's in solidarity as they prepare to face Mercer's competency examination before the dangerous extraction mission.
