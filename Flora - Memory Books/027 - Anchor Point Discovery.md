# 027 - Anchor Point Discovery

Thursday, June 15, 1843, 4:00 PM - 5:00 PM. The Ragnar vessel approaches Claymore Manor dock through inland channels, carrying Ryan, his wife Florence, ally Callista Venn, and newly rescued sisters Mina and Cora Adame. Howard departed ahead on a speedboat to notify Charlotte and Rachael of their imminent arrival. Ryan surfaces from exhausted sleep as the vessel docks, his back wound stitched with catgut by Callista earlier throbbing dully. Florence, despite her heavily splinted left ankle and exhaustion, insists on supporting him, refusing to yield to her own pain. Callista announces their arrival while scanning for threats, and Howard Blakeney waits on the dock with Charlotte, who displays controlled anxiety beneath her black-gowned composure.

Upon disembarking, Cora freezes at a dock support post, recognizing a weathered carving: a circle bisected by a horizontal line with three small dots arranged in a triangle beneath it. She drops her brass compass, revealing this was their father William Adame's personal symbol, carved into his desk as his "anchor point" and "way home." Charlotte confirms the same mark appears in the manor's foundation stones and wine cellar behind the third rack. Howard reveals William visited Claymore Manor before his death and the explosion, carving his intended escape route into the very dock of the estate that now shelters his son. The implication that William's route to Duran began here, fifteen years prior, hangs heavily over the group as the doom tension rises.

Ryan, bleeding through his bandages from reopened stitches sustained during the morning's cliff climb, insists on prioritizing medical care over investigation. He suggests Charlotte lead the evening assembly due to his compromised state, delegating authority publicly. Florence initially resists handing off support duties, her competitive pride wounded by the implication she cannot handle the burden, but Ryan clarifies his concern stems from care, not weakness. Mina steps in to assist, supporting Ryan's other side as they ascend the stone steps, while Charlotte offers formal welcome to the Adame sisters, revealing she stayed up all night drafting legal protections for them.

Ryan introduces Charlotte to Mina as his wife, describing her efficiency as masking a soft heart and noting her inability to handle gratitude without fluster. Charlotte blushes visibly at Ryan's thanks, her controlled demeanor cracking as Florence points out the flush beneath her black gown. Rachael Blakeney meets them at the manor entrance, immediately assuming command of the medical situation. She assesses Ryan's gray pallor and bleeding wound, dismisses Florence's stubborn insistence that she can continue carrying his weight, and physically transfers Ryan's support to her own maternal frame. Rachael orders Henry to fetch Mercer immediately for the east wing surgery, while Charlotte shepherds Mina and Cora inside for warmth and rest. Ryan welcomes his sisters to their new home as the group finally passes through the entrance archway, leaving the mysterious symbol and the questions of William's connection to Claymore Manor for later investigation.
