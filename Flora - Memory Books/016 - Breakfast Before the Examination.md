# 016 - Breakfast Before the Examination

Thursday, June 15, 1843, approximately 6:42-6:58 AM. Following dawn combat training with Howard and a coordinated combat assessment with Eva in Claymore Manor's eastern courtyard, Ryan walks arm-in-arm with Florence toward the manor for breakfast. Florence, wearing a grass-stained white blouse with a purple bruise forming on her forearm from the training, quietly raises the assembly plan for later today, noting she heard it but will not press for discussion until tonight after the extraction. Ryan promises to tell her everything on the road to Amber Isles, admitting it is time to get it off his chest and he wanted to mention it to her sooner.

Entering the dining hall, they find Howard Blakeney Ragnar at the far end of the mahogany table finishing his second breakfast, wearing a charcoal shirt with sleeves rolled to display scarred forearms. Charlotte Claymore sits at the midpoint with documents stacked in red-ribboned piles and wire-frame spectacles, while Rachael Blakeney stands near the sideboard in an emerald morning dress. The room smells of butter, rosemary, and bacon, with morning light streaming through eastern windows.

Howard notes that Ryan moves like his father William, endorsing his combat abilities, while Charlotte tracks Doctor Mercer's approach via the north road (estimated 8-10 minutes away). Ryan discusses his sword training limitations, expressing desire to focus on making the blade an extension of his arm rather than just an object he holds. Florence reminds Ryan of his promise to disclose something important, prompting Howard to remark that confession will not kill him before the physician arrives, while Rachael advocates for Ryan to eat first.

Ryan defers the personal revelation to 'on the road,' but reveals publicly he requested an assembly via Rachael to discuss discoveries from the previous day (regarding Edward, the suspected mole). Charlotte confirms the assembly framework is drafted as a routine household review, scheduled for after the extraction and rescue of Mina and Cora. Florence realizes she is being excluded from secret planning, prompting tension until Charlotte explains the restriction is operational, not personal.

Ryan places his hand on Florence's knee and whispers his first initiated physical affection, promising again to tell her everything on the road and explaining he did not mean to keep secrets but needed to ensure it would not compromise operations. Rachael then challenges Charlotte's decision to keep Florence ignorant of the mole intelligence, arguing that Florence deserves to know she might be walking into an ambush at Eleanor's household. Charlotte concedes, agreeing to brief Florence with limited operational details (no names) to protect her without compromising the investigation.

At approximately 6:55 AM, Henry announces Doctor Mercer's carriage has reached the front courtyard. Ryan prepares for the competency examination that will determine his clearance to participate in the extraction mission.
