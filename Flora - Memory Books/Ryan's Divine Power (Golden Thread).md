# Ryan's Divine Power (Golden Thread)
Ryan was blessed by the goddess [[Althena]] with divine power. No one else in the world has this power unless Althena gives it to them directly. Magic does not exist elsewhere – Ryan's power is unique.

## The Golden Thread (Mental Tool)
Ryan controls his power by visualizing a **golden thread** in his mind, within a space he calls **"the brick room"** (see [[The Sensory Link]]). The thread is **not physical** – it's a mental device that helps him measure and direct power.
**How it works:**
- Ryan imagines wrapping the thread around his body to control how much power he uses.
- **One loop around his finger** = very little power, just enough to catch a water droplet and make it float.
- **The entire thread around his whole body** = massive power: he glows like a beacon visible for miles, can stop rain and turn it gold, and create waves large enough to capsize warships (even by accident).
When Ryan wraps thread, he **feels a "catch"** when targeting something. Pulling on the catch makes the target move toward him. The thread can target water, stone, steel, and other materials. It can even target composite objects and isolate specific layers (e.g., pulling only the steel blade from a weapon).

## Material Thresholds (Tested)
| Material | Approximate Loops | Behavior |
|----------|------------------|-----------|
| Water (from humid air) | 2-3 | Easily yields. Water wants to move. |
| Water (basin volume) | 10 | Can pull a column. |
| Stone (small rock) | 10 | Resists but moves with enough loops. |
| Steel (karambit blade) | 15 | Dense and coherent. Requires more loops. |
| Composite objects | Variable | Thread penetrates layers; can isolate one material. |

## The Hard Limit: Living Flesh
Ryan has set an absolute, non-negotiable limit: **He will never use the thread to pull blood or water from a living person.** Doing so would rupture cells, collapse vessels, and cause catastrophic internal injuries. Even if technically possible, he refuses. He will not test on allies, enemies, or anyone he cares about. This is a moral boundary he will not cross.
## Sharing Power with Charlotte (Silver Manifestation)
**Critical: No one else can share Ryan's power.** The only reason Charlotte can access any of it is because of their unique **sensory link** (see [[The Sensory Link]]). This was never intended by Althena. It is a **loophole** – a harmonic accident resulting from their specific bond. Without that bond, power sharing is impossible.
**How sharing works:**
- Ryan must **explicitly** wrap the golden thread around his mental image of Charlotte (the silver presence he feels through their bond).
- This transfers a portion of his power to her, which manifests as **silver light**.
- Charlotte has **no power of her own**. The silver effect is Ryan's golden thread resonating through her.
**What Charlotte can do with shared power:**
- Freeze water into intricate crystalline structures (hexagonal lattices, ice pillars, floating ice architecture).
- Create sustained ice formations that hover.
- Build defensive barriers (crystalline stasis).
**Limitations:**
- Ryan must allocate thread to Charlotte. She cannot take power.
- Too many loops allocated to her (e.g., 10) causes **channel overflow** – she cannot hold the signal, and the manifestation collapses.
- If Ryan and Charlotte use matching loop counts (e.g., 10 each), **harmonic resonance** builds until the structure explodes inward unless released gradually.
- Charlotte must learn to "ride" the power, not force it. Silver crystallizes; it does not push.
## Resonant Projection (The "Shadow")
Ryan can send his awareness to a distant location while his body stays behind. This is called **projection** or **shadow form**.
**Requirements to project:**
- Ryan must have a strong **emotional connection** to the destination or person. He cannot project to random places or strangers.
- His body remains behind as the **anchor**. Charlotte serves as his primary anchor – she can feel his location and pull him back.
**What the projection can do:**
- See, hear, and speak (the shadow's voice is audible).
- Move around and observe.
- Feel temperature, texture, pressure (perception only).
**What the projection CANNOT do:**
- **Act on the physical world** – cannot touch, push, open doors, hold objects, or fight.
- **Channel divine power** – attempting to use the thread from the projection causes agonizing feedback (like being torn in two). Power requires a physical anchor body.
- **Interact with matter** – hands pass through objects and people.
**Dangers:**
- **Self-contact** (shadow touching own anchor body) causes neurological dissonance – vertigo, nausea, potential collapse.
- Overextending (e.g., projecting across an ocean) strains the thread and causes exhaustion.
- If the thread snaps while projected, the consequences are unknown.
**How to return:**
- Ryan can "gather himself" by feeling for the thread and intending to be singular.
- If he cannot find the way back, **Charlotte can pull him** through the bond. She feels his location as a "weight on a rope" and can reel him in. This works across hundreds of miles.
## Still Growing – Unknown Limits
Althena herself does not know everything this power can do. The bond with Charlotte created unintended harmonics. The rules are still being discovered through testing. Some things that seem impossible may become possible. Some things that seem safe may have hidden costs.
**Currently unknown:**
- Minimum loops for various materials.
- Whether other substances (wood, glass, bone) have different thresholds.
- Maximum projection range before thread break.
- What happens if the anchor releases while Ryan is extended.
- Whether projection can ever learn to act (current answer: no, but not final).
## Summary of Key Rules
1. **Only Ryan has divine power** – unless Althena gives it to someone else directly.
2. **Only Charlotte can share it** – and only because of their unique sensory link loophole.
3. **Power sharing requires Ryan's explicit permission** – he must wrap thread around her mental image.
4. **Projection is for reconnaissance only** – it cannot fight or channel power.
5. **Living flesh is off-limits** – Ryan will never pull blood or water from a person.
6. **Thread is mental** – not a physical object others can see or grab.
7. **The bond's existence is secret from enemies** – applies to power use as well.
