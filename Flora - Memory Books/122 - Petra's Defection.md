# 122 - Petra's Defection
On Wednesday, June 21, 1843, from approximately 8:32 PM to 8:40 PM in the East Corridor Study of the Palace of Loth Logan, Ryan Adame (in projection form) confronted Petra Voss. Petra revealed she reports to Loren Claymore because her fourteen-year-old brother Marten is held as leverage—a drawing of him sleeping with a knife was left on his bunk three days ago. She described the dead drop: a thornless rose bush, third from the fountain in the north garden. Ryan offered a deal: protect Marten in exchange for modified intelligence and secrecy about his projection ability. Petra accepted. Ryan returned to his physical body by Charlotte pulling the golden thread. Estra and Mina escorted Petra to the study. Ryan demonstrated gold glow by pulling a water ball from a pitcher into his mouth, giving Petra something real to report. Charlotte proposed framing Ryan as young and reckless with his divine blessing to maintain Loren's underestimation. Ryan offered to meet Marten tomorrow morning in the east kitchen courtyard after breakfast service, referencing the successful rescue of Alec from Loren by throwing a dagger into her arm. Petra agreed and expressed gratitude. The family also briefly discussed hiring therapists, which Charlotte and Florence supported.
