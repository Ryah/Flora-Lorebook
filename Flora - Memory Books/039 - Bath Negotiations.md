# 039 - Bath Negotiations

Friday, June 16, 1843, morning: Ryan arrives at Claymore Manor's Charlotte's Study at 8:30 AM with fresh bleeding from a reopened back wound suffered during combat with Estra. Dr. Thaddeus Mercer sutures the laceration (requiring twelve to fourteen deep stitches) while Charlotte works urgently on legal asylum documents for the defecting assassin, citing the need to file before noon to prevent Loren from challenging jurisdiction. Florence protests the business intrusion during medical treatment but assists in positioning Ryan, her protective anxiety evident. Charlotte reveals she is drafting irrevocable asylum under Cassia county authority backed by Blakeney-Ragnar alliance treaties, requiring Estra to formally renounce Duranian citizenship. After Mercer finishes and departs with warnings about scar tissue accumulation and a mandate for forty-eight hours of rest, Ryan suggests relocating to the Mistress Bath.

In the marble bathing chamber (8:45 AM), Charlotte and Florence bathe Ryan together in an intimate triad moment. Florence removes his clothing carefully to avoid disturbing fresh stitches, and both women help him into the copper tub. Florence, wearing only a cream shift with her golden braid unraveling, washes his hair while Charlotte, in her burgundy velvet gown with rolled sleeves, massages lavender oil into his shoulders, carefully avoiding the sutured wound. They discuss the evening's planned meeting with Estra at 8 PM, with Florence expressing jealousy but acceptance of the triad dynamic, and Charlotte softening her usual legal precision into tender caretaking. Ryan jokes about "noble vacation days" and PTO, prompting lighthearted anecdotes from Florence about skipping duties at seventeen.

The intimate atmosphere shatters at 9:15 AM when Estra Hamilton intrudes unannounced, entering in her Andrea's Cross disguise (wide-brimmed hat, leather shorts, loose blouse) and catching Ryan naked in the tub between his wives. Estra reveals she is early because Loren has accelerated the timeline: she holds Estra's brother Alec hostage, having taken him from their Valheim village six months prior. Yesterday, Loren sent Estra Alec's finger (left hand, little one) with a blood-stained linen note threatening his eye in eleven days and his heart if Estra fails to deliver Ryan or betrays Loren.

Despite this leverage, Estra declares she is choosing Ryan and the Claymores over her brother's safety, admitting she is "out of moves" and offering herself as a weapon to be aimed rather than an ally to be trusted. She reveals intimate knowledge of Loren's Duranian networks, drop points, and codes. Ryan, remaining exposed in the tub, defers to Charlotte's authority while challenging Estra to prove her loyalty given her earlier attempt on Florence's life. Estra collapses to her knees in desperation, offering to be chained or guarded, begging only that they move against Loren within eleven days to save Alec before he is further dismembered.

Charlotte accepts the blood-stained evidence and calculates the accelerated threat. Florence observes Estra's genuine tears, noting their authenticity. Charlotte dictates terms: Estra will attend the evening assembly as a "provincial curiosity" seeking patronage, reveal nothing of this intrusion, and follow instructions exactly. If she complies, they will discuss extraction plans for Alec that do not involve delivering Ryan to Loren. Florence acknowledges Estra's tactical value despite her jealousy. The scene concludes with Estra kneeling on the marble floor, accepted provisionally as a desperate asset, while the triad maintains protective dominance over Ryan's vulnerable state.
