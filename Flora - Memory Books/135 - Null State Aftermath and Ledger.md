# 135 - Null State Aftermath and Ledger
On Wednesday, June 21, 1843, at approximately 6:58 AM in the Solarium of the Palace of Loth Logan, Ryan Adame asked Althena a critical question about the sensory link: whether it can be dampened on demand without direct divine intervention, referencing the temporary suppression during Estra's wedding and its relevance to Charlotte's role as his Anchor who feels pain during projection. Althena confirmed that self-dampening is possible but clarified it is not a mechanical toggle switch. She explained the bond is alive—a connection between two souls—and dampening it requires learning to 'step back' or create internal distance without breaking the thread. This is a skill, not a mechanism, and comes with significant costs. Althena revealed that full dampening—closing the door entirely—would permanently silence the bond without severing it. The connection would remain technically intact but become dormant, like a bell that never rings. Charlotte would stop feeling Ryan and he would stop feeling her, leaving both in permanent isolation despite the bond's continued existence. Mina immediately categorized this as a last resort, not a convenience. Estra identified the more practical application: partial dampening, or learning to move along a spectrum from full connection to full silence with infinite gradations between. Althena confirmed this is possible but much harder to master than complete shutdown. Cora recognized the profound implications—emotional regulation at a divine level, the ability to choose exactly how much of another person to let in moment by moment. Charlotte felt sharp relief that she could potentially learn to block the pain during projection without losing the warmth and reassurance of the bond, but also experienced a flicker of cold loneliness at the memory of the bond going quiet during the wedding. Althena warned that mastery could take months or years, as the bond is honest by nature and teaching it selectivity is like teaching a river to flow uphill. Crucially, she emphasized the dampening cannot be learned alone—it must be practiced together by both Ryan and Charlotte because the bond belongs to both of them. Claire served Ryan tea during the discussion. Florence noted that Charlotte would need to reopen any door she closes, unable to leave it permanently shut. Althena adhered to the 'No Spoilers' rule, confirming the skill's existence and nature without revealing the method of achievement.
