# 022 - Sea Witch Departure

Thursday, June 15, 1843, approximately 9:40-9:50 AM. Under clear 64-degree skies at Briarhaven Dock, Ryan departs Thomas Greaves's carriage and boards the shallow-draft fishing vessel Sea Witch with Florence, wearing climbing clothes and carrying a map, knife, and locket. Ryan briefs Callista Venn—dressed in a salt-stained burgundy coat with a distinctive jaw scar—on the extraction plan: Howard will meet them at the dock to dispatch Ryan and Florence to infiltrate the Amber Isles and retrieve Mina and Cora, with a hard contingency that Howard will invade by force at noon if the couple fails to return or signal. Callista confirms her courier professionalism, reporting that despite enjoying Lady Charlotte's four-times-rate hospitality at Claymore Manor (hot food, soft bed), she executed three dawn detours through the village and six rear-guard checks to ensure no surveillance followed her to the extraction point. Ryan, newly elevated to nobility only two days prior and still bearing a healing knife wound on his back, offers Callista permanent retainer employment at Claymore Manor with improved compensation and reduced danger, directing her to negotiate with Charlotte while personally guaranteeing medical care and housing if injury occurs during the mission. Ryan and Florence adopt wrestling codenames 'Stardust and the Baron,' with Florence proposing finishing moves: the acrobatic 'Starfall' (a modified shooting star press from high ground) or the personally symbolic 'Basement Lock' (wrapping Eleanor in her own chains and casting her down the imprisonment stairs). As a Ragnar clansman deckhand announces their approach to the 'broken tooth' rock formation marking the drop point—a narrow cove accessible only through a dark cliff cleft barely wide enough for the vessel's beam—Florence establishes tactical climbing protocol: she takes point on the treacherous fisherman's path of slick rocks, with Ryan following her exact line, each committed to catching the other if they slip. Citing the map coordinates (left at bottom, eighth row, three presses), Ryan shares a desperate pre-combat kiss with Florence, acknowledging their mortality before facing the imminent extraction.
