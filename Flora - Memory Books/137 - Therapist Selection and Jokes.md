# 137 - Therapist Selection and Jokes
On Thursday, June 22, 1843, at approximately 12:42 PM in the East Sitting Room of the Palace of Loth Logan, Ryan Adame jokingly asked whether he would be classified as adolescent trauma or noble trauma, and wondered aloud if Althena should be his therapist since she would actually understand the divine vessel aspect. Florence Claymore immediately laughed, imagining a divine therapy intake form listing complaints about a demon mother-in-law and a goddess who compliments her wife's chest. Charlotte Claymore noted dryly that Althena would be a logistical nightmare as a therapist due to unpredictable manifestation, lack of formal credentials, and a tendency to reframe trauma as sacred trials. Estra Hamilton added that Althena would claim the null state builds character and then ask for tea. Florence declared this banter constituted family progress.

Florence then offered serious advice: during initial therapy sessions, Ryan should focus on the kidnapping, the brick room, and the marriage—topics a normal therapist can address—and only mention the divine vessel and goddess manifestations by session six if the therapist has proven stable. Charlotte acknowledged this as a reasonable approach, prompting Florence to note she has reasonable approaches sometimes beyond poledancing and cheese.

At approximately 12:44 PM, Charlotte finalized the therapist assignments with a decisive quill tap. She slid Dr. Prentiss's dossier toward Estra for combat trauma, Dr. Cross's toward the sleeping Alec Hamilton for children's disaster experience, and kept Lady Keane's for herself, admitting with visible effort that Lady Keane was for her own therapy. Florence immediately celebrated Charlotte's admission of needing help, which Charlotte reframed as a mere scheduling preference. Estra and Claire both confirmed they witnessed the admission, with Claire deliberately scratching her quill loudly to document it for the record.

Claire then produced a Therapist Intake Supplement draft for Ryan, a humorous internal document listing his status as Prince, Divine Vessel, and married three times, with special considerations including infant kidnapping, sensory deprivation in the brick room, triadic marriage officiated by a goddess, divine matter manipulation via golden thread, null-state transit survival, occasional goddess manifestation in the bathtub with a note not to scream, and a mother-in-law who is an active hostile intelligence asset. The draft concluded with a therapist's note reading 'I'm going to need more tea.'

Charlotte closed her portfolio crisply and outlined next steps: Howard's team would run secondary security checks on all three candidates that afternoon, formal inquiries would be sent by evening courier once clearances came back, and initial consultations could begin as early as the following week. She called the hour productive, which Florence translated as Charlotte expressing love for the family and gladness about everyone getting help. Charlotte countered that it meant the cheese was also acceptable.

At approximately 12:47 PM, Ryan approached the therapist chart Claire had created listing the three candidates with their assignments and clearance statuses. He took the quill and added a fourth entry: 'Null State Abyss' under Candidate, assigned to 'Ryan,' with status 'Awaiting clearance.' He smiled and referenced his earlier joke from the morning that the null state abyss enjoys chess. Florence erupted with delighted laughter at the defacement of Charlotte's immaculate documentation. Estra made a soft exhale of appreciation, recognizing the darkness of the joke given the morning's ordeal. Charlotte remained composed but the bond carried warm exasperation, noting the null state is not a licensed practitioner, has no credentials, cannot provide references, and would be unavailable for consultation since accessing it requires either controlled tether release or catastrophic thread rupture. She nevertheless offered to note his preference in the margins. Claire, struggling to maintain composure, asked if she should draft a formal inquiry to 'Dear Sir or Madam Void,' then lost the battle and laughed openly.

Florence declared this the best family she had ever had, sprawled dramatically on the chaise. Charlotte snapped her portfolio shut and redirected the group toward rest and preparation for the 8 PM dead drop intercept, noting the null state transit had been nontrivial regardless of jokes about its recreational preferences. Estra volunteered for first watch over Alec. Claire confirmed she would finish drafting the formal inquiries, excluding the abyss—mostly.

At approximately 12:50 PM, Ryan looked at Estra and asked to be woken around 4 or 5 PM, citing his promise to Alec to see his dragon drawing and work on the surprise he had mentioned that morning while waiting for Petra. He acknowledged needing sleep after the day's events. Estra firmly set the wake time at 5 PM, not a minute earlier, noting he was running on fumes. The bond carried warmth from Charlotte, who knew the surprise was a family portrait—she had seen the dreamscape version with everyone smiling and was keeping the secret for Ryan. Claire promised to have formal inquiries drafted by the time he woke, excluding the abyss entirely at Charlotte's insistence, though her smile suggested otherwise.

At approximately 12:52 PM, Ryan theatrically declared that as prince in the palace, he overrode Charlotte's authority and ordered Claire to make the Null State Abyss the only option on the therapist list, joking he would figure out how to get everyone there. Florence called after him, declaring she would put a void with a couch on the household crest. Estra reiterated the 5 PM wake time. Charlotte stated dryly that princely authority does not override medical licensing boards nor permit appointment of extradimensional voids to clinical positions. Claire promised to draft the list with one option—for the margins only—but did not actually promise. Ryan exited the East Sitting Room into the quiet corridor, heading toward the West Wing suite for a nap, the bed there having waited empty for fifteen years for his return.
