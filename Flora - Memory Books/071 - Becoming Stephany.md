# 071 - Becoming Stephany

Sunday, June 18, 1843, approximately 6:52 PM to 7:35 PM. In Claymore Manor's West Wing Guest Room, Ryan offers the nickname 'Etsie' to Estra, who wears a white linen shirt with rolled sleeves revealing scraped, bloodied knuckles. She admits no one has ever called her 'cute' or offered gentle names without hidden barbs, revealing deep-seated suspicion of softness. Alec interjects that he calls her Stephany, her birth name, referencing childhood stories she invented about a purple-eyed princess named Stephany saving her brother from dragons—a metaphor for their own survival. Estra confesses her past self: before Loren made her a weapon, she was Stephany, a soft girl who loved terrible poetry, books about dragons, and owned a cat named Mittens. She believed in kindness over blades and wanted to be a scholar, but abandoned that identity for survival, convinced she became the sword rather than the salvation and had failed to protect anyone. Around 6:58 PM, a sudden gale strikes, tearing tiles from the east wing roof and flooding the north tower. Mrs. Marie bursts in with flour dusting her grey hair, explaining Alec must evacuate his compromised chamber and no other rooms remain. Estra immediately insists Alec stays with her, refusing to let him sleep alone or on the floor, and asks Ryan to remain as well, expressing her raw need to see both safe in the same room. Servants deliver wool blankets and hot apple cider spiced with cinnamon—Stephany's childhood remedy for storms—which Estra grips while tracing the blood vigil scar on Ryan's palm. Ryan confronts her self-perception, firmly stating that she saved his life during their recent mission and forbidding her from claiming she never saved anyone again. As the storm rattles the shutters, Alec requests a bedtime story. Estra narrates an allegorical tale of a nameless knight serving a cruel queen, doing terrible deeds to keep her brother hostage in a tower, who meets a stranger fighting through choice rather than duty. The stranger sees the woman beneath the weapon and saves the brother, teaching the knight that she can choose to be a guardian rather than a blade. She admits the story is true, identifying herself as the knight, Loren as the queen, and Ryan as the stranger who chose to see her humanity. The scene concludes with Estra accepting physical comfort, her bandaged knuckles interlaced with Ryan's, as the three settle together against the storm, bonded by the revelation that Stephany still exists beneath the assassin.
