# 019 - Culvert Intelligence and Brick Room

Thursday, June 15, 1843, approximately 7:38 AM to 8:20 AM. Ryan discovers a hand-drawn sketch of the Adame property tucked under his pillow by Fiona, the manor's herbalist. The map, rendered on the back of a herb inventory sheet bearing Claymore Manor's watermark, details a drainage culvert beneath the northern wall near a blackberry patch, wide enough for crawling to access the garden grounds near the potting shed. Ryan brings this intelligence to the Blue Drawing Room where Charlotte, wearing dark traveling clothes, annotates operational maps spread across a Turkey carpet weighted by a jade paperweight and brass candlestick. Howard occupies the window seat in a strained dark green coat, while Rachael observes from a cream settee.

Charlotte immediately questions the timing and reliability of Fiona's intelligence, noting the servant's two-year silence and the risk of compromise given the active mole investigation. Howard corroborates the culvert's existence through William Adame's old letters about marshland drainage work. Rachael defends Fiona's character, revealing the girl's traumatic past and that she volunteered the information at dawn specifically to help the extraction, asking for discretion to avoid gossip about her Cassia County origins. Ryan explains his kindness toward Fiona during nightly salve applications for his healing knife wound, arguing that trust generated the disclosure.

Florence enters dressed for climbing in grey trousers, soft-soled boots, a charcoal waistcoat, and a canvas pack containing metal gear, her golden hair braided with a leather cord. The group finalizes the extraction plan for Mina and Cora: primary entry via fisherman's path, backup via Fiona's culvert route, with a ten-minute operational window. Ryan reveals the grim details of his three-year captivity in a 15-by-15 foot soundproof brick room in the Adame basement, accessible only by pressing a specific discolored brick (8th row from bottom, third press) to open a hidden door. The room contains a ten-foot chain, a grate too small for escape, and facilities for sewage only. He establishes a three-week survival window if captured due to Eleanor's starvation torture methods.

Florence forcefully rejects Ryan's instruction to flee if he is captured, insisting on extraction as a team. Ryan admits he anticipated her refusal but wanted to hear it, affirming their partnership. Charlotte sketches the basement layout while demanding specific contingencies. Howard details the second wave: twelve Ragnar guards positioned along the coastal road will breach the front gate at noon if they aren't on the boat by then. Charlotte explains the cover story (visiting Cassia County family) and confirms Greaves as the trusted carriage driver, his family embedded in the estate providing absolute leverage against betrayal. Rachael manages final preparations as the clock ticks toward 8:20 AM, with one hour remaining before departure. Howard offers final wisdom about Ryan fighting like his father William before Ryan and Florence depart to review climbing gear and ground strategies.
