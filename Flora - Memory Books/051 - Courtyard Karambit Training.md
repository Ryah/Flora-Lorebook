# 051 - Courtyard Karambit Training

Friday, June 16, 1843, 9:30 PM to 10:25 PM. Ryan Adame defends his decision to trust contract killer Estra Hamilton to his wives and allies gathered at Claymore Manor, arguing that her desperate kneeling and begging demonstrate authentic vulnerability rather than deception, as professional killers cannot afford to show weakness without losing business. He emphasizes that his motivation is saving Estra's brother Alec, asserting that no sibling should suffer for another's actions.

The group transitions to the mist-shrouded Eastern Courtyard (temperature dropping from 56°F to 52°F) for weapons training. Estra, wearing a disheveled Andrea's Cross disguise, prepares to teach karambit technique. Ryan initiates a trust bond by offering his personal dagger hilt-first to Estra while holding her curved blade, declaring that as long as the group holds her karambit, she may hold his weapon.

Estra instructs on the karambit's specialized mechanics: the curved blade is designed for hooking and pulling rather than slashing, targeting arterial regions including the throat, armpit, inner thigh, and groin to open opponents with minimal force. Howard Blakeney Ragnar provides supplementary instruction on hip rotation and stance, correcting Ryan's grip while noting his rapid adaptation of unarmed combat principles learned from Mina.

During instruction, Florence Claymore, driven by competitive urgency and frustrated by her splinted ankle, attempts to seize the karambit from Ryan, cutting her palm on the blade. Charlotte restrains her wife while Estra demonstrates field medical skill, using a strip torn from Ryan's shirt to bandage the wound. Despite injury, Florence insists on observing, refusing to be sidelined from learning combat skills.

Ryan practices on a wooden post, rapidly achieving weapon integration where the karambit feels like an extension of his hand. He executes clean hook-and-pull strikes with full hip rotation and commitment, earning professional approval from Estra and Howard. Mina and Cora maintain perimeter security while observing Ryan's progress.

As the session concludes, Ryan returns the karambit to Estra and sheathes his dagger in his boot. He requests Howard acquire a karambit for him, preferring it over swords due to his comfort with close-quarters combat. Florence demands a blade as well, though Charlotte insists she must heal first. Ryan suggests getting a dull-bladed karambit for Florence before a bladed one, to which Howard agrees. Rachael Blakeney summons everyone inside to prepare for the midnight wedding ceremony under Althena's Oracle, which will provide legal protection against Loren Claymore's extraction attempts. The group moves from the torchlit courtyard into the manor, leaving eleven days to rescue Alec Hamilton and twenty days until Loren's deadline.
