# 083 - Bath to Equalizer

Monday evening, June 19, 1843, following the consummation of their marriage in Claymore Manor's Lord's Bath. Ryan asks Estra if she wishes to sleep alone tonight. Estra reveals she has not slept alone in years, having constantly guarded Alec through his nightmares since their captivity under Loren. Despite her desire to be greedy and take up space in Ryan's bed, she confesses her need to check on her brother, who remains vigilant and anxious in unfamiliar territory. She asks if she may bring Alec to sleep with them tonight—acknowledging the damage inherent in bringing her brother on their wedding night—and Ryan accepts without condition, admitting he wanted to hold her as well.

Estra processes this unconditional acceptance with visible emotion, noting that Alec has already begun trusting Ryan, calling him 'brother' for the first time since their mother died. After they separate physically and Ryan lifts her from the cooling water, they dress in practical clothes (Estra in worn shirt and trousers with waistcoat unbuttoned over her bare chest). Ryan clarifies his expectations for their marriage: he does not require softness or elegance from her—qualities his other wives provide—but rather an 'equalizer' who understands darkness and can balance the triad from an outsider's perspective.

Estra accepts this role with stark honesty, warning that she remains functionally controlled rather than healed, sometimes waking with the urge to hurt simply to feel power. She promises to try not to damage Charlotte and Florence, recognizing Charlotte's wariness toward her. She notes that Florence showed her wedding dress fabrics earlier, and she selected a storm-cloud blue—neither too dark nor too light. They agree to find Alec in the informal parlor where Rachael has been keeping him company.

Holding hands as they walk the corridor, Estra explains Rachael's technique of positioning herself to make Alec feel safely cornered with walls at his back. She instructs Ryan to allow her to answer Alec's inevitable questions about their changed dynamic, as her brother trusts her voice above explanations from others, even as Ryan becomes 'something else' to them both. They approach the parlor door with Estra's tension returning as they prepare to transition from private intimacy to public household space.
