# 046 - Family Accords and Assassin Revelations

On the morning of Friday, June 16, 1843, in the East Wing Library of Claymore Manor, Ryan Adame confesses the full truth of his suicide attempt and extraction to his sisters Mina and Cora, revealing that he bled for three days in a closet because he feared betrayal. Charlotte Claymore breaks her rigid composure to confess her guilt over purchasing Ryan like furniture and failing to see the chains that bound him, admitting she failed to prevent his suffering. Mina, using her military assessment skills, acknowledges that Charlotte and Florence carry genuine penitent guilt rather than performative regret, accepting their role in Ryan's healing despite her protective rage. Cora analyzes the timeline with mathematical precision, correlating the assassination attempt three weeks prior with Ryan's sparring match that morning, deducing that Estra is the same agent. Charlotte demands and receives sworn secrecy oaths from the sisters before revealing that Estra is Duranian Intelligence operating under deep cover as Lady Hamilton, arriving tonight at 8pm in the Sion Quarter not as prisoner but as defector offering intelligence on Loren's network in exchange for help extracting her brother from Claymore custody. Florence grapples with jealousy over the mock-date while acknowledging the necessity of the meeting. Ryan explains the sparring match was wordless negotiation and assigns wedding preparations: Mina will coordinate security protocols with Howard Ragnar, while Cora will assist Rachael Blakeney with logistical vetting and ceremony planning for the triadic wedding scheduled for tomorrow, June 17. Ryan affirms his absolute commitment to remain and fight with his chosen family, rejecting extraction and displaying his matching ring while declaring he will protect them all. The group recognizes the parallel dynamics between the women—Mina and Florence as physical protective forces, Charlotte and Cora as strategic analysts—before departing to meet Rachael in the west solarium to transform the wedding into a defensive fortress against Loren's inevitable intrusion.
