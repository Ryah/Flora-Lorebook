# 017 - Mercer's Clearance

The scene takes place on Thursday, June 15, 1843, beginning at approximately 7:00 AM in the Claymore Manor Dining Hall. Doctor Thaddeus Mercer delivers conditional medical clearance for Ryan to participate in the extraction mission to rescue Mina and Cora from the Amber Isles. Mercer notes Ryan's abnormal tissue regeneration rate—approximately forty percent faster than standard medical texts suggest—which mirrors a case he once treated during the Skaag campaign. He imposes strict physical restrictions: Ryan must avoid rotational torque under load, cannot twist at the waist while carrying weight, and must evacuate immediately upon experiencing sharp pain at the wound site. Florence Claymore, sporting a darkening purple bruise on her left forearm and wearing a grass-stained white blouse, insists on accompanying the mission, refusing to enter Eleanor's household alone. She extracts a promise from Ryan to report any physical distress during their carriage ride to Briarhaven.

Charlotte Claymore, seated across from Ryan at the mahogany table with three red-ribboned document stacks, prepares operational parameters for the 9:30 AM departure. She plans to brief Florence and Ryan in the carriage regarding household layout, guard rotations, and intelligence about the security leak indicating a mole within Claymore Manor. Rachael Blakeney occupies the chair to Ryan's left while Howard Blakeney Ragnar holds position at the table's far end. Rachael ensures Ryan consumes butter-laden bread for tissue repair, then dispatches Henry to fetch Fiona, the servant-healer who formulated a miraculous salve from comfrey root, calendula, and beeswax. Rachael arranges for Ryan to receive fresh bandages and lumbar stabilization treatment in his chambers before departure.

Ryan reveals critical intelligence regarding his sisters' combat capabilities, stating that Mina trained him and possesses superior technique and positioning, though he could only defeat her by exhausting her stamina. He notes Cora fights analytically but lacks physical prowess. Charlotte challenges Ryan's confidence, arguing that three years of Eleanor's abuse may have broken Mina's combat reflexes, referencing Ryan's own psychological trauma that led to his suicide attempt. Howard refutes this assessment, recalling William Adame's description of Mina moving like her grandfather and reading blades before they struck, arguing that Mina's successful transmission of coded messages—including the phrase "the blanket" which serves as a specific call for assistance understandable only to Ryan, Cora, and Mina—proves she operates a makeshift resistance network within the household. Howard provides tactical instruction emphasizing that speed defeats brute force through angles rather than repetitive strikes, warning that Eleanor relies on overwhelming force that punishes attrition strategies. Florence departs to change into grey trousers, a dark shirt, and soft boots suitable for climbing. By 7:22 AM, Rachael dismisses Ryan to prepare for Fiona's medical treatment, with the extraction team scheduled to depart in approximately two hours, entering through the fisherman's path during the supply delivery window with the objective of extracting Mina and Cora before noon.
