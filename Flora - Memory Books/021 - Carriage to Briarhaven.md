# 021 - Carriage to Briarhaven

On the morning of Thursday, June 15, 1843, Ryan and Florence Claymore depart Claymore Manor at approximately 8:40 AM in a traveling carriage driven by Thomas Greaves, bound for Briarhaven to execute the extraction of Mina and Cora from Amber Isles. During the three-hour coastal road journey, Ryan reveals the full scope of Eleanor's intelligence network to Florence, including the identification of Edmund Girard as the mole, the deliberately small carriage dimensions designed to cause Ryan's head injury, the premature filing of the competency petition, and the second courier carrying documents for Cora's sale to Durant. Florence processes this information with controlled intensity, correcting Ryan's characterization of Eleanor as merely stubborn, asserting that Eleanor is calculating and dangerous, and expressing hurt at being excluded from Ryan and Charlotte's earlier tactical discussions. She demands immediate transparency going forward, reiterating the renewed oath of 'no more secrets.'

Ryan produces quill, ink, and parchment to draw a detailed tactical map of the Amber Isles house, revealing the layout: four upstairs rooms (for Mina, Cora, Eleanor, and his former quarters), a living room with stairs descending to the basement, and a secret brick room to the left of the stairs accessed by three presses on the eighth row. Florence memorizes every detail while maintaining physical closeness, her hand resting on Ryan's thigh as the carriage sways.

The conversation shifts to personal revelations. Ryan admits he once aspired to be a lute musician. Florence shares her secret ambition to become a professional wrestler under the persona 'Stardust,' complete with gold and silver costume and mask, proposing Ryan serve as her 'assistant to the executive manager' in a tag team. This leads to escalating physical intimacy; Florence, experiencing bicurious attraction she has never felt for a man before, climbs into Ryan's lap and kisses him with competitive ferocity, grinding against him as the carriage rattles over ruts. She trembles with uncertainty, whispering she has never been with a man and fears Charlotte's reaction, but Ryan encourages her to 'do what you want.'

The moment is shattered as the carriage rounds the final bend into Briarhaven at approximately 9:42 AM. Callista Venn waits on the dock, announcing the boat is ready and the tide is turning. Florence dismounts with unsteady grace, flushed and disheveled, squeezing Ryan's hand hard and reminding him of the extraction plan: 'Left at the bottom of the stairs. Eighth row. Three presses. We bring them home.' The scene ends with the party preparing to board the boat immediately or risk waiting six hours for the next tide.
