# 008 - Curtained Hall Investigation

On Wednesday, June 14, 1843, from approximately 5:45 PM to 6:30 PM in the curtained Main Hall of Claymore Manor, Ryan initiates a private conference with Charlotte to discuss a critical timeline discrepancy regarding his mother Eleanor's foreknowledge of his recent carriage accident. With the fireplace reduced to embers and the windows covered to prevent observation, Ryan points out that Eleanor could not have learned of his head injury and restored memories—nor dispatched a sealed competency petition—within the few hours between the incident and the courier's 4:00 PM arrival. Charlotte confirms his analysis, revealing she examined the petition's wax seal and determined it was pressed at least six hours prior, proving the document was filed before Ryan even departed the Amber Isles that morning.

Together they hunt for the intelligence leak. After considering Howard—Florence's father who arrived unannounced two days prior and specifically probed Ryan's mental state—they identify the carriage driver, Edmund Girard, as the primary suspect. Charlotte confesses that Eleanor provided her a list of three candidates for the position, and she had selected Edmund believing she was exercising due diligence, unaware that Eleanor had orchestrated the choice to place a compromised agent. Ryan theorizes the carriage itself was a weapon: intentionally undersized to guarantee head trauma on rough roads, with a midpoint stop at an unnamed town allowing Edmund to dispatch a pigeon relay reporting the injury to Eleanor before Ryan reached Cassia County.

During this exchange, Ryan flicks Charlotte's forehead to stop her spiraling self-blame, then confesses that his confident demeanor is a defense mechanism trained by his mother to lower others' guards and gauge their intentions. He clarifies that while he may omit information, he does not lie to those he cares about. Charlotte recognizes a kindred spirit—admitting she too perfected the art of strategic omission to survive her own household—and accepts him as a genuine ally, acknowledging they make a good team.

They agree on a counter-intelligence strategy: leave Edmund in place without confrontation, denying Eleanor the satisfaction of seeing them waste resources investigating while they focus on the imminent extraction of Ryan's sisters from the Amber Isles. They also decide to withhold the full truth from Florence temporarily, providing her only a cover story that they discussed extraction logistics and contingency planning, to prevent her from acting on emotion and alerting the mole. As the clock strikes 6:30 PM and dinner approaches, Ryan departs to meet Florence, leaving Charlotte to contemplate their new alliance in the dimming hall.
