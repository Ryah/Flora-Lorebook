# The Sensory Link (Ryan & Charlotte)

A permanent magical bond forged by the goddess [[Culture - The Faith of Althena]] when Charlotte bit Ryan during an intimate moment. The link lets them feel each other's **strong emotions** and **physical sensations** – but only as vague impressions, with no context or details.

<div class="meta-block">
[Category: Divine bond | Scope: Ryan and Charlotte only | Danger: Emotional vulnerability, distraction, pain transmission, unconscious exposure | Who Knows: Ryan, Charlotte, their trusted allies | Triggers: Intense emotion, physical sensation, vessel injury, mutual REM sleep | Consequences: Shared distress, mirrored pain, phantom sensation, dream convergence | Related: [[Althena]], the bite mark, [[Ryan's Divine Power]] | Secrecy: Enemies never know about the bond]
</div>

## Core Rules

- **Only allies know** about the bond. Enemies cannot sense or see it. This is never revealed to enemies.
- **No telepathy** – thoughts, memories, and intentions remain private.
- **No context** – feeling "heat" could be arousal, fever, or standing near a fire.
- **No omnipresence** – the bond does not let either person automatically know what the other is doing.
- **Charlotte has no power of her own** from this bond. Any power she gets comes from Ryan sharing (see [[Ryan's Divine Power (Golden Thread)]]).
- **The "silver thread" does not naturally exist** – it is Ryan's golden thread resonating through the bond.

## How Sharing Works

When Ryan feels something strongly, Charlotte feels a vague echo. When Charlotte feels something strongly, Ryan feels it. The sensation is diffuse – like an impression, not a recording.

**Examples of shared sensations:**
- Frustration → partner feels general agitation.
- Arousal → partner feels rising warmth or restlessness.
- Orgasm → partner feels a radiating wave throughout their body (not a shared climax – just the sensation. All sensation stops right at the point of orgasm, effectively edging the non-participating partner.).
- Pain → partner feels a dull ache in a similar area, doesn't know the cause.
- Calm or soft emotion → partner feels peace or gentleness.

**Pain transmission during projection:** When Ryan is projected (shadow form) and his physical vessel body is injured, Charlotte feels the **full phantom pain** of that injury through the bond – not just a dull ache but the actual sensation. This was tested when Estra made a shallow cut on Ryan's vessel forearm; Charlotte experienced the cut as if it were her own, screaming silently. The bond does not filter pain based on projection state.

## Dream Sharing (Convergence Events)

The bond remains active even when both Ryan and Charlotte are unconscious. If both are asleep at the same time – specifically, both in **REM sleep** – the link can create a **shared dreamscape**. This is called a **convergence event**.

**How it works:**
- Both people must be asleep simultaneously. If one is awake and the other sleeps, only normal sensations and emotions transmit – no dreams or memories.
- During mutual REM sleep, their dreamscapes can merge. Ryan and Charlotte occupy the same dream space and feel each other's presence.
- **What can be shared in a dream:**
  - Feelings of safety or comfort from the other person's presence.
  - Impressions of places from the other person's memories (e.g., Ryan's brick room, shallow water that isn't real).
  - Vague symbolic images (reflections, chains, hands reaching).
- **What cannot be shared in a dream:**
  - Full memories with context. The other person does not receive a complete, detailed memory – only fragmented images and feelings.
  - Inner thoughts or deliberate secrets.
  - Anything that isn't already accessible through the bond's normal emotional transmission.

**Example from experience:** Charlotte dreamed of standing in warm, shallow water with a perfectly still surface. When she looked down, she saw reflections of Ryan's childhood – the brick room, a chain against the wall, a loaf of bread pushed through a door gap, Mina's smaller hand reaching through. She felt safe even though the place was terrible because she felt Ryan's presence in the dream. Ryan, at the same time, had his usual recurring dream of the brick room but felt Charlotte there with him.

**Limitations and unknowns:**
- The bond does not transmit dreams during normal sleep when only one person is unconscious.
- It is currently unknown whether Ryan can enter Charlotte's dreams (only Charlotte has entered his so far).
- It is unknown whether they can deliberately communicate within a shared dream – this has not been tested.
- The bond does not give context to dream images. Charlotte saw fragments but did not automatically understand what the brick room was until Ryan explained it.

## Important Limitation: No Overreaction

Charlotte does **not** react to every small feeling Ryan has. The bond is background noise most of the time. Only serious or intense emotions warrant a visible response. Repetitive reactions ("Charlotte feels Ryan's X") are avoided.

## The "Brick Room"

Ryan visualizes his power using a mental space called **"the brick room"** – a dark, windowless room from his childhood where his mother, Eleanor, tortured him and kept him in chains for eight years. Loren kidnapped Ryan as a child and gave him to Eleanor to mold him into subservience. The brick room is where Ryan learned to survive, and now it's where he goes to control his divine power. It is not a physical place – only a mental construct. The brick room also appears in Ryan's recurring dreams, and during convergence events, Charlotte can see it as a dream location.

## Future Goal: Pain Transmission Toggle

After the vessel injury test, Ryan promised Charlotte he would find a way to suppress or control the sensory bond's pain transmission – possibly asking Althena for a "toggle switch." This has not yet been achieved. Currently, all physical pain experienced by Ryan (especially during projection) transmits fully to Charlotte.

## The Bond Is Still Growing

Even Althena did not anticipate everything the bond can do. It was meant only to share sensation – a comfort for her champion. But Ryan and Charlotte's connection has grown beyond her design. They are **discovering the rules as they go**. Some limits are unknown. Some abilities may emerge unexpectedly – including dream sharing, which was discovered by accident.
