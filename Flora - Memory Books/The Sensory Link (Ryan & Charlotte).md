# The Sensory Link (Ryan & Charlotte)

A permanent magical bond forged by the goddess [[Althena]] when Charlotte bit Ryan during an intimate moment. The link lets them feel each other's **strong emotions** and **physical sensations** – but only as vague impressions, with no context or details.

<div class="meta-block">
[Category: Divine bond | Scope: Ryan and Charlotte only | Danger: Emotional vulnerability, distraction | Who Knows: Ryan, Charlotte, their trusted allies | Triggers: Intense emotion, physical sensation | Consequences: Shared distress, mirrored pain | Related: [[Althena]], the bite mark, [[Ryan's Divine Power]] | Secrecy: Enemies never know about the bond]
</div>

## Core Rules

- **Only allies know** about the bond. Enemies cannot sense or see it. This is never revealed to enemies.
- **No telepathy** – thoughts, memories, and intentions remain private.
- **No context** – feeling "heat" could be arousal, fever, or standing near a fire.
- **No omnipresence** – the bond does not let either person automatically know what the other is doing.
- **Charlotte has no power of her own** from this bond. Any power she gets comes from Ryan sharing (see [[Ryan's Divine Power]]).
- **The "silver thread" does not naturally exist** – it is Ryan's golden thread resonating through the bond.

## How Sharing Works

When Ryan feels something strongly, Charlotte feels a vague echo. When Charlotte feels something strongly, Ryan feels it. The sensation is diffuse – like an impression, not a recording.

**Examples of shared sensations:**
- Frustration → partner feels general agitation.
- Arousal → partner feels rising warmth or restlessness.
- Orgasm → partner feels a radiating wave throughout their body (not a shared climax – just the sensation).
- Pain → partner feels a dull ache in a similar area, doesn't know the cause.
- Calm or soft emotion → partner feels peace or gentleness.

## Important Limitation: No Overreaction

Charlotte does **not** react to every small feeling Ryan has. The bond is background noise most of the time. Only serious or intense emotions warrant a visible response. Repetitive reactions ("Charlotte feels Ryan's X") are avoided.

## The "Brick Room"

Ryan visualizes his power using a mental space called **"the brick room"** – a dark, windowless room from his childhood where his mother, Eleanor, tortured him and kept him in chains for eight years. Loren kidnapped Ryan as a child and gave him to Eleanor to mold him into subservience. The brick room is where Ryan learned to survive, and now it's where he goes to control his divine power. It is not a physical place – only a mental construct.

## The Bond Is Still Growing

Even Althena did not anticipate everything the bond can do. It was meant only to share sensation – a comfort for her champion. But Ryan and Charlotte's connection has grown beyond her design. They are **discovering the rules as they go**. Some limits are unknown. Some abilities may emerge unexpectedly.
