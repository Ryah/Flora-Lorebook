# 045 - Family Truth and Integration

June 16, 1843, late morning to early afternoon, Claymore Manor - East Wing Library. Ryan shares lunch with blood sisters Mina and Cora alongside wives Charlotte and Florence, joking about manufactured happiness and deflecting questions about silk abrasion marks on his wrists. When Ryan asks for sparring feedback, Mina critiques his weak left side and notes his partner moved with assassin-like aggression, questioning why she pulled every strike. Ryan reveals he signed a new triadic marriage contract with reciprocal autonomy clauses extending Article III protections to all parties, ensuring free choice rather than transactional obligation. He announces a wedding ceremony for tomorrow (June 17) to publicly validate their chosen union, presenting the parchment for inspection. Charlotte and Florence swear solemn oaths before Mina and Cora to never chain, break, or compel Ryan, promising daily chosen commitment despite their earlier failures.

Ryan then confesses the truth of his suicide attempt three weeks prior: after intercepting a blade meant for Florence during an assassination attempt, he hid bleeding in a closet for three days, neglecting his wound because he believed the Claymores would betray him to Eleanor. He details how Claire discovered him via blood seeping through floorboards, triggering a swift extraction and medical intervention by Dr. Mercer, followed by integration into the Ragnar-Blakeney protection network. Cora mathematically deduces that the assassin from three weeks ago is identical to Ryan's sparring partner that morning—Estra Hamilton, a Duranian Intelligence operative. Charlotte confirms Estra defects tonight at 8pm in the Sion Quarter, offering intelligence on Loren's network in exchange for rescue of her hostage brother, Alec.

Ryan delegates wedding preparations to his sisters: Mina will coordinate security perimeter defenses with Howard Ragnar, while Cora will manage logistics and guest vetting with Rachael. Despite Charlotte's warning about complications if Ryan chooses to not be married to Charlotte and Florence, Ryan publicly rejects flight, affirming his commitment to remain and fight for his chosen family against the twenty-one day countdown preventing Loren from extracting him.
