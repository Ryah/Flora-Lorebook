# 018 - Fiona's Bandaging

Thursday, June 15, 1843, approximately 7:25-7:34 AM. In Ryan's chambers at Claymore Manor, morning mist presses gray against the windows as Ryan enters to find Fiona, a young servant with copper-brown hair in a tight crown braid, freckled pale skin, and old burn scars across her forearms, preparing medical supplies at the washstand. She wears a grey servant's dress with rolled sleeves and a herb-stained apron. At Lady Rachael's request, Fiona has come to apply fresh salve and bandaging to Ryan's healing knife wound before his departure for the Amber Isles extraction mission.

Fiona explains that the standard wrap will slip during climbing, offering instead her grandmother's figure-eight technique that anchors at the shoulder, crosses the spine, and locks at the hip with three contact points. Ryan removes his shirt and sits, reassuring her of his trust and asking after her sleep. Fiona, surprised by his familiar address and personal inquiry, admits she slept better than she has in weeks, noting that Lady Rachael had acknowledged her salve's effectiveness—validation she rarely receives.

She applies a golden comfrey and calendula salve to the inflamed wound tissue, her scarred fingers working with methodical precision. As she wraps the linen in crossing passes, she adds an extra pad at the deepest point where the tissue still weeps thin fluid. Ryan informs her that Dr. Mercer noted his healing is occurring 40% faster than similar cases, which he attributes to her remedy, validating her grandmother's thirty years of work that previously earned only a parish festival ribbon. Moved by this scientific recognition, Fiona promises to prepare a travel-sized jar for the road.

After testing the bandaging by rotating his torso and confirming the slight pulling on the left side is manageable, Ryan thanks her and expresses happiness that she slept well. He then asks if he may pet her head again for motivation before the dangerous rescue. Fiona becomes visibly flustered, color rising beneath her freckles as her composure cracks. She points out the power imbalance between them—lord and servant—and reminds him that she asked him to name his intentions, which he called friendship. She establishes a clear boundary, telling him to ask again upon his safe return when the house is quiet and he has considered what he actually wants.

Before departing with quick, uneven footsteps, Fiona reveals critical tactical intelligence: she grew up on a tenant farm in Cassia County, a half-hour walk from the Amber Isles ferry crossing. She discloses the existence of a drainage culvert on the north side of the Adame property wall, hidden by blackberry brambles, which she and her brother used to sneak through as children to steal apples. She urges him to bring his sisters home. Alone, Ryan reflects that her mask will eventually crack, dresses, places his locket around his neck, and heads to the drawing room to share the new intelligence and attend the operational briefing.
