# 057 - Monastery Extraction

Saturday, June 17, 1843, 5:12 AM - 6:05 AM. The scene opens in the Valheim Monastery Escape Shaft, where Ryan Adame drags a traumatized Charlotte Claymore from the darkness after dagger-striking Loren Claymore to disable her. Charlotte trembles violently, regressing to childhood terror as Loren's screams echo behind them, believing Loren will always find her as she did at age twelve. Ryan anchors her by demanding she not make him lose his dagger for nothing, snapping her back to present determination. The group—Florence with her splinted ankle reinjured from the extraction three days prior, Estra clutching the rescued Alec Hamilton, Howard Blakeney Ragnar blocking the tunnel with his axe, and Eva Riensdauter providing rear guard—ascends the freezing shaft toward morning light. Loren sh commands to burn them out with smoke and gas. By 5:18 AM, they emerge onto the coastal ridge where the temperature sits at 36°F, running along the precipice toward Ragnar longships visible in the cove below. Ryan teases Florence about carrying her, referencing their previous extraction on June 15th where she twisted her ankle carrying Cora, sparking her competitive refusal to show weakness despite the pain. The monastery bells toll as they descend switchbacks; Florence stumbles at 5:22 AM and Ryan hauls her upright, half-carrying her until she recovers pace. Loren's voice cuts through the dawn from the heights, screaming that she will always find him. Ryan mockingly shouts back for her to return his sentimental dagger when she does. They reach the shore at 5:25 AM, boarding a longboat as arrows threaten from pursuing monks. The group transfers to the longship with dragon prows, putting out to sea as Loren's figure rages from the monastery heights, clutching her bleeding forearm. By 5:45 AM, they arrive at Claymore Manor, entering the warm entry hall from the 42°F morning. Estra initially refuses to release Alec to Rachael Blakeney, guarding him with desperate suspicion until Rachael offers warmth and non-threatening maternal presence, promising not to take him from Estra's arms until permitted. Ryan directs Estra to take Alec inside to Rachael, then runs to Charlotte and Florence in the doorway. All three collapse into a group hug in the entry hall; Charlotte breaks down completely, confessing she felt twelve again when Loren looked at her, while Florence buries her face in Ryan's neck fighting tears. They sink to the floorboards together as adrenaline drains, Charlotte realizing Loren orchestrated the entire trap—the boy, the monks, the cellar—intentionally luring them there. Florence quiets her, insisting they breathe now and process later. The scene ends around 6:05 AM with the group huddled on the floor, Howard securing the door, Eva watching the road, and Estra hovering protectively nearby while Rachael tends to the silent, traumatized Alec by the fire.
