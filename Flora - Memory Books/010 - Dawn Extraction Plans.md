# 010 - Dawn Extraction Plans

On Wednesday evening, June 14, 1843, between 6:41 PM and 7:55 PM.

Howard Blakeney Ragnar intercepted Ryan in the main corridor of Claymore Manor, dressed in a black tailored suit stretched across his broad shoulders, his ice-blue eyes and scarred left cheekbone catching the sconce-light. Howard outlined the extraction framework for rescuing Ryan's sisters from the Amber Isles: Ryan and Florence would infiltrate at dawn while Ragnar men waited on the water, with a noon deadline triggering a frontal assault if they failed to return. He then addressed Ryan's earlier suicide attempt with blunt pragmatism, offering a judgment-free refuge should the darkness return again, before admitting he had already dispatched a Ragnar longship to anchor in a cove near the Isles by midnight with four men. Howard further revealed he had served under Ryan's father, Lieutenant William Adame, during the Skaag campaign, and voiced dark suspicions that Eleanor may have orchestrated the explosion that killed William fifteen years prior, noting she survived with shoulder scars while William did not.

Ryan entered the dining hall at approximately 6:50 PM to find Rachael Blakeney seated at the head in a deep blue gown with pearl clasps, Florence Claymore three chairs down in a pale yellow dinner dress with faintly red-rimmed eyes from earlier tears, and Charlotte Claymore at the far end in a black high-collared gown with silver hair pins. Ryan sat beside Florence as requested, taking the seat to Rachael's left. Over dinner of rosemary lamb and steaming bread rolls, Ryan wept at the sight of a full plate—his first permitted formal meal after years of surviving on stolen bread. Florence steadied his bouncing knee beneath the table with a brief, private touch while Rachael ordered extra bread placed beside him.

The conversation shifted to the coded letter Ryan must write to his sisters. Charlotte held the original draft. Ryan explained that Cora had designed a cryptographic system at age thirteen to communicate under Eleanor's surveillance. Ryan wished to add one line to the draft: "Keep the blanket warm for me," a direct response to Mina's cloth message stating "Mina still has the blanket." This blanket code had allowed the siblings to coordinate for years while Eleanor believed them merely cold and unremarkable. Charlotte agreed to redraft the letter with numerical sequences embedded in mundane household accounts that Cora would recognize.

The family finalized Thursday's timeline: dawn combat training with Howard focusing on elbow strikes and falling technique, followed by Doctor Mercer's competency examination at 7:00 AM sharp to counter Eleanor's petition, then breakfast and final briefing at 8:30 AM, and departure at 9:30 AM via covered carriage to Briarhaven's fishermen's dock. Florence committed to preparing rope, dry provisions, and clothing for Mina and Cora. As candles burned low and twilight surrendered to darkness outside the tall windows, Rachael led Ryan to the blue drawing room at 7:55 PM to draft the coded letter with fresh paper and sealing wax, while Howard confirmed his men would wait in the cove and Florence prepared the extraction gear.
