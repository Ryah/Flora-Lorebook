# 059 - Corridor Vigil

In the early morning hours of Saturday, June 17, 1843, at approximately 6:42 AM in Claymore Manor's East Wing Corridor, Ryan Adame keeps vigil with Estra Hamilton following their successful rescue of her brother Alec from Valheim Monastery. The temperature sits at 60 degrees Fahrenheit with clear weather as dawn light filters through the windows, illuminating Estra's severe exhaustion—her pale blonde curls matted with sweat, amber eyes bloodshot from dromamine withdrawal, and thin camisole stained with tunnel grime and damp from stress. She sits on the oak floorboards with her knife sheathed at her thigh, clutching the sleeping Alec who breathes shallowly and wetly against her neck, his small fingers knotted in the torn fabric of her camisole.

Ryan, gesturing to the fresh scab on his palm from the blood vigil performed the previous night, asks if Alec has spoken. Estra whispers that he has not, stating that his breathing is all that matters now, and that he will talk when ready. She admits she cannot sleep, perhaps not until they leave the island entirely, haunted by the eleven days Loren Claymore held Alec and fearing that if she closes her eyes, Loren will be "in the walls." She confesses her terror that Alec might not remember her, or worse, might mistake her for Loren upon waking.

Ryan reassures her by observing that Alec hasn't released her shirt since they escaped the monastery, indicating he recognizes her as safety and trust. He relates Estra's trauma to Cora's rescue from the brick room months prior, describing how Cora was malnourished, unresponsive, and speaking only in code, requiring Florence to physically carry her to the boat. He explains that he remained an emotional "anchor" and "rock" for Cora during the crisis, suppressing his own tears and rage until the mission concluded and he could break down privately with Charlotte and Florence. He notes that Rachael's maternal persistence and relentless care eventually convinced Cora of the Manor's safety, observing that the women are "spoiled now" with affection.

Across the hall, Rachael Blakeney sits in a rocking chair with bone needles clicking, knitting yarn the color of sunset while humming lullabies and watching over Ryan's sleeping wives Mina and Cora. When Estra admits she only knows "missions," "extraction protocols," and "dead drops" and doesn't understand how to be soft or safe, keeping her shoulders rigid and waiting for "the other boot to drop," Rachael intervenes with gentle maternal authority. She orders Estra to relinquish Alec to her arms and rest, promising she will guard him with her life and that Estra can sleep with her knife. After initial resistance, Estra finally hands the boy over, stipulating she will sleep for only one hour.

Ryan then approaches Estra slowly, telegraphing every movement to avoid startling her, before embracing her. He credits her with saving his life during the monastery ambush, referencing how she knocked down an attacker and their shared karambit training from the previous night. He reveals that he spoke in his sisters cipher in the cellar when Loren held a knife to Alec specifically so Estra would understand and be ready to strike. He insists she has earned her place as family, declaring they are "bound by blood" from the vigil scarring on their palms and that she successfully led the expedition by taking point.

Estra crumbles against him, her professional spy facade shattering as she reveals she led them into a trap and fears she could have gotten them all killed. She admits she has never had an "anchor" before—only missions—and doesn't know how to accept care, confessing she is afraid that if she starts crying she "won't stop until I'm hollow." Ryan guides her head to his shoulder, promising to remain sitting in the corridor until she wakes regardless of his family's expectations, dismissing "wives and sisters be damned." Estra finally surrenders to exhaustion, sliding down the plaster wall beside him, her body going slack against his side, her hand lax in his scarred palm, and her thigh pressing against his through the wool runner. She sleeps with her cheek on his shoulder, twitching occasionally in her dreams while Rachael keeps watch over both sleeping children and the Manor's morning fires begin to stir below.
