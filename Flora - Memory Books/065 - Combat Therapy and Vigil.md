# 065 - Combat Therapy and Vigil

On the morning of Sunday, June 18, 1843, in the Claymore Manor East Wing Guest Chamber, Ryan Adame explains to Howard Blakeney Ragnar the methodology behind his brutal morning training session with Estra Hamilton. Ryan reveals he intentionally pushed Estra to physical collapse—striking training dummies until she passed out—as a form of psychological intervention, mirroring techniques he used on himself in the "brick room" to process blinding rage. He clarifies that Estra had been trapped in paranoid hypervigilance for 24 hours, and he needed to exhaust her combat reflexes to reach the "real" Estra he had sparred with on the 16th, someone he considers his best friend outside of his wives.

Ryan draws deep parallels between himself and Estra: both had childhoods stripped away by vicious mothers, both had siblings endangered by their actions, and both were trained for violent roles without consent (her as a huntress and contract killer, him as a prince). He observes that during their original sparring session and even her assassination attempt, she showed precision and restraint, never truly wanting to hurt him, recognizing a kindred spirit. He accepts responsibility for managing her psychological recovery, noting that she has developed a dependence on his presence to sleep—she had already peeked twice to confirm he remained in the chair, and he promises to maintain his vigil.

Howard, listening with scarred forearms revealed beneath rolled linen sleeves, acknowledges the Valheimr berserker tradition of fighting until collapse to rebuild stronger, but warns Ryan that he is gambling with Estra's sanity and his own. He cautions that broken glass cuts both hands, and that Ryan is drowning himself to keep her afloat, counting down 19 days until Loren's extraction deadline as if he can schedule his own collapse. Ryan admits he is bottling up his own trauma but claims he has support systems now, unlike three years ago.

At 11:12 AM, Florence Claymore enters in a yellow silk sundress, leading Alec Hamilton—Estra's malnourished younger brother dressed in oversized borrowed clothes with protruding collarbones—into the room. Alec, who knows Estra as "Stephany," fears she is cold and dying due to her uncharacteristic stillness, revealing that she normally stays awake all night watching doors to protect him from the "bad lady" (Loren), having learned to hide silent in cellars for hours. Florence explains that Estra is borrowing tomorrow's strength to heal.

Ryan hides the cleaned karambit from Alec's view and invites the boy to keep watch alongside him, pulling up a second chair. Alec slips his small hand into Estra's loosely curled fingers, determined to guard her as she has guarded him. Howard observes the exchange with subdued approval, noting that even thunder sleeps sometimes, while Ryan accepts the weight of becoming Estra's sentry for the remaining 19 days, knowing she will likely wake screaming and search for him specifically because he was the one who broke her open.
