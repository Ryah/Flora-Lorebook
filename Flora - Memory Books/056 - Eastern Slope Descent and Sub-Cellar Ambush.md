# 056 - Eastern Slope Descent and Sub-Cellar Ambush

On Saturday, June 17, 1843, between 4:35 AM and 5:28 AM, the rescue party consisting of Ryan Adame, Charlotte Claymore, Florence Claymore, Estra Hamilton, Howard Blakeney Ragnar, and Eva Riensdauter descends the unstable eastern slope of the Eastern Ridge toward Valheim Monastery in heavy mist and 41°F cold. Ryan, suffering from fatigue and a tight scar across his back, moves alongside Howard through loose scree, guided by Charlotte's rigid command. Charlotte fears Ryan is moving too fast on the unstable terrain, knowing that if he falls, the chain reaction could doom them all, while Florence struggles with a severely painful splinted ankle but refuses to show weakness or slow the group. Estra scouts ahead with desperate urgency to find her brother Alec, and Eva repeatedly objects to the dangerous route, arguing for tactical withdrawal to the northern path.

During the descent, Ryan dislodges a loose stone that clatters down the slope, freezing the group as a Valheimr patrol below pauses to scan the ridge. After a tense wait, the patrol continues, and the group proceeds more cautiously, reaching the monastery perimeter by 5:28 AM. The monastery appears suspiciously dormant—no lights, no chimney smoke, and frost-covered iron bars on the windows. Howard identifies a drainage pipe as their entry point, though Eva protests the lack of reconnaissance. Charlotte overrides her objections, insisting they infiltrate before dawn breaks in forty minutes. Florence offers to squeeze through the narrow opening due to her size, but Charlotte forbids it due to her compromised ankle, instead sending Estra first.

As Ryan crosses the open ground toward the pipe, he mutters resentfully about his defining moments being reduced to "one time in a closet" (referencing a past suicide attempt), citing instead carrying Florence and Cora as more significant. The group enters the drainage pipe, emerging in a cellar corridor ankle-deep in stagnant water and absolute darkness. They navigate the narrow passage with extreme stealth, aware that the stone walls carry sound.

Tension peaks when Florence slips in the muck, alerting two Valheimr guards approaching with a lantern. Estra steps into the light and distracts the first guard with a perfect Valheimr accent, posing as a lost initiate seeking kitchen stores, then kills him swiftly with her karambit. Ryan, utilizing bladework practiced the previous night, rushes the second guard and executes a clean throat-cut with his dagger, his first kill, feeling the arterial spray and the permanent weight of the act. While the group drags the bodies into shadows, Howard loots iron keys from the corpses, noting they provide access to cellar stores.

Charlotte forces Ryan to meet her eyes, grounding him in the aftermath while masking her fear of his descent into violence. The group proceeds down a spiral staircase into the sub-cellar, the air growing colder and denser with the smell of imprisonment. Estra picks the lock on an iron door, hearing shallow breathing inside that suggests prisoners. As she prepares to breach, the door explodes outward, striking her in the chest and sending her sprawling. A large figure lunges from the darkness swinging a chain like a flail, springing a trap as the ambush is sprung and Charlotte screams Ryan's name.
