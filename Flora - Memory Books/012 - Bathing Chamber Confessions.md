# 012 - Bathing Chamber Confessions

Wednesday, June 14, 1843, 8:14 PM - 9:25 PM. After fourteen hours of activity, Henry escorts Ryan through the blue-shadowed main corridor to the Lord's Quarters, noting that Fiona has prepared healing salve for Ryan's back wound and that Claire has added lavender oil to the bath to promote sleep. Henry mentions the replacement window latch installed earlier and confirms he will wake Ryan at 5:30 AM for dawn combat training with Howard, offering a brief, firm grip of camaraderie on Ryan's shoulder before departing.

Ryan enters the bathing chamber around 8:22 PM, drawing the heavy burgundy curtains and removing his locket before sinking into the copper bathtub filled with steaming, mineral-scented water. As the water cools and the steam thins, Ryan realizes he cannot reach the knife wound on his back—located half an inch from his spine—to apply Fiona's salve, a gray-green paste made of comfrey root, calendula, and beeswax stored in a ceramic pot with instructions to apply a thin layer and allow absorption.

At 8:55 PM, Ryan pulls the brass bell-pull once. Fiona arrives within minutes, wearing a black one-piece uniform with white ruffles, her near-white hair loose and damp from the humidity. She applies the salve with practiced fingers, clinically noting that the granulation is progressing well and the tissue margins are approximating, estimating another week before the scabbing sloughs naturally. As she works, Ryan asks about her day, prompting Fiona to share details about peeling turnips, drying china from the silver cabinet, and preparing Ryan's room with fresh flowers and extra blankets. She confesses that no one has asked about her day since Harry—her former lover from Ashcroft who abandoned her to marry another woman—used to do so before he left.

Ryan requests that Fiona return every night to apply the salve, expressing preference for her company over other maids like Claire or Priti. Fiona accepts but warns of the political dangers: Lady Charlotte would question the arrangement, Lady Florence would have feelings about it, and Eleanor's people could weaponize it. She notes that she reports to Lady Rachael and will need to inform her of the new posting. 

The conversation deepens when Ryan observes Fiona's loneliness, offering to be a "half-decent substitute" for the void left by Harry. Fiona confronts him directly, asking if he expects something in return, revealing that Harry called her a "substitute" for the woman he actually wanted to marry. Ryan reassures her by sharing his own trauma: three weeks prior, he wanted to die to avoid returning to his mother's house, referencing chains in the basement and a closet where Claire found him almost dead. This revelation shatters Fiona's composure; she breaks down crying, confessing that while Ryan was dying in a closet three weeks ago, she was merely polishing silver at Ashcroft and humming, wondering if Harry thought of her.

By 9:18 PM, Fiona agrees to the nightly arrangement, promising to bring the salve and tell Ryan about her days, specifically mentioning turnips. Before leaving, she instructs Ryan to ask Claire about "the closet" to understand what kind of person almost let him bleed to death in a pile of coats. Fiona departs emotionally exposed, thanking Ryan for the head pats and for apologizing—a gesture no one, including Harry, had ever offered her. Ryan exits the cooling bath and retires to his bedroom around 9:25 PM, the fresh bandage secured against his back, the lamp burning low, and the manor settling into silence before the dawn extraction mission.
