# 131 - Carried & Crows
On Thursday, June 22, 1843, at approximately 6:19 AM in the East Kitchen Courtyard of the Palace of Loth Logan, Ryan Adame suddenly stood, walked over to Charlotte, lifted her in a bear hug, then walked backwards and collapsed onto the grass, ensuring she landed fully on him to avoid injury. Charlotte, caught off guard, made an uncharacteristic sound between a gasp and a protest, clutching his shoulders. She landed on his chest, her hair coming loose, and lay frozen for three seconds, her heart racing. Through the bond, Ryan felt shock followed by embarrassment mixed with relief. Florence scrambled over, laughing and crying, saying Charlotte's face was betrayed. Mina flatly noted Charlotte 'squeaked,' which Charlotte denied as a 'protest sound.' Florence pointed out Ryan made sure Charlotte never touched the grass. Charlotte, still on Ryan's chest, called him a deeply ridiculous man and stated she was not moving. Ryan kept one arm around Charlotte and grabbed Florence's hand, joking he needed a few minutes to catch his breath. Florence shifted to tuck against his side, questioning that he was winded from carrying Charlotte. Mina observed Charlotte still hadn't moved and was 'broken.' Charlotte's hand tightened on Ryan's waistcoat in amusement.

Ryan then mentioned he had now carried all his wives: Florence and Cora when fleeing Eleanor, Estra after she proposed, and now Charlotte. He added that the cloud earlier looked more like a turtle than a rabbit, rekindling the earlier argument. Florence asked if being the last carried bothered Charlotte. Charlotte defended herself as 'strategically relocated' rather than carried, claiming she was calculating weight distribution. Mina pointed out Charlotte didn't struggle. Ryan teased Charlotte that Estra at least pretended dignity and pounded his shoulder calling him a brute. Florence explained Estra's hitting is a sign of affection. Mina noted Duranians don't propose by stabbing. Florence said that just foreplay. Charlotte made a near-laugh.

Looking at birds, Ryan wondered if he could make birds land on him, imagining being encased in crows. Florence enthusiastically embraced the idea of a 'crow king' delivering messages. Charlotte dryly noted crows would steal shiny objects rather than deliver mail. Mina gave a tactical assessment about visibility to archers, though crows would hinder aim. Ryan asked about Althena's current location, mentioning they never told her which bathtub to use. Charlotte reported she was last seen in the west wing bathing chamber eight hours ago, though she could be anywhere, and noted the promised royal suite bathtub might cause the Queen to have opinions. Florence suggested warning the Queen. Mina said not warning her would be 'educational.' Charlotte argued against surprising the Queen, citing seventeen pages of documentation minimum.

Florence reminded everyone that Ryan called God 'hot' with 'nice tits' during the bath. Mina reacted with flat disbelief. Charlotte confirmed the summoning invocation was 'Big titty manifestation of all things holy, show yourself' and the goddess appeared immediately. She revised her understanding of divine communication protocols, concluding flattery with confidence works. Florence declared they should write down the divine summoning protocol: 'Step one, compliment the tits.' Mina tried not to laugh and gave up, saying the family would give her a headache. Florence said it was part of their charm. The group remained in the courtyard, the sky fully blue and birds singing, with Mina's smallest finger brushing Ryan's on the grass—she did not pull away.
