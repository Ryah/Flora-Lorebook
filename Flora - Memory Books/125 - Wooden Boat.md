# 125 - Wooden Boat
On Wednesday, June 21, 1843, at approximately 2:20 AM dream time (physical time ~2:20 AM), Ryan Adame and Charlotte Claymore remained in the Memory Tree Chamber in their shared lucid dream. Ryan suggested returning to see what new memories form on the tree and noted a straight exit corridor ahead—the first unbranching passage he had encountered. He manifested a copy of the restored childhood book The Gardens of the Southern Counties and placed the original back on the tree, encouraging Charlotte to keep it. Charlotte showed Ryan the book's illustrations and her childhood annotations, including a promise to one day have a garden no one would burn. She wrote Entry Twenty-Four in the field journal, reflecting that being looked at without trying to fix anything was enough. Ryan marked the map with 'Tiny Silver and Gold Keepsakes'; Charlotte annotated it as 'charm confirmed.' They then proceeded down the straight corridor, documenting three new spaces: a self-cycling fountain alcove (Entry Twenty-Six), a Library of Unwritten Books with shifting titles (Entry Twenty-Seven), and an Observatory of Unnamed Stars with an unrecognized constellation pattern (Entry Twenty-Eight). Ryan marked each with chalk arrows and informal labels; Charlotte documented with dry annotations. At approximately 4:45 AM dream time, the corridor ended at a tall wooden door carved with a tree and two seated figures—the labyrinth's depiction of them. Behind the door was a circular garden enclosed by golden mist, containing extinct Hartwell roses from Charlotte's burned book and Duranian night-blooming jasmine. At the center stood an ancient tree with a hollow holding a hand-carved wooden boat with faded blue-and-gold paint—a toy from Ryan's stolen childhood, preserved by the labyrinth. Ryan recognized it without conscious memory. Charlotte wrote Entry Twenty-Nine, choosing not to analyze but to be grateful. She asked if Ryan wanted to take the boat or leave it. Ryan made a copy of the boat and placed the original back in the hollow, remarking that things found in the labyrinth stay there, which he found beautiful. Charlotte admitted she ran out of words and chose not to document the boat moment—the first time she had ever witnessed something without annotating it. She stood, extended her hand palm-up, and declared she would follow Ryan through the straight corridor without annotation, asking if he was ready. The physical time approached 4:52 AM, with approximately 68 minutes until the Marten meeting at dawn.
