# 026 - William's Letter Truth

Thursday, June 15, 1843, approximately 11:15-11:45 AM: Below deck of the Ragnar vessel en route to Cassia County following the successful extraction of Mina and Cora from Amber Isles. The atmosphere is heavy with exhaustion—nine hours since rest—and physical injury: Ryan's back wound bleeds through saturated bandages from knife damage reopened during the climb, Florence's left ankle is swollen purple-black from a sprain, and Cora remains malnourished and fragile.

The scene opens with Ryan removing his blood-crusted shirt for medical treatment, the coarse fibers pulling against his torn flesh. Callista Venn prepares medical supplies while Florence, despite her own injury, commands Ryan to sit on a low bench and coaches him through breathing exercises she learned from him. As Callista pours alcohol over the wound—liquid fire that makes Ryan's vision swim—Florence grips his hand, her fingers cold but firm, grounding him through the pain.

While the treatment begins, Cora explores the medical storage area, drawn by the scent of cedar and old paper rather than medicine. She discovers a weathered oak sea chest wedged behind supply crates, banded with iron and bearing the initials "W.A."—William Adame. With Mina's hesitant assistance, Cora opens the rusted catch to reveal oilcloth-wrapped contents: a water-stained captain's log, a brass compass, and a bundle of letters tied with faded ribbon.

Cora extracts the topmost letter addressed to "Ryan and Mina" in their father's handwriting. The letter—dated before his death—reveals William was planning to escape with them to Duran aboard the merchant vessel Dawn Star, securing passage for midnight departure. He had arranged custody with a Duran solicitor, knowing Flora's courts would never grant divorce, and instructed them to meet at the old fishing dock at quarter past eleven. Crucially, William acknowledges seeing marks on Ryan (evidence of Eleanor's abuse) and apologizes for waiting so long, declaring "I was afraid. No longer."

The revelation shatters the siblings' understanding of their childhood. Mina realizes the explosion that killed William may not have been accidental—Eleanor returned with burns on her shoulders, suggesting she may have murdered him to prevent the escape and keep the children under her control. Cora clutches the compass as proof their father wanted them, repeating "He wanted us" through tears. Florence reads the letter with shaking hands and tear-filled eyes, recognizing that Eleanor deliberately let the children believe their father abandoned them when he was actually trying to save them from her abuse.

Callista interrupts the emotional devastation to stitch Ryan's wound—five stitches of catgut thread through ragged tissue. Florence holds Ryan's face, commanding him to look only at her while Mina grips his hand hard enough to shift bones. The stitching hits nerves, causing Ryan's vision to white out, but Florence anchors him with fierce whispers and physical pressure, refusing to let him endure the pain alone.

After the medical crisis passes, Ryan entrusts his precious silver locket—containing photographs and worn from years of secret clutching—to Florence for safekeeping. She tucks it into her inner pocket against her heart, promising "Nothing will happen to this... Not while I'm breathing." Ryan attempts to break the tension by joking that Florence got off easy with just a splint compared to his stitches, prompting her to request Callista's assistance with her ankle.

Callista applies wooden splints and linen bandages to Florence's swollen ankle while Ryan rests his head on Florence's shoulder. As exhaustion finally overwhelms him—the adrenaline of the mission wearing off—Ryan drifts into sleep dreaming of his new family: Florence, Charlotte, Mina, and Cora together. He sleeps through the gentle rocking of the vessel until awakened as they approach the final stretch of their journey home to Claymore Manor, where a post-extraction family meeting awaits.
