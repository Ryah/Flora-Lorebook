# 067 - Recovery Luncheon Wager

On Sunday, June 18, 1843, between 12:40 PM and 1:15 PM, the group gathers in the Claymore Manor Dining Room for a recovery luncheon following the extraction of Alec Hamilton. The afternoon is clear and warm at 76°F, with sunlight streaming through tall windows onto a white tablecloth set with silver tureens of venison stew rich with rosemary and red wine, melting butter sculptures, honey cake, and bread baskets. Ryan Adame whispers to Estra Hamilton that he calls Rachael Blakeney "mama" and that she belongs to all of them as family, causing Estra's breath to catch as she accepts inclusion despite her previous attempt to kill Florence. Rachael, wearing a soft peach afternoon gown, welcomes the "scattered little flock" with open arms, insisting there's always room for more chicks under her wing. Howard Blakeney Ragnar adjusts Alec's chair with a thick cushion so the small boy can see out the window, displaying gruff paternal care while monitoring the child's trauma responses. Florence Claymore, despite her wrapped ankle, claims the window seat and declares she will eat two rolls before any serious discussion, initiating a competitive dynamic with Estra. As the group settles, Ryan leans toward Charlotte Claymore and whispers that Howard took the blood sister inclusion to heart, joking that adoption papers will be submitted within two days. Charlotte murmurs back that Howard would sooner declare them Ragnar by blood oath than deal with solicitors, and Howard overhears, sliding torn bread toward Alec while grunting that the boy needs meat not legalities. The competitive eating escalates when Ryan announces he has ten gold pieces on Estra winning against Florence, citing Estra's days without food against Florence's breakfast advantage. Florence reacts with mock outrage at the betrayal, while Estra's competitive pride ignites, her bandaged hand flexing as she accepts the challenge. Rachael adds five gold from a velvet pouch retrieved from her décolletage, siding with the "Andie's Cross girl." Alec Hamilton solemnly offers his two coppers earned from shoe polishing to bet on his sister, causing Estra to turn toward the window with emotion before promising to turn them into gold for him. The stakes escalate further: if Florence wins, Ryan sleeps alone and Estra mucks the stables for a week; if Estra wins, Florence must publicly admit Estra is the better fighter while standing on her chair at dinner. Charlotte officiates with house rules—no vomiting, no cheating, and Florence must keep her ankle elevated—while maintaining physical contact with Ryan beneath the table, her hand on his knee and her thumb circling his palm as he feels genuinely safe. The competition begins in earnest with Florence tearing into drumsticks and rolls with desperate intensity while Estra eats methodically, citing her experience surviving three days on a cellar floor with only rainwater, matching Florence bite for bite as the room fills with chaotic laughter and the clink of gold coins sitting heavy on the tablecloth.
