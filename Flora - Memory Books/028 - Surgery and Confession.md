# 028 - Surgery and Confession

Thursday, June 15, 1843, 4:45 PM to 5:25 PM. Following a dangerous extraction operation, Ryan returns to Claymore Manor bleeding profusely from a reopened back wound sustained during a cliff climb and boat transport. Rachael Blakeney intercepts the group in the entrance hall, her immediate medical concern overriding any celebration as she notes blood seeping through Ryan's shirt. Despite suffering from a freshly splinted ankle and exhaustion, Florence Claymore refuses to leave Ryan's side, forming a supportive chain with her mother as they guide him to the east wing surgery.

Within the surgery, Dr. Thaddeus Mercer prepares his instruments with grim efficiency, confirming that stitches placed by Callista during the extraction have completely reopened. As Ryan settles onto the leather examination table, the severity of his condition becomes apparent—blood loss has rendered him pale and unsteady, the copper scent of bleeding mixing with beeswax polish. Mercer begins the painful process of restitching torn tissue with catgut, Rachael applying pressure above the wound while Florence anchors Ryan's hand, offering herself as a physical tether through the agony.

The procedure proves too taxing, and Ryan loses consciousness midway through, his hand going limp. Panic erupts as Florence screams his name and attempts to surge forward despite her injury, while Rachael restrains her with iron command. Mercer continues his relentless stitching—five rows deep into muscle and skin—while Rachael administers smelling salts and whispers prayers to Althena. Florence's desperate grip and tearful pleas serve as Ryan's anchor as the darkness pulls him under.

Ryan awakens twenty minutes later to find Florence holding his hand with possessive desperation. He jokes about not repeating a previous three-day unconscious stint, then references Florence's wrestling persona "Stardust," specifically requesting to see the gold and silver bodysuit and her signature shooting star press from the top rope once recovered. Florence, overwhelmed with relief, accepts but extracts a promise that he will heal properly first.

The conversation shifts to intimate revelations regarding "what happened in the carriage" during the extraction—a moment of near-death intimacy where they acknowledged their feelings. Ryan reassures Florence that he carries no guilt for bearing her weight during the escape, declaring he will never leave her or Charlotte behind. Florence confesses that their kiss felt "right" despite her fear, and reveals Charlotte waits anxiously outside, pacing with uncharacteristic concern.

As oil lamps gutter and lavender oil mingles with medicinal alcohol, the couple retreats toward Ryan's quarters to bathe and change before the evening assembly, where Charlotte plans to expose a mole and Rachael intends to strategize against Ryan's mother. Supporting each other—Florence leaning on Ryan while he braces against her shoulder—they make their way down the corridor, Florence's splinted ankle dragging slightly, choosing to remain in this private moment of vulnerability before facing the household's political dangers.
