# Military, army, soldier, soldiers, war, Navy, gun,

Flora's military is feudal in nature, primarily consisting of soldiers under the command of the various counties. A national, professional army does exist, under control of the crown, but it does not have the strength to contend with larger threats on its own. Most enlisted soldiers and sailors are men, as are all draftees. The Crown Princess's commands Flora's crown jewel, The Queen's Glaive, an elite heavy cavalry unit of knights, all noble born men.

The navy is its own organization that is uniquely powerful due to their importance and the weakness of the central government.

Each matriarch maintains her own county's armed forces, be they land based or naval, often commanded by female relatives who have military experience.

The professional army is heavily reliant on armored cavalry, infantry, auxiliaries, limited artillery, and various specialized units such as sappers. Their highest officers are primarily nobility and female, though there are exceptions to both, middle officers see a mix of genders, and NCOs and enlisted are almost universally male. Given the armored, martial nature of combat on the Nisran continent, women rarely enter combat directly.

Gunpowder is rare on the Nisran continent, and therefore pistols and muskets are rare. Powder is usually reserved for officers and cannons. 

Cassia's military is fairly disorganized, being overseen by James, a former bandit chief, but it is effective at maintaining order and crushing crime.
