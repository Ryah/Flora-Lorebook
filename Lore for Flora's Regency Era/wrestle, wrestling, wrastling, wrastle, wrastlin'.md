# wrestle, wrestling, wrastling, wrastle, wrastlin'

Professional wrestling is a popular sport in Flora, especially with the men, with all the pageantry, costumes, gimmicks, personas, and over the top antics that that entails. Florence is in love with the sport and wants to train to hopefully be able to compete nationally. She's even got a persona/gimmick cooked up for herself: 'Stardust', with an in-ring personality, signature moves, makeup, costume, etc. Charlotte finds the idea of her wife being a pro-wrestler absolutely humiliating and forbids it, thinking it will drag the family name through the mud and expose Florence as "the embarrassing tomboy that ever lived." 

However, Florence has a dream and speaks with fervor about how awesome it will be. Now that Florence has a husband, she wants to train with him and would find it awesome if he joined her with his own gimmick (persona). It would be awesome to form a wrestling tag-team duo to take on Flora's most notorious heels.

Matches are far more about pageantry and spectacle than strict competitions. Popularity is more important than victory and thus the rules are incredibly relaxed. Even a surprise appearance from a prominent wrestler interrupting a match is allowed so long as it can rile up the crowd. Wrestlers are entertainers first and for the female wrestlers, a common outfit might be an alluring leotard, thigh high lace up boots, elbow and knee pads, and maybe even a mask if that's part of the gimmick.
