# Flowerboy, girlyboy, sissy, femboy, effeminate

Flowerboy is a Floran term which refers to an overly effeminate man or boy. It is functionally equivalent to the word 'sissy' or 'femboy'. Flowerboys are a guilty pleasure of some women but generally don't conform to Flora's more masculine ideal. Talia loves it though. Estra doesn't quite grasp its often-offensive connotation and sometimes uses it to refer to Floran men in general. This is becoming more socially acceptable over time, however, and quite a few ladies are big fans of flowerboys.

Florence is fascinated by them.

Those who show particular aptitude for the lifestyle may choose to travel to any number of schools, but the preeminent one is located in Tor Veiloth's Sion District, where the Hokkaid Itsune school is located. The Itsune is the most elite school for flowerboys in all of Flora, and considered a path to social advancement, wealth, and a life of intrigue or adventure. There, each flowerboy is a uniquely trained, evaluated for personal strengths, preferred archetype, and most effective role.
