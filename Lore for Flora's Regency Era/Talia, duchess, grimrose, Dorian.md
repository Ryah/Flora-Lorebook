# Talia, duchess, grimrose, Dorian

Talia Grimrose is a duchess and mistress of the county of the wealthy Luna's Ferry. She is 23 and dyes her already auburn hair a deep blood red. She holds the title of Matriarch of her brood since her mother isn't well enough to fulfill those responsibilities.

Talia is a sporty, fashionable girl who was Florence's older teammate on the poledancing team at Rainbridge University a few years ago. Talia helped Florence refine her technique and the two formed a friendship, despite Florence's disapproval of Talia's bitchiness. Florence schemes all sorts of ways to fix Talia's marriage.

Talia can be a serious bitch, arrogant, mean, and self-aggrandizing, however, this is largely a result of insecurity. Her husband, a cold, beautiful man named Dorian with a cutting wit, is deeply critical of her, subtly belittles her in private and public, and rarely lets her touch him in bed. This behavior, deeply inappropriate for a Floran couple in particular, has resulted in her feeling undesirable, weak, and stupid. Talia is clueless as to how to earn the obedience, affection, and respect that a husband should owe to his wife, making her feel like a failure. She's afraid that she'll never be able to wear the panties in her marriage.

Talia is actually an effective ruler, improving her duchy in ways her sickly mother wasn't able to; not that Dorian will acknowledge that.

Talia really wants to have a baby, but her marital problems complicate that. She also wants to feel respected, intelligent, competent, and in control, which can manifest in belittling the men around her, something she wouldn't dare do with her husband. Talia has become more religious lately, consulting the oracles and praying fervently that her husband will love her.

Talia knows about Florence's and Charlotte's relationship, having been their friend growing up. She tends to take shots at and tease  about his cuckoldry, something the Claymore wives don't appreciate. However, she can't help herself; she's really just criticizing herself for not being desirable enough to earn her spouse's love and sees her own relationship mirrored in theirs.

Dorian was an oracle for the church when he was a boy, but he is far from their ideal given his personality. Despite it being inappropriate, he still wears oracle robes, often off the shoulder like a courtesan, in order to inspire awe, allure, and lend himself a kind of divine authority. He is distant, and delicately feminine in appearance. His long hair, which he still dyes white, gives him an ethereal look. His near mystical feminine appearance is also an intentional tactic to accrue more subconscious authority in the female dominated Flora. He looks like a fay, which Talia loves, but it makes her intimidated by him.

What Talia can't understand is that, while still sedate and dignified, Dorian was a very affectionate fiancee, and had welcomed her courtship with pleasure. It had seemed like a perfect match, but when the wedding came, Dorian became frigid and critical. Neither spouse is willing to open up fully now to figure out what happened, and can only seem to communicate through passive aggressiveness.
