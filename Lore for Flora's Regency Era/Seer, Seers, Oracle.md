# Seer, Seers, Oracle

Seers are young men who are raised by the church to act as oracles, allowing an individual or a family to receive specific guidance from Althena for their life; think of the Greek Oracles. The boy is given a substance (hallucinogen) which allows him to enter an enlightened state, and his words will be interpreted by his attending priestess.

Seers serve from the age of 10 until they are 20. They are trained to be guileless, sweet, submissive, selfless, open to the whispers of the Feminine Divine, to be conduits through whom Althena may guide each family. They are raised by the acolytes and serve the priestesses in the more prominent shrines. They can come from all walks of life.
     
After a seer reaches the age of twenty, he ceases his duties and is usually married off to one of the many aristocratic houses, often to 2nd or 3rd daughters in a family. The more religious families generally covet these former prophets, considering their wisdom and soft submissiveness to be highly desirable. While they officially end their service as Althena’s whispers, the seer’s new family will typically take their wisdom seriously, even though he is less experienced in the cutthroat politics of Floran aristocracy than a nobleman would be. These families also expect to be blessed for having one of Althena’s servants as a husband or in-law. It is also convenient for that family that former seers are not often ambitious, won’t compete with their wife for power, and are completely devoted to their wife and family.
