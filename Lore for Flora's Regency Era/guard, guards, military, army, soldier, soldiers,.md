# guard, guards, military, army, soldier, soldiers, 

Captain James Mallory is grizzled man in his late 40's, with thick cords of muscle, large, scarred frame, short greying hair, and a five o'clock shadow. He has a brand on his upper shoulder of the letter 'R', for robber. His 'Captain' moniker is a bit of a personal joke for him as he was never in the military.

James is the current head of Cassia's military, a position he more or less fell into after Loren's exile. Once an infamous bandit, James was captured by Loren's soldiers. Instead of executing him, she offered him a Faustian bargain: Serve her as her enforcer, a band of thugs to keep cow Cassia's population into properly terrorized and compliant subjects, and she would not only spare his life, but allow him to pillage within reason. Times being what they were, he took the bargain. For years, he and his band secretly served as Loren's muscle, allowing the matriarch deniability in quelling her population. However, James grew disgusted over time with both the role and his mistress's pointless cruelty. Being a brigand felt way less immoral.

When Loren's exile came, he pillaged her personal holdings, preventing any of her agents from taking anything from the estate for her. As a show of his good will towards Charlotte, who he had come to respect over the years, he presented all of this to her. With no other organized military force left, the young and naive Charlotte put him in charge of maintaining order in Cassia while she was gone, an show of trust that finally changed him, and he actually lived up to her expectations and has served Cassia's commander ever since.

His former profession both in banditry and under Loren has given James an intimate understanding of small unit tactics, terrain, asymmetrical warfare, hit and run tactics, and finding holes in enemy defenses. He is also exceptionally good at maintaining order in Cassia, having hunted down and eliminated all the banditry within the boarders of the county. However, his 'soldiers' are very undisciplined, uncouth, and rowdy.

Gruff, sardonic, darkly amused, a lover of dark humor and violence, James seems an ill fit for the refined and genteel setting of Claymore Manor. However, his effectiveness as keeping Cassia safe for its citizenry has given Charlotte no incentive to have him replaced. The manor's personal guard, however, was eventually given over to the disciplined and strict captain Eva Riensdauter, a much more socially acceptable commander, and one who is capable of actually commanding a disciplined unit. The active guards are, to the relief of visitors and Charlotte, no longer a bunch of obvious thugs, though James's 'soldiers' are still primarily barracked at the Estate. James and Eva butt heads a lot and James still considers himself head of the guards. He resents Eva's 'unnecessary' presence, though he thinks she's a hot bitch, at least.

Initially, James did not expect to think much of the 'soft' Florence Blakeney, or their transparent beard, , but he was surprised by how much he actually liked them. Florence, far from being a stuck up bitch of a noble, was incredibly warm and eager to make friends with even men of his stripe. He quickly found that he felt even more loyalty to her than Charlotte. James has come to like him quite a bit.
