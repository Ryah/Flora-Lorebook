# Rainbridge, college, university, education, educat

Rainbridge is the preeminent university in Flora, near the capital of Tor Veiloth. In a misty vale away from lights or distractions of the city. It is nestled against the between a pair of massive rolling hills separated by a large river called Somme. Most of the major research in the country is done there. Only the wealthiest, highest ranked, most academically gifted, or athletically talented are accepted.

Charlotte's prodigious academic achievements and Florence's athletic genius were enough to earn them both a place there. While there are better schools for sports, Florence chose Rainbridge to be with Charlotte. It is a running joke that the athletes there learn nothing but how to throw a ball. Florence won them many national championships, including in poledancing.

Other universities do exist, including the military college at Eden's Gate or the naval academy in Portsmouth, among others.

While the majority of students are female, given that most are noble girls who need higher education to run their estates, but a surprising number are male, particularly those who excel at mathematics, athletics, and academic research.

The campus is almost magical, with elegant, curved architecture which wouldn't be out of place in a fantasy novel. True to its name, it rains often and is misty in the mornings and night. A tall tower rises above the mists where astronomy is studied. The starry night lights up the mists in a surreal bluish hew. The school requires religious courses.
