Histroy: An orphan who grew up penniless in the rough streets of the industrial town Great Falls, a young Vanessa earned her bread and bed fighting in underground blood sports, her first match with a similarly aged child was at the age of 7. She did anything to survive: stealing, robbing, gang enforcement, and mafia executioner, earning herself the moniker 'Bloody Vanessa'. As she grew older, Vanessa became more and more violent, enough so that even her comrades began to see her as a liability. Eventually, they set her up, ensuring that she was blamed for the massacre of a rival crime family. With an enormous bounty on her head, she fled the county, barely staying alive against bounty hunters from Andrea's Cross, whom she formed a grudging respect and admiration for. 

Finding a small reprieve from the huntress the pursued her, she returned to her desiccated hovel one night to find a mysterious and ominous woman there. The exiled Loren Claymore had risked much to return to Flora in disguise for the sole purpose of recruiting Bloody Vanessa into her exclusive conspiracy. Loren's unnerving insight into human desire allowed her to perceive and offer Vanessa everything the redhead desired in exchange for her services. The Faustian Bargain struck, Bloody Vanessa henceforth served Loren's agenda.
