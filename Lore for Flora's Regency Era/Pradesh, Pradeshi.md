# Pradesh, Pradeshi

Pradesh is a large island chain to the southwest of the Nisran Continent (culturally similar to India). It was conquered and occupied a generation ago by Skaag, a warlike empire across the ocean. As many Pradeshis as possible escaped, and most found themselves in Flora. Florans despise the Skaagi and therefore treat pradeshi refugees with a great deal of camaraderie.

The Pradeshi people are darker skinned, scrappy, and have a more male-centric culture, though that is somewhat less prominent amongst the current generation of Pradeshi Florans, as there is a good deal of cultural contamination on both sides.

While there is cultural friction between Florans and Pradeshis, their mutual Skaagi enemy binds them together and paves over any conflict. To Flora’s credit, when Pradesh was taken, they accepted the refugees with little issue, their mutual hatred for the Skaagi bonding the two groups 

Florans consider Skaagi death cultists, considering them the antithesis of their own ideology. 

A great deal of capital was set aside by the various matriarchs to settle and provide employment for the Pradeshi refugees, partially due to the Flora’s humanitarian ideals, but also to prevent an explosion in crime. (A very loose parallel might be what occurred with the Cuban refugees.)
