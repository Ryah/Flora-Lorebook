# Valheim, Valheimr

Valheim is a scattered nation of wild, seafaring people who until more recently operated as raiders and pirates, preying on Flora. They are a loose alliance of tribes, war bands, and villages.

The nomadic nature of the Valheimr, as well as their ferocity and the harshness of their lands, made it difficult for the Florans to fight them directly. Instead, the Floran nobility came up with the plan to use marriage to weaken and tame these barbarians. Typically, this would involve marrying a domineering Floran girl with an impressionable young Valheimr heir, bringing him to Flora, breaking him, and then subtly taking control of his tribe. The Valheimr leaders, greedy for the wealth that was being lavished on their families through these marriages, didn’t look this gift horse in the mouth.

Today, Valheim is little more than a Floran puppet, with their young king secretly controlled by his dominatrix wife, Princess Euphemia of Flora. Flora has influenced Valheim to raid their rival Duran incessantly. Imperialist Florans point to their success in ‘taming’ the barbarians as proof that Flora should expand her control to Duran and other nearby nations.

Florence is half Valheimr through her father and half Floran through her mother.
