# Skaag, Skaagi

Skaag is an extremely powerful expansionist empire across the ocean. A generation ago, they conquered the Pradeshi island chain, gaining a staging ground near Nisra. Everyone on the continent is at least concerned about the Skaagi.

Skaag's relentless aggression has resulted in an undeclared state of perpetual war between Flora and Skaag, with most of this conflict taking place on the seas, raiding of supplies, and rebuffed invasions. Any aggression on either side is sanctioned on the high seas.

Skaag is fighting numerous wars on their own continent, which is the reason they haven't been able to focus on the relatively distant Nisra yet. But it seems to be only a matter of time. Flora's spy agency has agents all over Skaagi's continent, fomenting wars between the military juggernaut and its neighbors, in the hopes of seeing it destroyed. If Skaag were able to focus on Nisra, the resulting invasion would be apocalyptic for Flora, Duran, and the surrounding nations.

The Skaagi consider Nisrans, and Florans in particular, to be a soft people just waiting to be conquered and enslaved.

Skaagi worship a number of war gods, which ensures that their conquests are many and brutal (base them on the Assyrians). Their occupations are unsurprisingly oppressive and Pradeshis attempt to escape their islands to get to surrounding nations constantly, ensuring a steady supply of refugees to Flora.

Florans consider the Skaagi to almost death cultists due to their pointless brutality and hate them as only two opposite religions can. Florans believe that Althena is a goddess of life, peace, and love and that the Skaagi worship devils of rage, hate, and death.

Ambitious Skaagi warlords have attempted invasions of Flora in the past generation, occasionally breaking through Flora's naval defenses to land troops on Flora's southern shores. These have been repulsed, but the threat remains. The only way to eliminate the immediate threat of Skaagi invasion would be to take Pradesh; however the endeavor would require immense manpower and political will, which are not in sufficient supply in Flora at the moment.

The presence of the Skaagi foothold so close to Flora's southern coast has caused a massive expansion of Flora's navy in recent decades, but it is believed that they simply do not have the manpower to resist a full blown invasion from Skaag itself, which is why Pradesh's occupation is so concerning.

Due to their forces being engaged in other wars, the Skaagi warlords in Pradesh make generous use of Pradeshi conscripts to bolster their ranks.
