# Twins

Identical twins are believed to be the same soul split into two bodies by Althena, for an unknown and ordained purpose that cannot be accomplished by one person alone. This is not actual doctrine, but it is part of church culture. There also exists the belief that twins can feel each other's feelings and sensations regardless of distance. Fraternal twins, while not the same soul, are believed to be divinely connected on a spiritual and biological level, with similarly shared sensations.
