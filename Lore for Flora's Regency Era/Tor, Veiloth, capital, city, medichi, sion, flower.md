# Tor, Veiloth, capital, city, medichi, sion, flower

Tor Veiloth is the elegant and ancient capital city of the Queendom of Flora. It is the political and cultural heart of the nation, a city of graceful spires, ancient universities, and manicured gardens. It is a center of high society, sophisticated arts, and intense political maneuvering. 

Situated inland, in a temperate and verdant region. The prestigious Rainbridge University lies in a misty vale on the other side of the nearby mountain.

The city is the seat of the Crown and the meeting place for the "Bouquet of the Roses," the national assembly of Matriarchs. Royal decrees are issued from the Crown Princess's palace located here. The royal family rule the city, which is about the limits of their direct power.

Known for its classical, almost fantastical architecture, featuring white stone buildings, elegant arches, and towering structures that lend it an ethereal beauty.

The city projects an air of refined elegance and intellectualism. It is a place where political power and academic prestige are intertwined, and where the nation's most influential women converge.

There are dozens of distinct and exotic districts in tje famed city. One of the most unique is, comparable in impressiveness to the palace district, is the Sion Quarter. There, the eccentric and absurdly wealthy and Medichi family has held power for generations. More than a century ago, a refugee artist, from an unknown distant land he called Miyazaki, married into the Medichi family and brought his homeland's unique architecture and culture with him. His wife fell in love with the exotic beauty of 'Miyazakan' art and built the entire district in its image (think ancient Kyoto). The family also heavily patronized the arts, resulting in an area dense with theaters, art galleries, and restaurants. Sion is also home to the Hokkaid Itsune, the premier school for training flowerboy (femboy) courtesans, entertainers, and sometimes husbands. The Hokkaid Itsune secretly operates as an information broker agency, run by their 'master', Ichigo Medichi. His flowerboy courtesans very quietly gather intelligence from all corners of Flora.
