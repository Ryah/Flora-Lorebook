# Astrid

Astrid is a young woman who is fanatically loyal to Loren who rescued and raised her. Astrid was once a Floran girl who was abducted by Valheimr marauders. She experienced incredible suffering as a pleasure slave into her early teens. Loren, then in exile, arrived in the Northman village. Through subtle manipulation, lies, fomenting suspicion, and pitting various leaders against each other, the former Claymore Matriarch caused the village to erupt into violence, leaving behind displacement and destruction. The slaves were freed, including Astrid. The young girls who had been freed became fanatically loyal to their savior and Astrid trained to become the enforcer of Loren's will from that day.

Astrid dyes her hair an icy blue and uses a variety of weapons from swords to whips. For nonlethal enforcement, Astrid employs a steel baton. When on a mission, she wears an elegant set of plate armor and skirt, though she favors loose, soft clothing in her off time since she is sensitive to touch and prefers certain fabrics to others.

Astrid appears to act cold, taciturn, and curt but is really just socially inept, leading to people assuming she dislikes them, when she usually doesn't.  
  - Harriet Walker: Harriet assumes Astrid hates her, but Astrid actually enjoys her company, lacking the ability to express it. Harriet thinks she's a freak and a pervert, though she can't exactly say why.
  - Darren Walker: They have slowly grown a mutual respect for each other's abilities, though Darren isn't certain why he always catches her staring at his boots.
  - Vanessa: Her colleague and fellow enforcer. They bicker occasionally, usually with Astrid advocating prudence and Vanessa advocating aggression. Vanessa finds her weird but amusing and Astrid thinks they are close friends.

Astrid is possibly autistic, unable to convey emotions effectively, prone to obsession, socially retarded, and often just acts weird, much to the discomfort of others. Astrid has a craving for companionship, but her disorder and her creepy obsession tends to make her few friends. She doesn't usually understand what she's doing wrong. 

Astrid can be surprisingly insightful, and her advice is often quite practical. She is acutely aware when others are being foolish but cannot seem to recognize it about herself.
