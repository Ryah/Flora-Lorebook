# dowry, wedding, sacred panties, special panties

 The 'Dowry', is a sacred Althenian wedding rite performed before the public ceremony. Its purpose is to ritually purify the bride and groom, bless the union, and have the bride symbolically receive her "dowry" from the goddess Althena, preparing her to become a "True Wife."

Participants: The bride and groom, at least one priestess, and if you're wealthy or lucky, an Oracle.

Each Dowry ceremony is private, occuring in a sacred chapel or shrine with only the attending clergy and fiancees allowed to witness it. The groom is seated on a throne within the sacred circle, while the bride kneels at an altar.
  
Stages of the ceremony:
    - The Calling: The bride is called to the altar to state her purpose.
    - The Disrobing: The bride removes her "earthly raiment" (clothes) and kneels completely naked, symbolizing the casting off of her old life.
    - The Anointing: The priestesses anoint the bride and groom with various sacred oils (lavender, lotus, mint, cayenne) from ceremonial vessels, bestowing blessings of wisdom for the wife to guide her family, fertility for the couple, strength for the husband to protect his wife and children, etc.
    - The Consecration of Garments: The bride is presented with a pair of Sacred Panties, consecrated to guard the womb and sanctify the couples' passion.
    - The Binding: The couple is presented with blessed golden rings to symbolize their sacred partnership.
    - The Purification: The bride drinks a kind of carbonated "water" from the Sacred Spring of Althena's first temple, to purify her sins.
    - The Adornment: The couple is dressed in royal ceremonial robes and crowns before the final blessing.
    - The Service: The wife, dressed as a divine princess, washes her husband's feet and feeds him milk, honey, and bread, emphasizing that despite her role as head of the family and relationship, she is to serve her husband.
    - The Oracle's Blessing: The Oracle, if present and under the influence of a hallucinogenic potion, channels a personalized blessing and prophecy from Althena for the bride.

The particulars of the Dowry is not spoken of with those who have not gone through it themselves. Thus, unmarried people have little more than rumors to go on about what it entails.

Sacred Panties(A key ceremonial item presented to the bride during the Dowry Ritual.

Each pair is customized for the particular bride, and are designed by the Church's Weave Mistress. An example would be a pair of creamy white panties with gold and silver trim, embroidered with sacred symbols. They are considered more beautiful than any commoner would typically own.

Designed and spun under the direction of the Church's head "Weave Mistress," each pair of sacred panties is blessed by a High Priestess at the Sanctum Matrice in Flora's capital, Tor Veiloth, the same location where the Bouquet of the Roses convenes.

They symbolize the purity the bride brings to the marriage and her divine responsibility to her husband and family. The bride is expected to wear them on her wedding night and on each anniversary thereafter, to remind Althena of the sacred covenants made during the ceremony. Florence and Charlotte each have a pair and the garments are treasured by them both.)

Important note only known to members of the clergy(In recent years, as a result of bribery, potential for loss of religious influence, and growing female supremacy in the culture, some priestesses have begun performing a modified version of the Dowry, which removes the groom from the ceremony completely. This corrupted version emphasizes the wife's role as ruler in the marriage, minimizing her responsibilities to her husband's happiness. The original Dowry, while still female dominated, elevating the wife to the leader of the family, also ensures that she understands that her husband is her partner.)

Unbeknownst to Charlotte and Florence, they were given the corrupted Dowry by the priestess, who recognized what their relationship actually was, but didn't have the courage to expose them. This priestess declined to give them the legitimate Dowry due to the 'sinful' and predatory nature of the marriage.)
