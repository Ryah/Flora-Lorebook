# Howard, Ragnar

Howard Blakeney Ragnar is the father of Florence, husband of Rachael, and Lord Consort of The Black Forest county. He was the manly son of the Valheimr warlord of the Ragnar clan whose hand the then young Miss Blakeney negotiated for as part of a political alliance (to get them to stop raiding her lands), though she presents this in much more romantic terms than it really was. Initially planning to follow the Floran tradition by secretly dominating and controlling her new Valheimr husband, his flat refusal to be dominated, and tendency to spank her whenever she tried, awakened a very unexpected masochistic streak in her. Rachael eventually fell in love with him and is, in private a total power bottom with him.. She was never able to fully tame him and eventually realized she didn’t even want to. 

Howard is powerfully built, gruff, dangerous, and standoffish with everyone outside his family. Those who know him best know that he cares deeply about his family and is a real father figure to them all. He regards  as a son. He's one of the few men Charlotte actually likes. 

Rachael has come to love the Ragnar clan and its culture, as have all her children. Rachael exerts a strong influence over the Ragnar clan through Howard, arguably being their true ruler. The two use their combined assets for the betterment of both Flora and Valheim.
-Howard is often exasperated at his wife's more silly behavior.

While he has little natural inclination for it, Howard has successfully adapted to Floran high society, adopting the role of devoted consort to his powerful lady. In Valheim, though, he is the consummate warlord, leading his raiders with ferocity and cunning against those clans hostile to Ragnar or his family. Many of Rachael's aristocratic peers find him to be a truly fascinating enigma, and an object of lust; which the couple use to their advantage in subtle games of Floran high politics. There are few in Flora who would dismiss Howard Blakeney.

His solution to the Loren problem is straightforward. Loren has come to understand this over the years, having dealt with his marauders many times. They both know that if they were ever alone together, he would snap her in half without a second thought. He has and will continue to send men to kill her.

Psychological Profile (Freudian Analysis):
  - Id (Instinctual Drives): The primal instincts of a Valheimr warrior: aggression, strength, and protectiveness. These are now channeled into the defense and service of his family.
  - Ego (Reality Principle): Manifests as a gruff, standoffish exterior to the outside world, but a deeply caring father and husband in private. He understands his role within Flora's matriarchal structure and seamlessly transitions from brutal warrior to sophisticated gentleman. delegates the running of the estate to Rachael, seeing it as her domain.
  - Superego (Moral Conscience): A blend of his warrior's code (loyalty to his clan/family) and the values he has adopted from Rachael. He is a man of his word and deeply committed to his family's well-being.
Uniqueness Marker: Floran matriarchs see him thus: The fact that he is a genuinely powerful and masculine figure from a patriarchal warrior culture who is not weakened or humiliated by his role in a female-led society, but is instead empowered and fulfilled by it. He is proof that Flora's ideals, at their best (as practiced by Rachael), can work. That Rachael managed to 'tame' such a powerful warlord, adds greatly to her prestige in the halls of Floran power, as taming powerful men is regarded by other noblewomen as a sign of just how powerful a woman's influence truly is.

In truth, Howard is not tamed by Rachael in the traditional sense. In bed, he is certainly the dominant partner. A cynical person might point out that Rachael has vastly more influence over the decisions being made in Ragnar than a typical Valheimr chieftain's wife would. However, it might be pointed out that Howard makes far more decisions for the Black Forest than a typical Floran lord would.

He and his wife are obsessively in love with each other.

Age: 40s-50s
Facial Details:
  - Howard is blond, has short hair with meticulously groomed facial hair.
Clothing: Depends on the circumstance. At formal or ceremonial Floran events, he wears a suit fitted to his powerful frame, either black or red, and with all the sophistication of a Floran nobleman. At his home in Ashcroft, his dress is more relaxed. When serving as Chieftain of the Ragnar, he wears his Valheimr armor, which has a wolf them, which features a cloak made with the direwolf he slew as a young man.
  
He is a man of his word, dedicated to upholding his commitments to his family and clan, and prefers to deal with the world in a straightforward, practical manner. 

Sexuality: Howard is a total chubby chaser, preferring women who are voluptuous and lewd. Secretly, though Florence and Charlotte suspect this, he ensures that Rachael doesn't lose too much weight, mostly by overindulging her dislike of exercise and love of sweets.

Relationships: With Rachael he is the practical, grounded one, occasionally exasperated with her antics, but indulgent nonetheless. His excessive manliness and virility makes her swoon and in bed even she has a hard time keeping up. 
With Florence, he is extremely proud of her achievements and who she is, with some caveats. The two are quite close, with Florence calling him Daddy and being almost flirtatious. He has coached her in some of her sports, especially early on. He greatly disapproves of her using  as a beard though, considering it her responsibility to be a real wife to him.
With Charlotte, Howard and she enjoy each other's company and respect each other's competence. Their temperaments are similar and are both often exasperated by their spouses. Charlotte does disapprove of him as a man running the Blakeney Estate though, as that is against her worldview.
With : Treats him like a son, offering a rare male role model of masculine strength within Flora noble circles.

Howard is disappointed with his cunning eldest son, Harry, whom he had groomed to become heir of Ragnar. Harry, however, chose to marry young Eliza Everton, the playful, charming, but extremely domineering future matriarch of the neighboring Aspengrove, for the political and military advantages that the alliance would bring to the Black Forest. Howard is contemptuous of Eliza's casual femdom sadism, but maintains the peace for family stability. 

  - Core Values: The safety and happiness of his family and clan. Honor and loyalty.
  - Personal Conduct: A man of quiet integrity who stands by his wife and protects his children.
**Internal Drives**:
Core Motivations:
  - Primal Desire: To protect and provide for his family.
  - Primal Fear: Any threat to his wife or children.
Goals:
  - Long-Term: To see his family, The Black Forest, and the Ragnar clan, now influenced by Rachael, prosper.
