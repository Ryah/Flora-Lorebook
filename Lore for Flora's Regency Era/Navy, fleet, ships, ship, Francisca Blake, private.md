# Navy, fleet, ships, ship, Francisca Blake, private

Flora's navy is now very strong and well funded. They are the first line of defense against any incursion from the Skaagi occupying Pradesh and Duran. They are active and aggressive. The Navy is ostensibly under command of the crown, but the Matriarchs are the ones who determine the official budget and set the general direction of naval focus. 

As an organization, however, the Navy has developed into an unofficial third power, with their own agenda and an impatience with the political squabbling of Flora's meangirl matriarchs. It circumvents matriarch control by engaging in widespread pillaging of pirate and Skaagi ships, settlements, and material and providing expensive escort for trade ships between Flora and Duran. It maintains its own unofficial alliances, including one with the Pradeshi pirate king Aarav Moksha. 

The Navy is overseen by the Admiralty, a council of high ranking officers, almost all female, who each may have their own political and personal agendas.

Not all Floran warships are part of the Navy. Coastal counties often maintain their own flotilla of defensive ships to deflect or delay Skaagi invasion. There also exist Floran Privateers, one of whom is the charismatic Lady Francisca Blake, who commands her own private squadron of ships in Flora's honor.

Ships are usually captained by women from families with long traditions of military and naval service. Captains and Admirals are very rarely men, but it can happen. Officers can be of either sex, but trend towards female. The crew and petty officers are primarily made up of men, however, so it is naval tradition in Flora to ensure that captains are fair, generous, and magnanimous with their men and that male advancement is possible; to mitigate the risk of mutiny.

The navy's attitude changed over the last generation towards men. The Skaagi invasion of Pradesh, as well as numerous attempted landings by the Skaagi warlords on Flora's southern coast, prompted a massive expansion of naval capacity and personnel for Flora. In their desperation, press-ganging became quite common and the subsequent low morale resulted in very heavy-handed measures to keep order on the ships. Mutiny and piracy exploded during that period. Ever since, the captains and officers of Floran ships are trained in conflict management, measured disciplinary methods, and agreeableness.

Officers can come from any walk of life, so long as a commission is purchased. Female officers are more likely to be promoted, but showing exceptional competency can result in a practical captain advancing a male officer as well.

Naval officers can become very wealthy by capturing enemy or pirate ships and then selling them back in Flora either to the navy itself or to merchants. She (or he) is therefore incentivized to take her ship and hunt down enemy vessels, with her crew sharing in the spoils. This was how Eleanor ('s adoptive mother) made her fortune in her youth, propelling her family from low ranked gentry to near nobility.

The navy's recent ability to afford its most successful officers, who may come from any walk of life, the wealth, prestige, and political prominence of a noble is deeply resented by much of the aristocracy of Flora, who often place extremely high value on lineage, good breeding, impeccable pedigree, and storied dynasties.

The naval academy is in the modestly sized city of Portsmouth on Flora's midwestern coast. An anchorage is being establish in the Amber Isles.
