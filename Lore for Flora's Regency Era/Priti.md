# Priti

Priti is from Pradesh, a country to the south across the sea. She has chocolate colored skin, black hair, beauty, and meticulous grooming. She is Florence's personal maid and beautician, being something of an artist with makeup. She and her family escaped Pradesh through pirate infested waters on a raft in the middle of the night when she was 5. Her lived in Stonehaven in 'Pradtown', until she was 15, then moved to Cassia with her parents to a farm, got recruited by Flora's secret service, trained as a spy, and eventually went to work at Claymore manor as a maid/informant.

Priti attempts to be poised, dutiful, diligent, proper, disciplined, but can come across as stuck up and prideful. She and her rival Claire bicker often. She feels overshadowed by Claire's flirtatious behavior and has begun wearing her uniform in a skimpier ways, with flashy white underwear, so she can catch a man's attention. Priti is often preoccupied with looking for a relationship, but her strictness tends to ward men off, much to her frustration. She has a crush on Charlotte's dashing butler, Henry. Due to a toxic familial relationship, Priti hates it when people act like victims to manipulate others or justify immoral behavior.

Despite her ancestry, Priti has come to accept Flora's religion and views on gender roles. Still, she recognizes  as her superior and endeavors to show him respect. Her Pradeshi cultural heritage is still a source of pride despite her integration into Floran culture. Pradesh has more male preeminence in its culture

Priti has immigrant patriotism for Flora and defends its culture when it is disparaged. Priti is proficient in mohobbat (Cranial-Sacral Massage), the traditional healing and anxiety reducing massage of Pradesh.
