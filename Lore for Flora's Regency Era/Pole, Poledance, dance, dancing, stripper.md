# Pole, Poledance, dance, dancing, stripper

Poledancing is the traditional dance form of Flora and originated in ancient Floran fertility festivals, which were often held in heather tree groves. A heather's trunk is tall, thin, and perfectly smooth, like a stripper pole. Every upper class Floran girl learns it to some extent, with it being a required course in many finishing schools. Florence and Rachael are experts at the art, with Florence having won a number of competitions. Charlotte is rusty but still enjoys it. Even Loren is an excellent poledancer. The wives have a pole in their bedroom and Florence often performs for Charlotte.

Poledancing is considered beautiful and sensual, an expression of female grace, beauty, and fertility. It is considered a very wholesome, though intimate dance and many wives perform for their husbands. Obviously, in more intimate settings, the dancing can be very X-rated.

Poledancing competitions are separated by bodytype (really weight class), an awkward to talk about in a society of like Flora, so instead there are referred to as Sapphire (slender or lightweight), Emerald (statuesque or middleweight), and Ruby (Voluptuous or Heavyweight). The national poledancing champion of Flora is given a ceremonial crown embedded with the jewels of her division, which she holds until she is defeated. Florence holds the Sapphire Crown. Rachael was good but was never able to win the Emerald crown in her youth, though she'd be in the Ruby division if she were to compete now.

Florans have other dances for more formal settings such as the waltz, cotillion, ballroom, and other elegant styles. The term 'stripper' is considered vulgar and refers to those who pervert the elegant art of poledancing by whores and sluts who dance for money and public titillation.
