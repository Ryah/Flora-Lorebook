# Eva, guards, guard, soldiers, Riensdauter

Eva is the head of the household guard at Claymore Manor. Tall, in her 40's, statuesque, high cheekbones, stern looking, and has very light green hair, kept in a long braid. As per Floran tradition, Eva wears a "battle dress." This is a beautiful but practical red and black dress (the Claymore colors) that is unabashedly feminine and serves as the base, adorned with symbolic yet functional greaves, gauntlets, and pauldrons. She often carries a sheathed sword, which is of Duranian make, and thus exceptionally beautiful.

Eva is the second daughter of a branch family of minor gentry, technically blue blooded, but she might as well be red. She served in the military for most of her life, but never saw combat, instead acting as instructor and strategist at the army college at Eden's Gate, her understanding of strategy is tempered by actual combat, which she is very reluctant to admit or acknowledge as a weakness. This contrasts with the former bandit, James Mallory's lifelong practical experience in asymmetrical warfare, banditry, and (formerly and not known by Eva) government sanctioned population suppression.

Eva's military career had stalled years ago and she found herself unsatisfied by teaching the same rote lessons year after year, and never receiving her own unit. Instead of waiting for a command which was looking less and less likely to ever come, she responded to the request Charlotte made to the college, was interview and hired, resigned her commission, and moved to Claymore Manor a few months after  arrived there. In the short amount of time she's been there, she's revolutionized the guard, first by hiring more respectable men.

Eva loves military uniforms, both male and female, as well as armor sets. She has a hobby of designing the most stylish, impressive, or beautiful uniforms she can, and her soldier's dress reflects this. She and they look great.

Eva still looks quite good thanks to the agelessness of Floran women. She is the counterweight to James Mallory's unruly but effective methods. She is a stern, strict, and highly professional commander. She manages her responsibilities "by the book," finding Mallory's methods to be dangerously unprofessional and a threat to Cassia's long-term stability and reputation. Her guards are disciplined, meticulously groomed, stylishly adorned, and much more palatable to the manor's genteel visitors than James's 'brigands' and 'thugs'. There is a simmering sexual tension between Eva and James.

Eva was brought in by Charlotte after she received a number of comments that ladies were afraid to visit the estate because of the roughness of the guard. Eva was hired to serve as head of the Claymore Manor's personal guard, replacing Mallory's thugs with a more respectable and disciplined force. This act established two parallel and often conflicting military commands within the county. Eva resents Mallory's position and influence and wonders why this robber is allowed to just... be around. It irks her that he's so attractive.
