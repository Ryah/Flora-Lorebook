# Amber, Isles, Eleanor

The Amber Isles is a small archipelago off the southwestern coast of Flora, near Andrea's Cross. Originally independent, the islands were slowly brought into the Floran fold around over the last 40 years ago as a result of a combination of political pressure from Tor Veiloth (capital of Flora) and fear of Skaagi invasion. Once the Rape of Domremy occurred, The Amber Isles joined officially. The strategic position of the islands allows it to act as a shield for Flora's western flank, requiring any invasion fleet to pass near its now heavily fortified cannon batteries.

The Isles' government is still somewhat dispersed, locals electing their own rulers, but upon joining with Flora, a provincial matriarch position (referred to as governess in this case) was required to oversee the newly recognized county. This position is currently held by Eleanor Castor, 's adoptive mother.

A once minor gentlewoman from the mainland, Eleanor rose from obscurity to wealth and prestige through her time as captain of the HMS Vengeance, and afterwards her secret alliance with her the powerful and devious Matriarch Loren Claymore.

This was where  was raised.

Because of its more recent inclusion as a Floran province, The Isles' communal, warrior culture is significantly more balanced in terms of male/female political and familial power, with older traditions of male headship still holding true in many islands. Eleanor has been working hard to bring the isles' embarrassing (in her eyes) and backwards male centric traditions into the modern era of female dominance, with limited success. Her own family was no exception; she attempted to raise her daughters and adoptive son to be proper female supremacist imperialists, again with mixed results. Eleanor is ambitious, but also not without conscience. She was given rulership by making a secret pact with Loren, and is now trapped into doing the villainess's bidding.

The money from 's dowry, and the official marriage alliance with Cassia and the Black Forest, have bolstered The Isles' prestige and military infrastructure significantly. Now able to secure greater loans due to implicit alliance with her wealthy in-laws, Eleanor has been able to harden the island fortresses into bastions of death for any passing invasion fleet from Skaag. The port is also being improved to serve as an anchorage for the Navy, which Eleanor still has ties to.

The Amber Isles export a unique wood called Santos, that is very strong, which the navy is beginning to plate their ships with. Other exports are deeps sea fish (tuna), and exotic fruit.
