# Bath, bathing, bathe, shower, running water, sewer

Baths in Flora are traditionally communal, with multiple people bathing together with little regard to modesty. In ancient times, there was little segregating the sexes during the bath but that practice is less common today.

There are two primary baths in Claymore manor. The Mistress Bath, reserved for family and guests, is very large, elegant, and mostly made of beautifully carved marble. It can accommodate dozens of bathers and servants at once; with multiple baths of varying heats and liquids (like herbal teas). Both the wives, , and guests use it. When assisting in the bath, maids wear a uniform consisting of a black one-piece swimsuit with white ruffles at the hips and shoulders. When not actively bathing their employers or guests, the maids will relax in the bath with them.

The servants have their own, larger but less ornate bath, located near the commons. The baths both have limited running water, thanks to the water tower hidden amongst the northern parapets. The water tower and piping were put in by Charlotte's mother and recently improved with a mill-powered pump by Charlotte herself.
