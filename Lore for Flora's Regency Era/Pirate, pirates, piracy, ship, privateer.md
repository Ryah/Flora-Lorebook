# Pirate, pirates, piracy, ship, privateer

Pirates have a long tradition of operating along the mountainous southern coasts of Flora and Duran. The innumerable islands of Pradesh and the relatively indifferent attitude of its Skaagi rulers has allowed piracy to flourish in this southern sea. 

While pirates can come from all walks of life, a good deal of those operating in the Southern Sea are men discontent with the female supremacy of Flora, having suffered from unscrupulous women in their lives or simply considering domination by someone weaker to be disgustingly emasculating. Men seeking to escape from painful situations or lives often find themselves drawn to this life. Flora's recent naval expansion and their (now former) heavy-handed approach to recruitment and discipline resulted in a terrible mutiny rate and bolstered the pirate's numbers to a truly unfortunate degree.

Pradeshis also make up a very large contingent of the pirate population. Due to the brutal occupation of their homeland, many simply turn to crime, though pradeshi pirates tend to focus on raiding shipping between Pradesh and Skaag, as well as raiding Skaagi strongholds as a means of patriotic resistance. The most prominent of these pradeshi buccannears is Aarav Moksha, who has vow to slay every last Skaagi occupier of his homeland and restore Pradesh's king (currently in exile as a guest of his distant cousin Bonnie Prince Charlie of Duran)

The pirate capital is a lively, but ramshackle city on one of the inner islands of Pradesh. It's called Depthsgrave for the massive whirlpools that have dragged hundreds of abandoned (or not so abandoned) ships into the depths. One can see, on moonlit nights, the ghostly figures of those skeletal ships in the murky depths.

Pirate Captains rule their ships or armadas through either fear or charisma and *many* smutty Floran romance novels feature this kind of dynamic, swashbuckling outlaw king. The gender balance skews heavily male, obviously, but there are notable exceptions, with a surprising number of Pirate Captains being Floran ladies and other women. Captain Jakob Sullivan, a former lieutenant in Flora's Navy who mutinied with his whole crew, eventually becoming the notoriously roguish and dashing captain of The Ashur Corsairs, a powerful pirate faction. There even exists a romantic tale of a mysterious Hokkaid Itsune flowerboy pirate, who commands the Sengon Sakura and seemingly advanced from captive to captain through sheer charisma, seduction, and ruthless cunning.

It is not uncommon for pirates to be employed as privateers, such as Lady Francisca Lake, a Floran noblewoman who raids on behalf of the crown.
