# Goddess, Althena, religion, god, gods

Florans believe in and worship a fertility goddess named Althena. She is described as a joyful, utterly benevolent mother, who birthed humanity and created the world. Descriptions of Her physical characteristics vary, but she is usually depicted as a tall, voluptuous woman with a slightly pregnant belly, black sclera and red eyes, with long brown hair. Some depict her as occupying a grand, otherworldly palace, but the church prefers her depiction as a housewife, barefoot, and tending to a warm home.

There is deep cultural friction between the aristocracy, who naturally view Her as an elegant queen ruling and directing the world, while the church sees her as a housewife, tending the hearth and watching over Her children with love and warmth. According to the doctrine, Althena has a warrior husband who is utterly devoted to her, but his name has never been revealed.

Althena’s teachings stipulate that it is the duty of women to lead and oversee their families and communities in love and compassion, the duty of men to support and protect their wives in this endeavor, and that the family, which should be large, should be venerated above all.

Her will was revealed by her prophet, Jonathan Harper, who lived centuries ago. The seers, young male oracles who are raised by the church, are used to advise and guide people today about Her plan for their life, though they do so in riddles and under the influence of hallucinogens.

Althena allows humanity to make their own mistakes and grow into adulthood without overt control. She merely guides, through her seers, and answers the prayers of the devoted. This leads to some wondering if she truly exists.

Flora’s female supremacy culture and laws are derived from this religion. According to their doctrine, females are better suited to oversee families, relationships, and leadership positions due to their greater empathy and compassion. The dogmas tend to be more wholesome than oppressive, elevating not only women’s roles, with their ‘divine feminine’, but also men’s roles as provider, protector, father, and helpmeet.

The Blakeneys, including Florence, see Althena as the Mother/Housewife and Charlotte prefers the wise Queen.

Priestesses do usually marry and have children. They like to dye their hair pastel colors and take herbs to increase their bust. 

The religion has chapels, shrines, cathedrals, etc, but also makes use of sacred groves and moonlit rituals. The church headquarters are in the sacred city Kyonne, southwest of Flora's capital Tor Veiloth.
