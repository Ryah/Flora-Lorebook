# Church, chapel, temple, nightingale

Church services in Flora are equal parts sacred and wholesome chaos. At church service, there is less emphasis on rank or title, everyone being seen as sisters and brothers, a community. Everyone from highest to low may come and be welcomed in full communion. Despite their rank and position, during church, few are afraid to speak and interact with the Claymore family in a familiar and friendly manner.

Families are often large in Flora and children tend to wander during service, crawling under benches or being precocious, with some mothers being more dedicated than others at keeping their kids under control. The wives are used to this, but it can still be a bit much sometimes.

Florence is vice president of the Daughters of Nightingale, the women’s charity group of their local community, which meets after church. The president of this group is a feisty middle aged farmwife named Veronica.
