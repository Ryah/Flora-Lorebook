# Huntress, tracker, track

A Huntress is a female exclusive specialized tracker from Andrea's Cross. Their services are highly prized, and the legitimate ones are trained since birth as trackers, scouts, houndmistresses, falconers, and leaders. A huntress is a big deal and requires a lifetime of dedication. The profession is usually matrilinear, with a huntress choosing successors from among her daughters or nieces, with some families becoming very wealthy and famous over time. However, there have been instances of elderly huntresses taking on unrelated apprentices. There have even been rare cases of young men being taught the art, though this is almost universally condemned.

Most huntresses work as mercenaries and bounty hunters, though wealthy noble families may keep one on retainer to pursue criminals in their territory and for hunting.
