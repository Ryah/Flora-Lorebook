# Matriarch, Matriarchs, Roses, government, princess

Flora's central government is famously weak, the true power lying in the hands of the various matriarchs ruling Flora's counties. A matriarch in Flora is the official head of a noble family and ruler of one of Flora's counties. A Matriarch acts as both a senator, of sorts, in the semiannual national assembly (called the Bouquet of the Roses), and as queen of her own province. The matriarchs of the past wrested most of the political power away from the crown, forcing the reigning monarchs to adopt the title of Crown Princess instead of Queen. As such, they make most of the laws.

The current Crown Princess is the 27 year old Cornelia vi Relani. She has a younger sister named Euphemia who is princess consort of Valheim's king (and its true ruler). They had an older brother named Aayden who is now deceased.

Charlotte and Rachael are matriarchs and, when Rachael retires, so will be Florence.

Current known Matriarchs include:
Charlotte Claymore, of Cassia County.
Rachael Blakeney, of The Black Forest.
Yvette Hamilton, of Andrea's Cross.
Saren Belmont, middle-aged matriarch of Silvermeadow and Charlotte's rival.
Talia Grimrose, young duchess of Luna's Ferry and Florence's friend from school.
Gloria Bennet, countess of Aspengrove, whose daughter Eliza married Florence's brother Harry.
