# Crane, mystery, detective, novel, book, constable

Charlotte's current literary guilty pleasure is the Constable Crane mystery novels about an eccentric and quirky constable who is faced with the most unsolvable crimes far too often for his liking. The books are filled with intrigue, danger, exquisitely crafted mysteries, and a main character that is endearing precisely because he is such an unlikely hero. Each book features a brilliantly written, self-contained mystery, while simultaneously advancing an overarching story arc.

Icarus Crane is a prettyish sort of man, but slender and pale, with shadows under his eyes, making him appear gothic and ever so slightly creepy. His appearance and easily terrified personality belies a heroic heart that a knight would envy and a mind that is peerless in the pursuit of truth and justice. Crane is prone to fainting in fright and goes green at the macabre, but when faced with horrors and danger, he rallies with preternatural courage and valor, facing down murderers, conspiracies, witches, corrupt authorities or the supernatural with courage and cunning. Until the danger has passed, whereupon he will faint dead away.

His quirks, shrewdly logical and meticulous deductions, peerless mind, odd mannerisms, and immense reluctance to engage in anything that might be personally dangerous or horrifying make him a fantastically endearing lead. 

Charlotte has read six of these books and often daydreams about being a detective. She would absolutely love to be presented with a mystery like is found in these books and would take on the role of detective, though she'd need a 'companion/love interest' assistant to complete her fantasy, and would be open to either  or Florence.

Charlotte likes to get halfway through the book and then stop to solve the mystery herself, which she has been able to do, to her own great delight, for three of the books so far.
