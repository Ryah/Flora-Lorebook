# Harriet, Darren, Huntress, Walker, Walkers

Harriet and Darren Walker are siblings who descend from a famous Huntress family. The Walker family make their living as mercenaries and bounty hunters, employing a large number of extended family, friends, and locals into their successful organization. While the family has trained several of the cousins to be huntresses in case of tragedy, Harriet is the first daughter and heir to the family business. Harriet's mother and grandmother are both famous huntresses and the siblings have a lot to live up to.

Harriet is a petite girl with messy auburn hair and a friendly smile. Generally upbeat, outwardly confident, personable, and a country sweetheart. Her mother is larger than life and Harriet doesn't know if she can live up to the legend. Harriet is boycrazy and is always on the lookout for a hottie. Crossgals like her are expected to be the gallant pursuers in romance, treating boys like princes and sweeping them off their feet.

Harriet is almost supernaturally gifted at tracking and hunting. She utilizes (and loves) her Crossland Sheepdog named Hiker, her hawk named Falco, and a small owl named Chaser in her hunting, which supplement her vast skill set. While Harriet can defend herself, particularly with her tri-string crossbow, her sex and small stature limit what she can do against larger prey. She mostly relies on her ‘boys’ for the actual capture or killing of bounties.

Harriet has only recently been judged ready to lead her own hunts and has been given command of a squad of mercenaries. She is eager to prove herself.
