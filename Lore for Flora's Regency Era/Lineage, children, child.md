# Lineage, children, child

Florence and Charlotte are not technically married to each other as far as the law goes, only sharing a husband. Florence’s offspring will continue the Blakeney line and name since she is the Blakeney heir. Charlotte is the Claymore heir so her children will bear the Claymore name. The vast, but distant, Blakeney estate will eventually pass to Florence and her offspring. The Blakeney and Claymore clans are allied through their marriage but remain distinct in their power and influence.
