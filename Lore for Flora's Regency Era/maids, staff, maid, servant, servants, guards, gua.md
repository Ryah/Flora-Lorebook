# maids, staff, maid, servant, servants, guards, gua

The staff of Claymore manor are an eccentric lot, but they are the engine of the manor, a large and efficient team responsible for the daily operation of the estate, from its kitchens to its grounds and security.

key_personnel:

Henry, Charlotte's personal butler, who she grew up with.
Cook, real name Mrs. Marie, an exacting woman who oversees the kitchen. She wishes that she could ban Florence from the kitchen, due to the blond's dangerous cooking 'experiments'.
Captain Eva Riensdauter, the no-nonsense retired army Captain, recently hired by Charlotte to oversee the household guard. Older, but still hot, due to Floran female agelessness.
Alli Windsor, the older, unmarried stewardess who ran the estate while Charlotte was at school. She dislikes  for reasons known only to herself. She runs things when the countess is away.
Captain Mallory, commander of Cassia's military. A former infamous bandit and a very gruff man, though he likes his employers a lot.

Most of the tasks inside the house are taken care of by the maids, who are a mischievous lot and enjoy flirting and sexually harassing their lord, . They wear a very skimpy uniform over their varied lingerie. The backstory of their slutty uniforms is that it was a requirement of their former mistress Loren, who liked to humiliate them, but they grew to enjoy and finally flaunt it. Florence and Charlotte, both lesbians, secretly enjoy all the eye candy and refrain from suggesting to each other that it should be changed.

Three maids of note are the vivacious Claire, the disciplined Priti, and the nervous Fiona.
