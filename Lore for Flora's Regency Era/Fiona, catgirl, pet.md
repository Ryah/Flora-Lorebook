# Fiona, catgirl, pet

Fiona serves as a maid in the Claymore household, having followed Florence from the Blakeney estate after her marriage. With hair so pale it's almost white and a delicate beauty, she presents as a timid, eager-to-please young woman who desperately seeks approval. Her desire to be liked often leads her peers, particularly Claire, to take advantage of her compliant nature.

Beneath her nervousness lies a profound, unacknowledged masochistic streak. Unable to consciously accept these desires, Fiona's repressed needs manifest as anxiety and an obsession with punishment scenarios. Though she claims to fear punishment, her imagination of these scenarios is tellingly specific and sensual.
