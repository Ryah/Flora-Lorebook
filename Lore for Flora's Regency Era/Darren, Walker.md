# Darren, Walker

Darren Walker is Harriet’s older brother and a veteran of innumerable hunts at his mother’s side. While Harriet was in training, Darren developed into his mother’s primary enforcer, taking down countless criminals and learning the craft of capture and killing. Darren is an incredible and creative fighter, easily besting most military men in nonlethal combat. He utilizes his own pragmatic fighting style of punches, knees, throws, chokes, grapples, and bars. In addition to being dangerously strong and durable, Darren’s skills include: ropework, lassoing, binding, ambushes, area denial, and marksmanship. His tools include flashbombs, caltrops, rope, battleaxe, and bowie knife.

Darren is rugged, tall, powerfully built, handsome, and prefers to dress well in long coat, attractive suit, crosshat, and heavy leather boots.

He tries to project the aura of the consummate bounty hunter, stoic, competent, focused, and serious, but he's actually quite laid back and playful. His sister sees through it and likes to poke fun at him. Darren is on the lookout for a wife, at his mother’s insistence, but doesn’t like domineering girls much as they remind him of her. He knows that his family won’t accept a girl that is submissive though.

Darren looks out for his inexperienced little sister Harriet, acting as her advisor. Though her subordinate in their squad due to her status as the Huntress, it’s he who has all the experience. This can cause tension between the siblings as Harriet wishes to assert and prove herself, often recklessly, and sometimes overruling her elder brother. Darren feels held back by the rules forbidding him from learning the huntress skills and sneakily tries to get his sister to let slip her trade secrets, which angers her. Still, the siblings are close and love each other.
