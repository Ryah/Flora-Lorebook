# Session: Your Wives - 2026-04-20 03-41-18

# Session Summary: June 20, 1843 | 8:35 PM - 9:10 PM

## Key Events & Plot Developments
*   **Ryan affirmed his commitment to Estra** in the east wing guest chamber, telling her he was marrying *her*, not "a bride," and that Althena would be smiling between them at the altar. Estra, deeply moved, ordered him to leave to preserve the dress surprise.
*   **Ryan exited the room with eyes closed** and was intercepted in the corridor by Mina and Florence. Mina guided him toward the main hall while Florence adjusted his waistcoat and cravat pin.
*   **In the main hall, Ryan prayed aloud to Althena** to wake the unconscious Oracle, arguing the wedding needed Church documentation.
*   **The Oracle was revived by divine intervention**, staggering downstairs claiming Althena had "kicked" him awake with a threat to turn him into a shrub if he didn't witness the union. Althena's voice corrected this to a "nudge."
*   **A debate ensued about Cornelia vi Relani's attendance**. Ryan argued for her presence, citing Althena's advice about using chaos and unpredictability against Loren. Charlotte reluctantly agreed under heavy guard. Mina was sent to fetch her.
*   **Althena accepted a "rent discount"** for her bathtub residence and confirmed Cornelia "smelled of longing, not malice."
*   **Ryan, Florence, Mina, and the Oracle moved to the Great Hall** to view the completed banners. Florence showed three panels of blue silk with dimensional gold Althena symbols and the inscription "Four vessels. One covenant."
*   **Ryan validated Florence's work**, calling it "perfect." He then kissed her and whispered a promise to negotiate a "reward" from Charlotte for her efforts.
*   **The group proceeded to the chapel** to finalize the layout. Ryan outlined the processional order: William would walk Estra down the aisle, with Florence and Charlotte following slightly behind on left and right. He also proposed tactical positioning at the altar, with Mina and Cora standing guard behind the wedding parties for protection and easier evacuation.
*   **Charlotte, feeling Ryan's contentment and intimacy through the sensory link from the distant main hall, abandoned her strategy table and maps** and began moving toward the chapel.

*   **The Oracle's Awe-Filled Exhaustion:** Revived by a divine "nudge," the Oracle observed events with manic clarity, fearing shrub transformation but determined to witness the sacred covenant.
