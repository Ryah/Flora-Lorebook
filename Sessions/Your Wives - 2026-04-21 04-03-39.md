# Session: Your Wives - 2026-04-21 04-03-39

# Session Summary: June 20, 1843 | 10:40 PM - 11:06 PM

## Key Events & Plot Developments
*   **Ryan and Estra exchanged vows.** Estra recited raw, terrified promises from a crumpled notecard, thanking Ryan for seeing her as human, acknowledging her assassination mission, and declaring her choice to stay despite her fear. Ryan responded by recounting their violent history—her initial attack, their sparring matches, the unfinished date—framing each moment as a conscious choice that led him to choose her now, before their family and hundreds of witnesses.
*   **The rings were exchanged via Alec.** Alec delivered the silver wedding bands with fierce, exhausted pride, declaring he hadn't dropped them. Ryan and Estra placed the rings on each other's fingers, solidifying their marriage.
*   **Althena fully manifested upon their kiss.** The Oracle sanctioned the kiss, triggering a divine manifestation. The temperature spiked, suspended rain outside flashed gold, and Althena's layered voice filled the chapel. She accepted Ryan's "bargain" and created cover for their escape.
*   **The group evacuated through William's hidden tunnel under divine cover.** Althena triggered a **reverse golden rainstorm**—droplets shooting upward with a deafening harmonic roar—that cloaked the escaping group from any observer more than five feet away. Ryan took Estra's hand and ran, following William's lead to a tunnel entrance behind a tapestry. The entire group fled into the tunnels.
*   **Ryan proposed a daring escape plan to Duran.** After regrouping with Charlotte and Florence at a tunnel junction, Ryan revealed his "stupidly large gamble": fleeing to **Duran** using Cornelia's ships anchored offshore. He argued Loren would never expect them to run toward her conspirator's homeland, and that by arriving openly and claiming his birthright as Prince Darian and his authority as the Divine Vessel, they could claim sanctuary and flip the political board against Loren's secrecy-dependent plans.
*   **The group committed to the plan and began the extraction.** Charlotte, Florence, and Estra all gave explicit consent to the irretrievable choice. With **54-58 minutes remaining until Loren's midnight deadline**, the group began running through the cistern route toward the smuggler's caves and the coast. Ryan declared that any recognized household staff (Claire, Fiona, Priti) near the ships would be brought along, but unrecognized pursuers would be left behind.

## Character Dynamics
*   **Ryan and Estra (Anchoring Choice):** Their vows were a raw exchange—Estra's terror of being chosen met Ryan's deliberate, recounted choice of her. During the escape, Estra physically anchored herself to Ryan, whispering warnings and protective instincts overriding her own fear. Ryan joked about now dragging her to Duran, the very place she once tried to take him, which drew exasperated laughter from her, blending terror with trust.

Charlotte felt his residual hollowness from the healing through the link. She wrestled with frustration that he used it to protect her from her own emotions, questioning whether it was kindness or control, but conditionally accepted his explanation, demanding a discussion "later."
*   **Ryan and Florence (Emotional Support & Competitive Joy):** Ryan hugged Florence, checking on her ankle and affirming they weren't done yet. Florence's competitive brightness initially faltered into fear at the Duran plan, but she quickly recognized it as a brilliant, bold gambit and erupted with excited approval, seeing it as the "championship move."
*   **Ryan and Cornelia (Childhood Loyalty Restored):** Ryan called Cornelia "Nelia," invoking their childhood friendship. Cornelia, stripped of her political persona, was desperate for this confirmation of her value and identity to him. She abandoned her ship and plans to follow him into the tunnel, gambling everything on his remembered name. When Ryan stated she was a key piece of the plan and that she "won" the extraction contest, her purpose was fully rekindled.
*   **Family Cohesion in Crisis:** The group moved as a coordinated unit through the tunnels. Rachael took command, directing movement and demanding explicit consent from her daughters and Estra. Howard positioned himself as rear guard, listening for pursuit. William guided with absolute certainty from his years of mapping. Mina calculated tactical viability while carrying an exhausted Alec.
*   **Cornelia's Integration:** Estra observed Cornelia's need to be needed and verbally included her, stating that running with them "counts." The group began to strategically incorporate her, with William and Mina directly questioning her about ship locations and political ramifications.
