# Session: Your Wives - 2026-04-19 23-38-05

# Session Summary: June 20, 1843 | 1:12 PM - 2:05 PM

## Key Events & Plot Developments
*   **Ryan confirmed Althena's physical presence** in the copper bathtub in his Lord's Quarters, describing her as having a form that was "not exactly real but still a body."
*   **Ryan shared Althena's strategic advice:** She counseled the group not to try to outthink Loren, but to "make her play a different game" by embracing chaos, defiance, and protecting others instead of themselves.
*   **The group adopted a "chaos strategy"** for the impending midnight assault, deciding to abandon purely rational counter-planning in favor of unpredictable actions.
*   **Ryan proposed and the group agreed to proceed with the wedding ceremony** at the 10 PM church gathering as tactical cover, with Althena officiating.
*   **Specific, multi-layered deception was planned:**
    *   William Adame—presumed dead for 15 years—would walk Estra down the aisle, creating a public shock.
    *   Alec would serve as ring bearer.
    *   Althena would manifest as a witness during the ceremony.
    *   During the ensuing chaos, three groups would evacuate via William's three secret tunnels.
*   **Ryan directly invoked Althena from the drawing room.** She answered, agreeing to be "present" and "witness" the events, accelerating Loren's discovery of her, but reiterated she would not directly intervene.
*   **Ryan, accompanied by Mina, Howard, and Rachael, visited Priti** in the second-floor linen closet where she was imprisoned.
*   **Priti confessed** she believed she was sending intelligence to Crown Princess Cornelia vi Relani to protect Flora, not to Loren Claymore. She revealed the **dead drop location** was a specific thornless pink rose bush in the north garden's third row.
*   **Ryan fiercely defended Priti** against Mina and Howard's suspicions, emphasizing her youth, verified good intentions, and current state of distress. He ordered that she be fed and treated as staff, not a prisoner.
*   **Priti offered to become a double agent**, providing recognition codes and helping feed false information to Loren's network through the dead drop.

## Character Dynamics
*   **Charlotte and Ryan:** Charlotte's twenty-year foundation of strategic planning was fundamentally challenged by Ryan's chaos strategy and direct communication with the divine. She demonstrated a significant shift, moving from attempting total control to placing strategic trust in Ryan and faith in Althena's presence.
*   **Estra and Ryan/Alec:** Estra showed deep vulnerability, confessing she only knew how to be "a servant or a killer" and didn't know how to be a public bride. Her protective focus remained split between Ryan and Alec, ultimately choosing to stay with her trembling brother rather than accompany the group to see Priti.
*   **Mina and Ryan:** Mina's protective fury toward Priti clashed directly with Ryan's order for mercy. She obeyed but left the scene visibly angry and tense, highlighting the conflict between her military instincts and her loyalty to Ryan.
*   **Ryan and Priti:** Ryan's compassionate approach broke through Priti's terror, transforming her from a sobbing prisoner into a clinging, grateful source of intelligence who pledged her help. This created a rift with Mina, who viewed the interaction as dangerously naive.
*   **Family Solidarity:** The group collectively pivoted to support the audacious wedding/chaos plan, with Florence channeling her hurt over Priti's betrayal into dressmaking enthusiasm, Cora calculating its statistical advantages, and Howard acknowledging its tactical elegance.

## New Information Revealed
*   **Althena's Direct Communication:** The goddess spoke to the group from the drawing room, confirming her presence and agreement to witness the events, defining her role as an observer rather than an intervenor.
*   **Loren's Intelligence Network:** A specific dead drop location (north garden, third row left, thornless pink climber rose bush) was identified as a conduit for Priti's messages.
*   **Priti's Motivation & Status:** She was a dupe, believing she served the Crown, not Loren. Her handlers used Cornelia's identity as credible cover.
*   **Evacuation Route Details:** William confirmed the existence and specifics of a third, three-year-old tunnel from the north tower foundation to Briarhaven docks, unknown to Loren.
*   **The Oracle's Condition:** The Oracle from the morning ceremony remained unconscious in the east wing, his availability for official documentation uncertain.
