# Session: Your Wives - 2026-04-22 22-55-11

# Session Summary: Wednesday, June 21, 1843 | 12:30 AM - 1:08 AM

## Key Events & Plot Developments
*   The group arrived at the preserved west wing suite. Queen Maya showed Ryan his childhood rooms, which she had kept ready for fifteen years.
*   Ryan declared Cornelia vi Relani "family" rather than just a friend, referencing her critical help with the escape and their childhood bond. Cornelia was overwhelmed, collapsing into tears and accepting his forgiveness.
*   Ryan requested the integration of the Claymore household staff (Henry, Priti, Claire, Fiona) into the palace. Queen Maya immediately agreed, ordering the east wing prepared for them.
*   Cornelia offered to use her knowledge of palace politics and hidden corridors to help the staff settle safely, which Charlie and Maya accepted.
*   Ryan, Charlotte, and Florence retired to the bathing room. Estra, after a moment of hesitation and reassurance from Rachael (who took the sleeping Alec), joined them.
*   The four shared a moment of quiet intimacy in the copper tub, their first chance to breathe since arriving.

## Character Dynamics
*   **Ryan & Cornelia:** Ryan publicly claimed Cornelia as family, forgiving her past betrayal. This act shattered Cornelia's guilt, transforming her devotion from penitence to fierce, grateful loyalty.
*   **The Triad & Estra:** The dynamic solidified as Estra was physically welcomed into the private bathing ritual. Ryan showed her his wedding rings to affirm her place. Charlotte's controlled acceptance and Florence's joyful exhaustion created a warm, secure atmosphere for Estra to begin trusting her new reality.
*   **Queen Maya & Ryan:** Maya's overwhelming joy was tempered by her effort to give Ryan space. She accepted all his choices—forgiving Cornelia, bringing his entire household, and his triad marriage—with desperate trust, though she struggled to let go of his hand.
*   **The Group & Cornelia:** The circle expanded to include Cornelia. Florence physically embraced her, Estra and Mina tactically accepted her as a new ally, and Charlotte offered a nod of guarded acknowledgment. Charlie was profoundly relieved to have his oldest friend welcomed back.
