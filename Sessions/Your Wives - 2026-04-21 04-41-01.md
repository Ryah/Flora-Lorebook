# Session: Your Wives - 2026-04-21 04-41-01

# Session Summary: June 20, 1843 | 10:48 PM - 11:12 PM

## Key Events & Plot Developments
*   **The group fled the chapel under Althena's divine cover.** Althena triggered a reverse golden rainstorm—droplets shooting upward with deafening noise—that cloaked the escaping group from any observer more than five feet away. Ryan took Estra's hand and ran, following William's lead to a hidden tunnel entrance behind a tapestry. The entire group, including Cornelia who abandoned her ship to follow, fled into the tunnels.
*   **The group navigated William's tunnels in total darkness.** They ran blind through the mapped passages, reaching the Tower Junction where Charlotte and Florence waited with a lantern. William presented three escape routes: north to Briarhaven, east to cliffs, or down through the cistern to old smuggler's caves leading to the coast.
*   **Ryan proposed and the group committed to the "Duran Gambit."** Ryan revealed his plan to flee to **Duran** using Cornelia's ships anchored offshore. He argued Loren would never expect them to run toward her conspirator's homeland, and that by arriving openly and claiming his birthright as Prince Darian and his authority as the Divine Vessel, they could claim sanctuary and flip the political board against Loren's secrecy-dependent plans.
*   **Charlotte, Florence, and Estra gave explicit consent.** Rachael demanded explicit consent from her daughters and Estra for this irretrievable choice. Charlotte and Florence consented mid-stride while running through the cistern. Estra declared she'd rather die in Duran than hide, giving her consent despite her fear of execution as a deserter.
*   **The group began the extraction via the cistern route.** With **54-58 minutes remaining** until Loren's midnight deadline, the group began running through the waist-deep water of the cistern route toward the smuggler's caves and the coast. Ryan declared that any recognized household staff near the ships would be brought along.
*   **Household staff joined the evacuation.** Led by **Cora Adame** following Althena's guidance, the staff—**Claire, Henry, Fiona, and Priti**—emerged from the tunnel behind the main group, all soaked and following the divine instruction to flee.
*   **The group reached the cave mouth and sighted the ships.** They emerged at the smuggler's cave to grey twilight and a churning sea. Cornelia confirmed her three ships—*The Relani's Crown* and two escorts—were anchored three miles offshore with signal lanterns visible. William assessed they had a **20-minute tidal window** before low tide made departure impossible.
*   **Ryan manifested visible divine power.** In response to Estra's fear of execution, Ryan declared Duran was *his* kingdom and he would pardon her, triggering a visible surge of **golden divine light in his eyes**. Alec noted he looked like Althena. The light was seen by the Duranian ships, which began preparing longboats.
*   **The extraction reached its final stage.** The group began the treacherous descent to the smuggler's beach in chest-deep, freezing tidewater. Duranian longboats breached through the suspended rain, throwing rope lines as the group formed a chain to reach them.
