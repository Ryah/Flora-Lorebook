# Session: Your Wives - 2026-04-23 06-34-37

# Session Summary: Wednesday, June 21, 1843 | 10:25 AM - 11:25 AM

## Key Events & Plot Developments

- **Ryan and Charlotte achieved 22 seconds of stable joint manifestation with matched 10-loop counts**, creating a crystalline ice pillar through harmonic resonance. The structure collapsed due to harmonic amplification overload rather than degradation, revealing a new challenge: controlled release of excess resonance.
- **Ryan successfully demonstrated solo solid matter manipulation**, pulling a stone from Estra's hand with 10 loops. The catch felt denser than water, and the pull dragged Estra forward a full step, proving the thread does not distinguish between a target object and anything physically attached to it.
- **Ryan increased to 15 loops and extracted a steel karambit blade from Estra's grip**, isolating the metal from the composite weapon (stone pommel, leather-wrapped wood hilt) without pulling Estra herself. He caught the flying blade. Cora documented that 15 loops were sufficient to overcome steel's structural integrity threshold.
- **Ryan refused to test blood or flesh manipulation on Estra**, establishing a hard ethical boundary. He stated he would never use the power to control or pull blood from someone, and would not test on anyone he cared about. He redirected the session to resonant projection training.
- **Ryan intentionally projected his awareness to Claire across the solarium**, creating a visible but intangible shadow-form that could see, hear, and speak. Claire witnessed both his motionless anchor body and his projected form simultaneously, experiencing dual perception of him.
- **Ryan attempted to channel power through the projection to move water**, which failed catastrophically. He felt a distant muffled catch and raised a single drop before acute neurological feedback struck, described as a spike driven through his skull. Althena intervened, explaining power requires a physical anchor — projections can only perceive, not act.
- **During Ryan's return from projection, a new phenomenon occurred: Charlotte's silver thread manifested in Ryan's mental brick room.** She actively pulled him back rather than passively anchoring, becoming a participant in his recombination rather than a stationary tether. Althena expressed genuine surprise at this development.

Claire witnessed both his forms, tried to touch the projection and passed through it, and became increasingly overwhelmed. - **Ryan & Althena:** Althena expressed approval of Ryan's ethical boundary, reinforcing that she chose him "not because you can hold the thread, but because you know when not to pull." She later appeared genuinely surprised by Charlotte's active silver manifestation during recombination.
- **Florence & Claire:** Florence guided Claire to a bench and attempted to calm her after the failed channeling attempt, explaining "He's okay. See? He's still standing."
- **Estra & Althena:** Estra asked tactical questions about projection limitations. Althena confirmed projection is for reconnaissance only — it cannot fight, manipulate, or defend.

## New Information Revealed

- **Resonant projection follows emotional connection, not spatial distance.** Ryan can only project to people he has a bond with — he cannot project to strangers, empty rooms, or unknown enemies.
- **The projection cannot channel divine power.** Althena explained: "The projection is perception — it sees, it hears, it feels — but it does not *hold*. It cannot channel what it does not possess." Attempting to channel from projection causes acute neurological feedback.
- **The anchor body is vulnerable during projection.** Estra noted the tactical weakness — if an enemy attacks Ryan's physical form while he is extended, he would feel it but could not respond.
- **Charlotte's silver thread can actively manifest in Ryan's mind.** During recombination, Charlotte's presence transcended passive anchoring and became active participation. Althena's reaction ("Oh. That was… unexpected.") suggested this is a new phenomenon even the deity did not anticipate.
- **Layered resistance in composite objects confirmed.** The thread penetrates materials sequentially, encountering variable density at each boundary — stone yields, steel responds, living flesh fights back.
- **Power scaling established:** 10 loops for stone manipulation, 15 loops for steel extraction. Full thread length wrapped around Ryan's body previously generated enough force to topside an entire ship.
