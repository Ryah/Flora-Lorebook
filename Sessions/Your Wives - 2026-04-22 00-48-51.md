# Session: Your Wives - 2026-04-22 00-48-51

# Session Summary: Wednesday, June 21, 1843 | 1:28 AM - 1:38 AM

## Key Events & Plot Developments
*   Ryan invited Charlotte to try accessing his divine power through their sensory link, suggesting she envision his mental "brick room" and the golden thread.
*   Charlotte attempted to access the power but was rejected by the architecture. She touched the threshold and felt feedback, a cold metal taste, and an echo of Ryan's isolation—but could not draw power herself. She determined the system is gated to preserve Ryan's autonomy.
*   Ryan then attempted to *share* the power by envisioning Charlotte in the brick room with him, wrapping the golden thread around her finger and his own hand until he began to glow.
*   This succeeded. Charlotte manifested divine power, but with silver light instead of gold. The water beneath her hand responded by forming geometric, crystalline patterns and freezing into ice hexagons, rather than rising in droplets.
*   Ryan instructed her to search for the "catch" in the water and pull on it before the shared power faded.
