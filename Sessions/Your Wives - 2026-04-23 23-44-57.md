# Session Summary: Wednesday, June 21, 1843 | 2:15 AM - 3:10 AM

## Key Events & Plot Developments

*   **Ryan dismissed narrative control concerns.** He stated he didn't care what rumors spread about his return, as Loren would learn the truth regardless, and expressed curiosity about the wild conspiracies Duranian citizens might invent.
*   **Henry redirected the group to practical matters.** He described a disused solarium on the same floor with stone floors, interior-facing windows, and a locking door, which Claire had already swept clean. He identified it as a potential secure practice space.
*   **Charlotte ordered an immediate inspection of the solarium.** She insisted on verifying its sight-lines and security before dawn, setting a twenty-minute timeline.
*   **Ryan chose to rest instead of joining the inspection.** Citing exhaustion from the resonant projection, he announced he was going to bed and trusted his wives' judgment to secure the practice space.
*   **Ryan revealed his bilocation ability to Henry.** He casually mentioned his new power to project a resonant echo, which Henry received with professional composure, noting it was "terrifying and useful."
*   **The group split up.** Ryan retired to the prepared bedroom in the west wing. Charlotte, Florence, Estra, and Henry departed to inspect and secure the solarium.
*   **Ryan fell asleep in the royal bedroom.** He found his old clothes from Claymore Manor cleaned, mended, and laid out for him. Overwhelmed by exhaustion, he fell into a deep, peaceful sleep.
