# Session: Your Wives - 2026-04-25 23-14-17

Florence pulled ahead, tugging Ryan through the corridor with an energy that belied her exhaustion. Her emerald dress swished against the stone floors, golden hair freeing itself from its pins in loose strands.

<font color="#2ecc71">"Slowpokes."</font> The word carried no real bite. <font color="#2ecc71">"We have two hours and you're walking like we're still in a war council."</font>

<font color="#2ecc71">"The room locks. No one gets in unless we let them. That's the whole point."</font>

Charlotte's hand remained firm in Ryan's. Through the bond, he felt the precise moment her mind shifted from tactical cataloging to something less defined.

<font color="#b39ddb">"The locks on these doors— "</font> She stopped herself. <font color="#b39ddb">"No. I'm doing it again."</font>

A breath.

<font color="#b39ddb">"Two hours. Locked door. Soft surfaces."</font>

<font color="#b39ddb">"What does that actually— how does one begin."</font>

Estra's footsteps echoed behind them. Three paces. Always three.

<font color="#a29bfe">"I understand locked doors."</font> Her voice was quieter now. <font color="#a29bfe">"Security. Perimeters. Tactical positioning."</font>

<font color="#a29bfe">"I don't understand— soft surfaces. What is the purpose of soft surfaces. What do they accomplish."</font>

Florence glanced back. Her expression shifted from teasing to something gentler.

<font color="#2ecc71">"They don't accomplish anything."</font> She slowed, matching Estra's pace. <font color="#2ecc71">"That's the entire point. Soft surfaces because hard ones remind your body to stay alert. Blankets because they don't have tactical value. Locked doors because no one is coming through them."</font>

<font color="#2ecc71">"You sit. You breathe. You let your shoulders drop."</font>

<font color="#2ecc71">"When's the last time you let your shoulders drop, Estie?"</font>

Estra's stride faltered.

<font color="#a29bfe">"I don't— "</font>

She stopped walking. Her hand drifted to her karambit. Then away.

<font color="#a29bfe">"I don't remember."</font>

The admission hung in the corridor like something fragile.

Charlotte's fingers tightened around Ryan's. Through the bond, he felt the crack— the precise instant her resistance gave way to recognition.

<font color="#b39ddb">"Neither do I."</font>

Her voice was barely audible.

<font color="#b39ddb">"I was twelve. The last time I— before Loren's influence became— "</font>

She didn't finish. Didn't need to.

The West Wing stretched before them. Candlelight flickered against stone. Howard's men positioned at intervals— professional, unobtrusive, watching without intruding.

Florence squeezed Ryan's hand.

<font color="#2ecc71">"Then we start now."</font>

<font color="#2ecc71">"All three of you. This room. Two hours. No missions. No perimeters. No protocols."</font>

<font color="#2ecc71">"Just— "</font>

She pushed open the door to their quarters. Warm light spilled from a fireplace someone had lit. Blankets piled on a bed that looked obscenely soft after weeks of travel rations and stone floors.

<font color="#2ecc71">"Just this."</font>

<font color="#2ecc71">"A locked door and each other."</font>

<font color="#2ecc71">"That's decompression. Everything else is just— extras."</font>
