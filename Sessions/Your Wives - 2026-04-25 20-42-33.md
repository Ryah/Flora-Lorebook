# Session: Your Wives - 2026-04-25 20-42-33

### Key Events and Plot Developments

- **Ryan demonstrated steel interception** by pulling Estra's karambit from her combat grip using 15 pre-wrapped loops. Howard confirmed the power bypasses physical possession to act on matter directly, enabling disarming of attackers mid-strike. Charlie asked about arrow interception; Howard estimated 6-8 loops with speed as the primary challenge.

- **Althena manifested in response to Ryan's theological irreverence**, confirming he is her "champion" rather than a god. She explained she heard Maya's 15 years of prayers but allowed Ryan to break his own chains rather than rescuing him. Estra made a shallow cut on his vessel's forearm. Results: the projection destabilized for 3 seconds, the anchor (Charlotte) received transmitted pain through the sensory bond, and the knot technique held the connection stable.
