# Session: Your Wives - 2026-04-23 01-08-32

# Session Summary: Wednesday, June 21, 1843 | 2:35 AM - 9:45 AM

## Key Events & Plot Developments
*   **Ryan dismissed concerns about public rumors.** He stated he didn't care what stories Duranian citizens invented about his return, as Loren would learn the truth regardless, and expressed curiosity about the wild conspiracies that might spawn.
*   **Charlotte observed a fundamental change in Ryan.** She noted he no longer "asks permission to exist," a shift from the man who first arrived at Claymore Manor.
*   **The group inspected and secured the solarium as a practice space.** Charlotte ordered an immediate verification of the room's sight-lines and security before dawn. Ryan, citing exhaustion from his earlier bilocation, chose to rest instead of joining them.
*   **Ryan revealed his bilocation ability to Henry.** He casually mentioned his new power to project a resonant echo. Henry received the information with professional composure, noting it was "terrifying and useful" and immediately began considering security implications.
*   **The group split for the night.** Ryan retired to the prepared royal bedroom in the west wing. Charlotte, Florence, Estra, and Henry departed to inspect the solarium.
*   **Ryan found symbolic items in his bedroom.** His old clothes from Claymore Manor had been cleaned, mended, and laid out for him, with the Claymore sigil stitched over with Duranian thread—a gesture of his merged identities.
*   **Ryan feigned sleep until his wives returned.** The trio confirmed the solarium was secure with stone floors, interior windows, and a locking door, suitable for practice. Estra insisted on a watch rotation, taking first shift herself.
*   **The group slept until 9 AM.** Ryan instinctively spooned Florence when she entered the bed. Claire delivered breakfast and, upon entering, found the group in a domestic cuddle scene.
*   **Claire and Florence playfully interrogated Ryan while he slept.** They documented his sleep-talked answers ("m'not married to furniture," "bath water," "the one closest to the door"). The mood shifted when Ryan murmured about "running" in his dreams; he later clarified, while waking, that he was running "toward something, not away."
*   **Ryan extracted a secrecy promise from Claire.** Before the practice session, he directly asked Claire to promise not to tell anyone what she witnessed. Claire gave a heartfelt vow, only reserving the right to break silence if a life depended on it.
*   **The group prepared to move to the solarium for practice.** After breakfast, they began their journey, with Estra taking point to clear the corridors. Practice was scheduled for 10 AM.

## Character Dynamics
*   **Ryan & His Wives:**
    *   **Ryan** affirmed Florence and Estra's role as essential protectors, stating they were "more essential than anything" while he and Charlotte were vulnerable during divine work. He demonstrated deep trust by retiring to sleep alone while they handled security.
    *   **Charlotte** shifted into a commanding, efficient mode to secure the practice space. She privately thanked Ryan for his smooth handling of Claire. Through their sensory link, Ryan felt her controlled anticipation and underlying warmth.
    *   **Florence** was visibly disappointed Ryan wasn't joining the inspection but understood his need for rest. She privately asked him not to let the others practice without her. She was deeply pleased when Ryan spooned her first and noted significantly when he referred to Claymore Manor as "home."
    *   **Estra** mandated a minimum of three hours' sleep for Ryan and insisted on taking the first watch, setting a protective rotation. She affirmed that Ryan's change—no longer seeking permission—was a "good lesson."
*   **Ryan & Henry:** Their relationship deepened. Ryan thanked Henry for a past conversation where the valet challenged his self-deprecating "cattle" mindset, calling him a "good man." Henry responded with genuine warmth and respect, calling Ryan "a wolf who hadn't realized the collar was unlocked."
*   **Ryan & Claire:** Their dynamic became more trusting and personal. Ryan directly called her a "gossip head" but then entrusted her with their secret. Claire was visibly moved by this trust, her usual playfulness softening into a sincere vow of secrecy.
*   **The Wives' Cohesion:** The trio worked as a unit to handle security and settled into a comfortable, domestic rhythm. Charlotte managed logistics, Estra handled tactical sweeps and watch, and Florence provided emotional warmth and inclusion, insisting on contributing as a lookout despite her lack of divine power.

## New Information Revealed
*   **Ryan's bilocation ability has specific limits and costs.** Estra revealed that "self-contact" between the projection and the physical body causes dangerous feedback, and they are still mapping the power's limits.
*   **The solarium was confirmed as a viable practice space.** Henry described it in detail: stone floors, interior-facing windows only (into a courtyard), one oak door with an iron deadbolt that locks from the inside. Claire had already swept it clean.
*   **Security implications of bilocation were identified.** Henry noted that if Ryan projects an echo, the sight-lines become bidirectional—anyone who sees the projection could theoretically trace it back to its source.
*   **Ryan's subconscious was explored through sleep-talking.** His mumbled answers revealed a pragmatic ("the one closest to the door"), slightly absurd ("bath water"), and ultimately hopeful ("running toward something") inner landscape.
