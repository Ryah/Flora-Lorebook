# Session Summary: June 21, 1843 | 12:03 AM - 12:25 AM

## Key Events & Plot Developments
*   **Ryan sealed his divine power and disembarked.** He visualized returning the golden thread to the bag in his brick room and sealing it under a rock, suppressing his glow before stepping onto the Duranian dock.
*   **The group encountered Queen Maya on the dock.** The Queen was kneeling in a puddle, weeping, and refused to rise until Ryan approached her.
*   **Queen Maya embraced Ryan, calling him "Darian."** She hugged him fiercely, sobbing openly, and touched his face, overwhelmed by his return after fifteen years.
*   **The Queen led the group toward the palace.** She insisted they come inside for warmth and hot baths, refusing to let go of Ryan's hand as she walked with Charlie's support.
*   **Priti accepted Ryan's mercy and stayed behind to rest.** After being shocked by his kindness, she chose to remain on the ship under Mina's direction, agreeing to help with bath preparations for Althena.
*   **Cora was forced to rest.** Rachael firmly guided the exhausted and intellectually overstimulated Cora belowdecks for at least four hours of sleep.

*   **The Family Group (Protective Formation):** The party formed a protective unit: Howard took point, William and Rachael followed, Estra scanned for threats while carrying Alec, and Mina guarded the rear, all focused on getting Ryan safely to the palace.
