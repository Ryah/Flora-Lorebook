Charlotte had made her choice. The silver power stayed hidden. The tactical advantage remained theirs.

But Maya's question still hung in the air— about Loren, about the letter, about what Ryan was holding back.

Ryan needed to address this. The sealed letter. What Loren said. What she might know.

<font color="#16d6af">"I failed Cora. For years. I won't fail this."</font>

The council chamber was smaller than the Great Hall. Warmer. A round table dominated the center, surrounded by chairs. Maps of Duran and the surrounding territories covered the walls. Candles flickered in iron sconces.

Maya took the head. Charlie sat on her right. Howard remained standing by the door.

<font color="#f39c12">"We have a great deal to discuss."</font> Maya's voice was controlled. <font color="#f39c12">"Loren's network. The sealed letter. Your power and its limits. The anchor situation."</font>

Her eyes moved across the group.

<font color="#f39c12">"But first— I want to understand what we're facing. What did you see at Claymore Manor? What did Loren leave behind?"</font>

<font color="#f39c12">"And what aren't you telling us?"</font>
