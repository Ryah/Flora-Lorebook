# Session: Your Wives - 2026-04-24 21-24-43

# Session Summary: Wednesday, June 21, 1843 | 11:32 AM - 12:28 PM

## Key Events & Plot Developments

- **Ryan attempted to channel power through his projected form to manipulate water.** The attempt failed catastrophically — he raised a single droplet before acute neurological pain struck. Althena intervened, explaining that projections can only perceive, not act, and that power requires a physical anchor. She compared the attempt to "trying to drink water through a painting of a cup."
- **Charlotte discovered she could pull Ryan back through the bond.** When Ryan tried to find his own way back from the failed projection, Charlotte reached through the sensory link and exerted force on his extended consciousness, yanking him back to singularity. Althena confirmed this "anchor retrieval" was an emergent property of their connection, not something she had designed.
- **Ryan successfully projected across the Cerulean Strait to Claymore Manor.** Targeting the parlor as a location rather than a person, he used the emotional weight of "home" as his connection point. The extreme distance caused severe strain, forcing Charlotte to pull him back after only three minutes instead of the agreed five.
- **Ryan discovered Loren Claymore inside Claymore Manor.** She stood in the abandoned, dust-covered parlor, ran a finger along the mantle, and left a sealed letter on it. She spoke aloud: "You ran well, little prince. But you forgot something. I'll be seeing you soon." Loren did not perceive Ryan's projection.
- **The group agreed to keep Charlotte's silver thread and anchor-pull ability secret from the royal family,** preserving it as a tactical advantage. The golden light would be shared as public knowledge; the shared silver power would remain between Ryan, Charlotte, and their inner circle.
- **Estra broke down emotionally after Ryan's confrontation.** Ryan pulled her into a hug and refused to let her diminish herself. Estra admitted she had never been protected before, confessed she doesn't know what to do with being chosen, and pressed her hand over Ryan's heart. She confirmed that his Althena-blessed marriage document meant something to her.
- **Claire prepared Ryan for the royal audience,** shaving him, dressing him in Duranian formal wear (forest green shirt, dark trousers, silver-embroidered doublet), and feeding him cheese and spiced honey drink. She expressed that Ryan is different from the noblemen she has served — he fights for others and chooses family rather than accepting what is given.
- **Mina confronted Ryan about Queen Maya's fifteen-year fantasy** of who her son would become. She warned that Maya might prefer the idealized image over the real, scarred man. Ryan responded that Maya would have to compare the real him to her fantasy and decide who her son truly is. Mina gripped his shoulders and promised to burn the palace down if Maya hurt him.
- **Ryan met Queen Maya alone in the private dining room.** Maya was smaller and simpler than expected, dressed in a practical grey brocade gown. She broke down upon seeing him, apologized for failing to protect him, and asked permission to embrace him. Ryan ran forward and hugged her, calling her "mom."
- **Queen Maya transitioned from emotional reunion to direct inquiry.** After composing herself, she asked about the golden light in the harbor and demanded to know what Althena had given him.

Ryan's refusal to test blood on her and his subsequent hug forced her to confront being chosen and valued. She called him "terrifying" — not as an insult, but as acknowledgment of his power and conviction. She set a boundary of her own: if he dies doing something stupid, she will drag him back from the afterlife herself.

She physically gripped him to emphasize her promise to burn the palace if Maya hurts him. Her vulnerability surfaced only after Ryan's declaration that his family (wives and sisters) are the best anyone could imagine.
- **Ryan & Claire:** Claire's role shifted from maid to caretaker and confidante. She performed intimate grooming, expressed genuine admiration for Ryan's character, and delivered a fierce reminder before he entered the Queen's presence: he is no longer the scared boy in the brick room.
- **Ryan & Charlotte (via bond):** Charlotte felt Ryan's emotional intensity during Mina's confrontation and transmitted warmth and fierce pride through the bond. She remained in the solarium with Estra and Florence during the royal audience, monitoring through the link.
- **Ryan & Queen Maya:** Maya's initial reaction was raw maternal grief — no games, no courtly masks. She showed hesitation and asked permission to hug rather than demanding the embrace. She admitted she had a speech prepared and forgot every word. She apologized for being unable to protect him. Her shift to direct questions about his powers signaled that she is processing his survival as both mother and sovereign.
