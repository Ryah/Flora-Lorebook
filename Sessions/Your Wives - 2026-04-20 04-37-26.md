# Session: Your Wives - 2026-04-20 04-37-26

# Session Summary: June 20, 1843 | 8:43 PM - 9:20 PM

## Key Events & Plot Developments
*   **Ryan entered the main hall** after leaving Estra's chamber, intentionally fluttering his heart so Charlotte would feel it through the sensory link. He asked what he missed while he was "asleep."
*   **Charlotte briefed Ryan** on security preparations: William verified the north tower tunnel could accommodate twenty people, Howard had positioned six Ragnar men in the village, and the south entrance was compromised, forcing a diversion through the wine cellar.
*   **Florence reported on banner progress**, excitedly describing the blue silk and gold thread work.
*   **Ryan prayed aloud to Althena** to revive the unconscious Oracle, arguing the wedding needed Church documentation.
*   **The Oracle was revived by divine intervention**, staggering downstairs claiming Althena had "kicked" him awake with a threat to turn him into a shrub if he didn't witness the union. Althena's voice echoed from upstairs, correcting this to a "nudge" but confirming the shrub threat.
*   **A debate ensued about Cornelia vi Relani's attendance**. Ryan argued for her presence as an unpredictable variable against Loren, embracing chaos. Charlotte reluctantly agreed under heavy Ragnar escort after the Church elders arrived.
*   **Althena accepted a "rent discount"** for her bathtub residence from Ryan.
*   **Florence led the group to the Great Hall** to view the completed banners: three panels of blue silk with dimensional gold Althena symbols and the inscription **"Four vessels. One covenant."**
*   **Ryan validated Florence's work** as "perfect," dissolving her competitive anxiety. He then kissed her and whispered a promise to negotiate a "reward" from Charlotte for her efforts.
*   **The group proceeded to the chapel** to finalize the layout. Ryan outlined the processional order: William would walk Estra down the aisle, with Florence and Charlotte following slightly behind on left and right. He proposed tactical positioning at the altar, with Mina and Cora standing guard behind the wedding parties for a protective "box" formation and easier evacuation.
*   **Ryan confronted Mina and Alec** about their hypervigilance, calling it "toxic behavior" born of trauma and demanding they learn to relax and be happy for the wedding.
*   **Mina broke down**, admitting she had been using the past as an excuse and had forgotten how to smile, but agreed to genuinely try to change.
*   **Alec confessed** he didn't want to be scared anymore and wanted to believe they could be safe now.
*   **Florence and Charlotte returned** to the chapel, reporting the banners were up and the first church guests were arriving, urging the group to move to the great hall for the 10:00 PM gathering.

Charlotte's reaction to feeling Ryan's intimacy with Florence was complex, but they presented a united front when returning to the chapel to move the plan forward.
*   **The Oracle's Awe-Filled Exhaustion:** Revived by a divine "nudge," the Oracle observed events with manic clarity, fearing shrub transformation but determined to witness the sacred covenant, offering wry commentary on the unfolding "ecclesiastical precedent."
