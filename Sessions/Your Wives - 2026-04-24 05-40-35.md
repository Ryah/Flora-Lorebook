# Session: Your Wives - 2026-04-24 05-40-35

## Key Events & Plot Developments

- **Ryan requested the Great Hall cleared of all servants** for security reasons. Queen Maya ordered all staff out, and Charlie sealed all entrances. Howard swept for listening devices, finding none but warning the palace should be assumed compromised after fifteen years of Loren’s access.
- **Charlotte proposed a controlled public narrative** for the court: only that the lost prince had returned with Althena’s blessing — no specifics about divine power demonstrations. Maya accepted the strategy.
- **Ryan demonstrated steel interception using the golden thread** — he wrapped fifteen loops around his awareness of Estra’s karambit, asked her to hold it in a combat grip, and pulled it into his hand on her nod. The blade ripped free from her grasp and snapped into his palm.
- **Howard analyzed the tactical implications** — the power bypassed physical possession entirely, acting on matter directly. He estimated arrow interception would require eight to ten loops, with the challenge being speed of recognition and catch.
- **Ryan established a hard limit against using his power on living bodies** — he declared this non-negotiable, citing his own history with chains and refusal to subject anyone else to that.
- **Althena manifested in response to Ryan’s irreverent theology**, appearing as a golden-light feminine form with a pregnant belly suggestion. She confirmed Ryan was her champion, not a god, and explained she did not answer Maya’s fifteen years of prayers because Ryan needed to break his own chains to become the man he is.
- **Althena declared Ryan’s family his protection against manipulation and betrayal** — she stated his power cannot protect him from non-physical threats, but he and his family can together.
- **Maya wept openly** as she received a direct response from the goddess after fifteen years of unanswered prayers. Althena advised her to “stay and try” rather than expect to know what to do.
- **Ryan asked if anyone was listening** — Althena confirmed she does not surveil everything, only what specifically interests her (Ryan, his family, and the threads connecting them to the board).
- **Charlie shifted to tactical questioning** about arrows, range, and consequences. Charlotte requested asking about Loren’s fifteen years of preparation.
- **Alec asked if Althena had opinions about him** — Estra reassured him his drawings were excellent and She would be wrong if She didn’t like them.
- **The divine presence departed**, leaving the hall feeling colder.
