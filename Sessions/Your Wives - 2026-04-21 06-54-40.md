*   **The group successfully boarded the *Relani's Crown* and cast off.** After Ryan suppressed his divine light, Bonnie Prince Charlie ordered the ship to cast off. The *Relani's Crown* surged into the channel, leaving the flooded cove and the delayed *Sea Witch* behind.

*   **The Group and Charlie (Integration into Duran):** Charlie began integrating the group, declaring them all his sisters and family. He ordered the exhausted and soaked party belowdecks for warmth and rest, asserting his captain's authority while showing care.
