# Session: Your Wives - 2026-04-25 23-00-57

Maya drew a sharp breath.

She steadied herself. The Queen reassembled behind her eyes. But her hands betrayed her— folding and unfolding against the table's edge.

<font color="#f39c12">"Thank you."</font>

Two words. Simple. But the weight behind them pressed against the silence.

<font color="#f39c12">"Two hours. The evening meal. Just family."</font>

She turned. Walked toward the door. Paused at the threshold.

<font color="#f39c12">"Howard. The Ragnar men. Station them. I want eyes on every entrance to this wing."</font>

<font color="#4a7ba7">"Already done."</font>

<font color="#f39c12">"Good."</font>

She left. Charlie followed, pausing only to clap Ryan's shoulder once more.

<font color="#16a085">"Brother."</font> A single word. Acknowledgment. Then he was gone.

The council chamber emptied in stages. Howard positioning himself by the door. Rachael guiding William toward a side passage— his gaunt frame straighter than before, purpose filling the hollow where despair had lived. Mina checking exits with professional efficiency. Cora slipping her notebook into her satchel.

Estra lingered near the sealed door. Alec's small form pressed against her side.

<font color="#e056fd">"Can we eat now?"</font> His voice was small. Exhausted. <font color="#e056fd">"The scary part is over, right?"</font>

<font color="#a29bfe">"The scary part is never over."</font> Estra's hand found his hair. Gentle despite her flat tone. <font color="#a29bfe">"But yes. We can eat now."</font>

Charlotte's fingers loosened from Ryan's arm. Through the bond, you felt the shift— calculation replacing relief.

<font color="#b39ddb">"The quarters. We need to verify they're secure before the meal."</font>

Her jaw tightened.

<font color="#b39ddb">"Your mother has twelve trusted men. That's more than most. But fifteen years of infiltration—"</font>

She didn't finish. She didn't need to.

<font color="#2ecc71">"Charlotte."</font> Florence's voice was soft. <font color="#2ecc71">"Two hours. We have two hours before anything else happens."</font>

<font color="#2ecc71">"Can we just… breathe? For a moment?"</font>

Charlotte looked at her. Through the bond, you felt the war between duty and exhaustion.

<font color="#b39ddb">"After we check the quarters."</font>

Not a refusal. A compromise.

The group moved toward the corridor. Servants had already begun preparing the West Wing— candles lit, linens changed, the smell of bread drifting from distant kitchens.

<font color="#b39ddb">"Your mother—"</font> Charlotte's voice dropped. Low enough that only you and Florence could hear. <font color="#b39ddb">"She accepted the refusal. But she'll have questions. Private questions. What are you going to tell her?"</font>

Her hand found yours again. Steadier now.

<font color="#b39ddb">"Because whatever you decide to share— or not share— we need to be prepared for it to spread. She's the Queen. She has advisors. Spies. Informants."</font>

<font color="#b39ddb">"If she learns something she shouldn't…"</font>

She didn't finish.
