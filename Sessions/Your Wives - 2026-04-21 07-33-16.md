# Session: Your Wives - 2026-04-21 07-33-16

# Session Summary: June 20, 1843 | 11:35 PM - 12:04 AM

## Key Events & Plot Developments
*   **Ryan successfully suppressed his divine light through visualization.** He imagined storing the golden threads in a bag for later use. Charlie confirmed it worked, and the crew's awe subsided.
*   **The *Relani's Crown* cast off and escaped into the channel.** The ship surged into the night, leaving the flooded cove and the *Sea Witch* behind. Loren's midnight deadline passed with the group safely at sea.
*   **Ryan secured a formal promise of royal sanctuary from Prince Charlie.** After Ryan's divine light flared as a show of authority, Charlie, as Regent of Duran, swore protection for everyone Ryan claimed as family, declaring them under Duran's protection and his sisters.
*   **Ryan tested his divine power, creating a beacon.** He manifested a thread of golden light into the ocean water, illuminating the sea and drawing answering signal flashes from the Duranian coast guard tower.
*   **Ryan revealed his divine sensory bond to Charlie.** He showed Charlie the faintly silver-glowing bite mark on his shoulder, explaining the two-way sensory and emotional link with Charlotte that Althena had consecrated. The link had been dulled until midnight to aid the escape.
*   **The group began the final leg to Duran.** William reported coastal beacons were visible, with an estimated arrival in harbor by 2 AM. Charlie ordered everyone belowdecks to rest and get dry before the formal arrival.

## Character Dynamics
*   **Ryan and Charlie (Deepening Brotherly Bond):** Charlie acknowledged Ryan's trust issues and promised to earn his trust daily. He shared vulnerable details about their parents' intense, grieving love. The two brothers shared a flask of rum on deck, with Charlie expressing awe at Ryan's power and stories, and a mix of terror, relief, fury, and pride at his return. Their interaction solidified into a warm, conspiratorial rapport.
*   **Ryan and His Wives (Public Affirmation & Separation):** Before the wives went belowdecks, Ryan kissed each of them, publicly affirming his commitment. **Charlotte** was left frozen, fingers touching her lips in a rare unguarded moment. **Estra** was reluctant to leave his side, memorizing his shape against the storm. **Florence** claimed the kiss as a victory but was softened by the tenderness.
*   **Estra and Charlie (Explicit Acceptance):** Estra explicitly demanded clarity on her status as a defector and assassin. Charlie declared her family as Ryan's wife, stating "Duran does not abandon its own." This direct acceptance began to crack Estra's terror of hope.
*   **Alec and Ryan (Trauma & Reassurance):** Alec had a trauma response when Ryan mentioned his promise not to die, grabbing Ryan's coat and demanding confirmation. Charlie knelt to reassure the boy, creating a moment of protective, familial solidarity.
*   **The Group and Charlie (Integration & Warnings):** Charlie began integrating the group, declaring them all his sisters. He warned Ryan that the Duranian court would be hostile to his Floran wives and that Loren undoubtedly had agents within Loth Logan itself.

## New Information Revealed
*   **Charlie's Personal Life:** Bonnie Prince Charlie is unmarried, having refused matches while Duran was still officially mourning Ryan's loss. He expects their mother, Queen Maya, to immediately begin matchmaking for both of them.
*   **The King and Queen of Duran's Character:** Queen Maya is emotionally intense, prone to weeping and overwhelming embraces. King Arlan expresses frustration through physical destruction. Charlie warned Ryan they would need to learn to say "no" to a queen who still sees him as a child.
*   **Ryan's Recent History:** Ryan summarized the chaotic two weeks leading to his escape: his purchased marriage to Charlotte and Florence, falling in love, Estra's assassination attempt turned courtship through sparring matches and a date, stabbing Loren to save Alec, the divine manifestation at the wedding contract signing, and the discovery of William alive in the tunnels.
*   **Divine Mechanics:** Ryan discovered he can consciously suppress and manifest his divine light through visualization. He can project it externally to affect the physical world, as demonstrated by the golden beacon in the water.
*   **Internal Security:** Ryan revealed that Priti, a former Floran spy for Cornelia, is on the ship, and that his group has systems of coded communication in place to bypass eavesdroppers.
*   **Logistical Request:** Ryan requested a spare bathtub in the Duranian palace to fulfill a promise to Althena.
