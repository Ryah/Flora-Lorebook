# Session Summary: June 20, 1843 | 10:01 PM - 10:51 PM

## Key Events & Plot Developments
*   **Ryan mentally requested Althena dampen the sensory link with Charlotte** to spare her from feeling his intense emotional reaction to seeing Estra. Althena granted the request as a wedding gift, muting the bond's intensity until the evacuation was complete, and thanked Ryan for the hot bathwater Claire had prepared.
*   **Charlotte felt the link quiet** and expressed relief and gratitude to Ryan before leaving to administratively "verify" Estra's readiness for the ceremony.
*   **Florence led Ryan to the antechamber**, where she shared a quiet insight: Charlotte was terrified Ryan would look at Estra and realize he'd been "settling for something cold."
*   **Ryan hugged and kissed Florence**, firmly declaring his permanent commitment to staying with his three wives. Florence was emotionally overwhelmed, fighting tears of joy, before departing for her position.
*   **Mina and Cora retrieved Ryan** from the antechamber and escorted him to the chapel altar, taking up flanking guard positions.
*   **The wedding processional began at 10:30 PM.** Alec waited at the altar with the rings. William, with Estra on his arm flanked by Charlotte and Florence, walked down the aisle. William announced, "My daughter, to be given to my son."
*   **Ryan turned to face Estra**, seeing her in her midnight blue silk wedding gown with silver steppe-bird embroidery for the first time. Both were tearful.
*   **The Oracle arrived to officiate.** Estra recited her vows from a crumpled notecard, thanking Ryan for seeing her as human, acknowledging her assassination mission, and swearing to stay despite her terror, ultimately abandoning her notes for raw honesty.
*   **Ryan gave his vows**, recounting their violent history, their sparring matches and unfinished date, and declaring he would always choose her. They exchanged silver wedding rings via Alec.
*   **The Oracle sanctioned the kiss.** As Ryan and Estra kissed, **Althena fully manifested**. The temperature spiked, the suspended rain outside flashed gold, and Her layered voice filled the chapel.
*   **Althena created a divine cover for the evacuation.** She triggered a **reverse golden rainstorm**—droplets shooting upward with a deafening harmonic roar—that cloaked the escaping group from any observer more than five feet away.
*   **The family evacuated.** Ryan took Estra's hand and ran, following William's lead to a hidden tunnel entrance revealed in the north wall tapestry. He called for Cornelia to follow. The entire group—including Charlotte, Florence, Alec, Mina, Cora, William, Rachael, Howard, and Cornelia—fled into the tunnels under the cover of divine chaos, leaving the blinded witnesses and the smiling Althena behind.
