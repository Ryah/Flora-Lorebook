## Key Events & Plot Developments
*   **The wedding ceremony concluded.** Ryan and Estra exchanged vows and silver wedding rings, officiated by the Oracle. Estra recited raw, terrified promises from a crumpled notecard, while Ryan recounted their violent history transformed into deliberate choice.
*   **Althena fully manifested upon their kiss.** The temperature spiked, suspended rain outside flashed gold, and Her layered voice filled the chapel. She triggered a **reverse golden rainstorm**—droplets shooting upward with a deafening harmonic roar—that cloaked the escaping group from any observer more than five feet away.
*   **The family evacuated through William's hidden tunnel.** Ryan took Estra's hand and ran, following William's lead to a tunnel entrance revealed behind a tapestry in the chapel's north wall. The entire group fled into the tunnels under the cover of divine chaos.
*   **Ryan proposed a daring escape plan.** After regrouping with Charlotte and Florence at a tunnel junction, Ryan revealed his "stupidly large gamble": fleeing to **Duran** using Cornelia's ship anchored at Briarhaven, arguing Loren would never expect them to run toward her conspirator's homeland.
*   **The group assessed the plan.** William presented the eastern cliff route to a smuggler's cove as the path to boats. With **68 minutes remaining until Loren's midnight deadline**, Charlotte demanded an immediate decision on whether to commit to the Duran plan or split up.
