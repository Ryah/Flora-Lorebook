# Session: Your Wives - 2026-04-22 02-13-02

# Session Summary: Wednesday, June 21, 1843 | 1:28 AM - 1:52 AM

## Key Events & Plot Developments
*   Ryan suggested Charlotte attempt to access his divine power directly through their sensory link, hypothesizing her physical link (the bite mark) might grant her access.
*   Charlotte attempted to access the power by visualizing Ryan's mental "brick room." She touched the threshold but was rejected by the architecture, experiencing only feedback, a cold metal taste, and an echo of Ryan's isolation. She determined the system is gated to preserve Ryan's autonomy and cannot be externally drained.
*   Ryan then attempted to *share* the power by envisioning Charlotte in the brick room with him, wrapping the golden thread around her finger and his own hand. This succeeded.
*   Charlotte manifested divine power, but with silver light instead of gold. Her power was structural and crystalline, causing water to form geometric patterns and freeze into ice hexagons, unlike Ryan's golden, fluid manipulation.
*   Ryan instructed her to find the "catch" in the water. She located a crystalline structural resistance and, upon pulling, flash-froze a hexagonal ice column.
*   Ryan doubled the shared thread, resulting in Charlotte creating a complex, three-dimensional crystalline lattice that sustained for 4.7 seconds.
*   During this experiment, Ryan felt a presence. Following it through the sensory link, he manifested a bilocated form in the corridor outside the bathroom while his physical body remained in the tub.
*   In the corridor, Ryan found Cora Adame, exhausted and secretly documenting their experiments from outside the door.
*   Cora opened the bathroom door, confirming Ryan was perceiving and physically present in both locations simultaneously. Her hand passed through his corridor manifestation, identifying it as a partially material "sympathetic echo" projected along the golden thread, using the sensory link as a spatial anchor.

## Character Dynamics
*   **Ryan & Charlotte:** Their bond evolved dramatically through successful power-sharing and the subsequent bilocation event. Charlotte moved from analytical caution to complex wonder, experiencing a unique silver resonance with Ryan. The sensory link transformed from purely receptive to a "sympathetic, resonant" conduit and, ultimately, a pathway for spatial projection. During the bilocation, Charlotte felt Ryan's attention fracture and split, responding with controlled fear and a desperate need to anchor him.
*   **The Wives' Reactions:**
    *   **Florence** reacted with competitive excitement to Charlotte's silver power, demanding to know her own color (preferably orange). The bilocation shocked her, but fascination overrode her exhaustion.
    *   **Estra** immediately began revising tactical assessments, noting the advantage of dual divine frequencies (golden fluidity and silver stasis). She assessed the bilocation for its reconnaissance and misdirection potential while growing hypervigilant about Ryan's physical vulnerability.
*   **Cora's Involvement:** Cora, discovered documenting everything, shifted from exhausted scientific observation to terrified, manic intellectual excitement. She began analyzing the bilocation phenomenon in real-time, questioning Ryan about his dual tactile sensations.
*   **Group Cohesion:** The intimate experiment was interrupted by the volatile, frightening bilocation event. The group's dynamic shifted from collective awe to protective alarm, with all three wives reacting to Ryan's divided state with varying degrees of fear and tactical reassessment.
