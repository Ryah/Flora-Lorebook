# Session: Your Wives - 2026-04-19 01-18-27

# Session Summary: June 20, 1843 | 1:40 PM - 2:45 PM

## Key Events & Plot Developments
*   **Ryan visited Priti in the linen closet** with Mina, Howard, and Rachael accompanying him for security.
*   **Priti confessed in detail,** revealing she believed she was sending intelligence to Crown Princess Cornelia vi Relani, not Loren Claymore. She provided the **exact dead drop location:** a thornless pink climber rose bush in the north garden's third row, left side, fifteen paces from the north wall near a broken sundial, marked with blue thread.
*   **Ryan fiercely defended Priti** against Mina's and Howard's suspicions, arguing she was a manipulated 16-year-old, not a willing agent. He ordered that she be treated as staff, fed, and integrated.
*   **Priti offered to become a double agent,** providing recognition codes for Loren's network: the approach phrase "the sheep are restless" and the response "the shepherd watches."
*   **Eva Riensdauter scouted the north garden** and confirmed recent activity at the dead drop site, verifying Priti's information.
*   **The group moved to the War Room,** where Ryan introduced Priti and advocated for her utility.
*   **Estra requested that their wedding vows explicitly negate her blood debt** and establish her marriage as a choice of equals, not an obligation.
*   **Charlotte Claymore drafted the vow language,** framing it legally as a public closure of debt followed by a statement of autonomous, mutual commitment.
*   **Priti and Cora Adame collaborated on ciphers;** Priti revealed Loren's codes were adapted from Crown cryptography, enabling Cora to algorithmically decrypt future messages.
*   **Alec Hamilton's discovery of a fourth escape route** behind a north tower tapestry was validated by William Adame and integrated into the evacuation strategy.
*   **The group's tactical plan evolved:** they now planned to use four escape routes, seed false intelligence through Priti's dead drop, and decrypt Loren's communications.

## Character Dynamics
*   **Ryan and Priti:** Ryan's compassionate, steadfast defense broke through Priti's terror and shame, transforming her into a fiercely loyal asset determined to prove her worth. She clung to him as a lifeline.
*   **Ryan and Mina:** Ryan's mercy directly clashed with Mina's protective instincts. Though she obeyed his order not to harm Priti, Mina left the scene furious and maintained hostile vigilance, only grudgingly shifting focus when Priti's intelligence proved valuable.
*   **Ryan and Howard:** Howard moved from military suspicion to gruff respect, acknowledging the strategic advantage of Ryan's "soft" approach to turning enemies into assets.
*   **Estra and Ryan/Charlotte:** Estra showed profound vulnerability, confessing her fear that the wedding would be seen as coerced. She sought and accepted Charlotte's legal framework to publicly transform her status from debtor to chosen wife, seeking "victory" in the vows.
*   **Florence and Estra:** Florence redirected her competitive energy and hurt from Priti's betrayal into fiercely championing the perfection of Estra's wedding attire and ceremony, framing it as an act of defiance.
*   **William and Alec:** William expressed awe and paternal pride in Alec's discovery of a tunnel he himself had missed, validating the boy and giving him a sense of crucial contribution.
*   **Group Cohesion:** Despite tensions over Priti, the family collectively focused on practical tasks: Charlotte on legal vows, Cora and Priti on ciphers, William and Howard on mapping, and Florence on wedding details.

## New Information Revealed
*   **Dead Drop Mechanics:** The specific location and recognition codes for Loren's north garden dead drop were revealed. The drop uses a loose stone at the base of the rose bush and waterproof tubes.
*   **Cipher Breakthrough:** Priti identified that Loren's network codes are structurally adapted from Crown cryptography, giving Cora the key to break them systematically.
*   **Fourth Escape Route:** Alec discovered and mapped a previously unknown, dusty escape route from behind a tapestry in the north tower, exiting near the stables past the garden wall.
*   **Vow Language:** Charlotte drafted the precise legal wording for the wedding ceremony, designed to publicly acknowledge and then nullify Estra's blood debt, establishing the marriage as a mutual choice between equals.
*   **Priti's Messages:** She confessed to leaving three specific reports at the dead drop in the past week: concerning Ryan's injury, the sisters' arrival, and the church gathering.

## State Changes
*   **Priti's Status:** Officially transitioned from imprisoned spy to a protected double agent and integrated member of the war council. She was to be fed, removed from the linen closet, and put to work deciphering codes.
*   **Documents Created:** Charlotte began drafting the formal wedding vow script that includes debt release.
*   **Tactical Assets Updated:** The group gained a functional double agent (Priti), a broken cipher (Cora), and a verified fourth evacuation route (Alec).
*   **Locations:** The scene moved from the Blue Drawing Room to the Second Floor Linen Closet, then to the War Room.
*   **Operational Plans:** The evacuation strategy was updated to utilize four routes, and a counter-intelligence plan to feed false information through the dead drop was initiated.
